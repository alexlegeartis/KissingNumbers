# Exact obstructions for two low-dimensional Leech fibre constructions

Neither result changes a kissing number lower bound.

- [22D](D22.md): two disjoint forced odd sign cycles make the proposed 49,920-point assembly unprofitable, for any choice of the two nearest banks. With the other two small banks and both poles allowed for free, this construction has at most 49,880 points, below the 49,896 benchmark. For adjacent banks, the stronger pointwise argument permits arbitrary independent real heights and final rescaling and lowers the scoped ceiling to 49,440.
- [23D](D23.md): the mixed short/long projection recovers the known 93,150-vector section, but its additional 9,072-point corner cannot improve the fixed stock, even with arbitrary per-point heights and deletions. Separate whole-fibre certificates treat both this plane and the complementary dodecad plane.

The accompanying Node.js scripts reproduce the exact combinatorial and rational checks without external data.
