# Construction-theory pilots, 12 November 2026

These are finite research pilots against PackingStar's November 11 coordinate files. None of the following pilot configurations improves its current 31-dimensional bound. The restrictions below are part of each result; they are not general impossibility claims.

## Whole signed-octad redesign

Keep the 98,304 odd Leech vectors `(±3,±1^23)`, use all `2n(n−1)` roots `±4e_i±4e_j`, and place all 128 even-parity sign patterns `±2` on selected eight-supports. A support is admissible if it is a core Golay octad or contains at least two tail coordinates. Every non-Golay admissible support meets the core in at most six positions, giving odd-core product at most `2(3+5)=16`. If supports intersect in at most four positions, every cross-support product is at most 16.

The resulting cardinality is `98304 + 2n(n−1) + 128M`. In dimension 31, `M=1181` gives 251,332; `M=1190` gives 252,484; `M=1250` gives 260,164. These are conditional targets, not achieved values.

The pilot enumerated all **4,731,285** admissible 31D supports. An optimized coordinate assignment in the published 1,713-support 32D parent retained only 871 admissible supports, and a greedy refill reached 901. A separate Golay seed refilled to 854. After 418 shared-deletion/refill rounds in 90 seconds, the best witness had **928 supports**, giving **218,948 points**. A direct support-pair check found zero intersections above four. Files: `support_pilot.cpp`, `support_pilot.log`, `support31_best.hex`.

Naively shortening the 32D dense binary layer does not rescue this route. The shortened `[31,16,8]` code has 65,536 words; its dual has 31 weight-seven and 93 weight-eight words. A support whose restriction has full rank eight admits no signed octad alongside the full dense layer. Across all 32 coordinate shortenings of the 1,713-support parent, even the conflict-ignorant dense+octad+root count is at most 236,996. This is a check of that parent and that construction, not a bound on 31D kissing numbers.

## Shrunk midpoint triangles

For each extended Golay sign word `s`, the 24 odd vectors `u_i=s*(1−4e_i)` form a clique with norm 32 and mutual product 16. Their midpoint `h_ij=s*(1−2e_i−2e_j)` has norm 24 and only its two parents violate the retained Leech shell. Midpoint heads from intersecting edges have product 20; disjoint edges have product 16.

Replace a two-point antipodal tail fiber by the three points

`(2√2/3) (h ; 2√3,0)`, `(2√2/3) (h ; −√3,3)`, `(2√2/3) (h ; −√3,−3)`.

Each has norm 32 and their mutual product is 16. On three fixed orthogonal tail planes, each with two opposite triangle phases, the exact head-product thresholds are 6 for the same plane and phase, 12 for opposite phases in one plane, and 18 for different planes. For sign heads, a fixed phase therefore requires even Hamming distance at least ten.

There is an exact 280-point bound for each fixed phase: with the binary length-24 Krawtchouk polynomials,

`f(d)=(10−d)(14−d)(16−d)/8 = 1+(27/32)K1(d)+(1/4)K2(d)+(3/32)K3(d)`.

Its nonconstant coefficients are nonnegative, it is nonpositive at every allowed even distance, and `f(0)=280`. Hence the six fixed phases admit at most 1,680 heads. Variable planes and phases are outside this restriction.

The executable pilot priced **138,240** triangle bundles: 23,040 heads from all 11,136 current full-support midpoint heads plus three fixed edges in each of 4,096 cliques, times six phases. Every bundle had at least **334 incumbent blockers**; median 754, maximum 1,316. The best bundle's blockers split into 1 equatorial, 289 midpoint, 42 auxiliary, and 2 pure-tail points. All comparisons were reduced to bounded integer products with independently checked quadratic-radical thresholds. Files: `triangle_bundle_pilot.py`, `triangle_bundle_report.json`, `even24_d10_exact_dual.json` (the disposable triangle pool is regenerated by the pilot).

## An explicit 2,688-point layer with three core and five tail coordinates

All 21 five-subsets of seven tail coordinates can be used simultaneously with suitable three-subsets of the 24 core coordinates. Index a tail support by its omitted edge of `K7`. Core triples for incident omitted edges must be disjoint; those for disjoint edges may intersect in at most one coordinate. The supplied triples satisfy both conditions.

The first two coordinates of each triple use two orthogonal edge colorings of `K7`, `a XOR b` and `a^3 XOR b^3` in `GF(8)` with modulus `x^3+x+1`; an independently checked eight-coloring supplies the third. These triples occupy 22 core coordinates. All 128 even-parity sign patterns on every resulting eight-support give **21×128=2,688 mutually compatible points**. The layer is also compatible with the entire original Leech shell and all 31D coordinate roots.

Against the much larger PackingStar incumbent, source points have at most four nonzero tail coordinates. Thus every five-tail candidate support contains a zero coordinate in each incumbent point, and a whole 128-sign fiber blocks exactly those norm-128 incumbent numerators `X` satisfying `sum_{i in support}|X_i|>16`. This exact quotient reduces 251,254 points to 2,578 absolute profiles.

All **42,504** possible three-core/five-tail support fibers were priced. The smallest individual whole-fiber cost was 672 deletions for 128 additions. Inside the normal octad, the minimum was 677 and median 777.5; outside it, the minimum was 1,272 and median 2,154. Mixed triples `{11,23,j}` contained several low-cost supports.

In 675,877 coordinate-permutation annealing moves, the best whole 21-fiber orientation cost **9,649 shared deletions**, a net loss of 6,961. Independent exact support checks, direct integer checking of all 2,688 new points against one another, and exact absolute-profile pricing reproduced this. A maximum-flow calculation certifies that **no subset of the 21 whole fibers in this one orientation has positive net gain**. This leaves partial sign fibers, other orientations, and simultaneous changes to other layers open. Files: `layer_3plus5_design.json`, `optimize_3plus5.cpp`, `optimize_3plus5.log`, `verify_3plus5_layer.py`, `layer_3plus5_verified.json`, `layer_3plus5_points.npz`, `full_3plus5_bundle_costs.npz`.
