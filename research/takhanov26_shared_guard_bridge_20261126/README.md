# A seven-coordinate shared-guard bridge for two 26D chambers

**Benchmark and status.** Rustem Takhanov v0.2 owns 201,212, at [the pinned primary release](https://github.com/k-nic/KissingNumbers/tree/77f0860bf93a745140e6e25481d3f60773b11441/version2); KissingNumbers project README retains its own 201,204. Strict target 201,213. Nothing below is presented as a new lower bound unless a 27-row block is independently certified. The frozen exterior has 200,864 points, and the 10-dimensional projected frame has 2,601 integer normals with radius cutoff p·x≤2 in unit coordinates (projection divided by 32). The source uses 20 signed axes, 224 signed tetrad rows and four clipped 26-row chambers. Hosted independently reconstructed the shell and normals from Takhanov's actual pinned 83-KiB certificate, rather than relying on a prefiltered candidate list.

## Why the single payment is new

Let g=(e0+e4+e8+e9)/2, one row in the signed tetrad on (0,4,8,9). Opposite main chambers use (0,1,2,3,9) and (4,5,6,7,9). In the old six-coordinate model using alternate tail 8, every linear constraint on the first of these chambers induced by g is duplicated by changing sign of its invisible coordinate 4; similarly the other chamber cannot distinguish the sign on 0. Removing g alone does nothing in either of those old models. Instead add the formerly invisible axis to each chamber, forming A=(0,1,2,3,4,8,9) and D=(4,5,6,7,0,8,9), with displayed local order. A row may now target that one signed guard exactly. This is not another change of latitudes within a 5D chart and is distinct from the earlier paired six-dimensional family that paid three skeleton rows.

In the displayed local coordinates the exact quartet is (5,4s₁,4s₂,4s₃,1,1,5)/10, for the four even signings of the three regular coordinates. Its norm is one. A typical within-quartet dot is (25−16+1+1+25)/100=36/100. The dot against g is 3/5. Changing any of the four guard signs reduces this dot at least 1/10, so it meets all other fifteen rows in that guard's tetrad. The replay checks every other skeleton point, both copies and all outside rows through the exact 2,601-normal projected source. All twenty signed axes remain.

**Witness at 201,211 and conditional +1.** Fix the other two full 26-row templates in the unchanged source order. In each opposite chamber place the stock template with local coordinate permutation [1,0,2,3,4] and sign mask 17 (negative signs on positions 0 and 4, zero indexed), delete old rows 4,10,14,16 (zero indexed), and insert the four bridge rows. No other old row needs removing. This replaces four old points by four new points in each chamber and removes only the one guard, giving 201,211. Comparing against each of the 3,840 signed orientations of a single stock chamber confirms that retaining more than 22 with this exact fixed quartet is impossible; this does not bound freely replaced rows. The seven-coordinate mirror is checked using the actual normals, not assumed an ambient symmetry of the exterior.

For a replacement chamber require the main five coordinates to be ≤1/2 in absolute value and the two borrowed coordinates (positions 4,5) to be ≤1/5. The two opposite blocks then automatically agree across all pairs: in their only common coordinates (old body entry, borrowed body entry, old tail, borrowed tail), dot ≤ 2(1/2)(1/5)+(1/2)²+(1/5)²=49/100<1/2. All other constraints are simply the two sets of actual half-spaces projected from the exterior, remaining skeleton and unchanged old B/C chambers. The abstract target is **one 27-point unit code in this explicitly defined real polytope, with max off-diagonal dot ≤1/2**. Copy it into both blocks; count 200864+20+223+52+54=201213. One 28-point chamber against the three old chambers and same cut is another possible route, but not needed for the paired count. A successful output must pass integer replay, not just its numerical tolerance.

The five main sign labels remain meaningful. Two rows of the same main orthant have main scaled L1 at least 2(1−2(1/5)²) by their norm and coordinate caps. From ab≥(a+b)/2−1/4, their main dot is at least 2(1−2(1/5)²)−5/4; the two extra coordinates subtract at most 2(1/5)². Thus total dot ≥3/4−6(1/5)²=51/100>1/2. There can be at most one in each of the 32 labels (assign zero signs arbitrarily), and a 27-point search can choose its five missing labels as part of the optimization. Its aperture depends on actual 7D coordinates, not just an all-pairs sign table.

## Bounded comparisons; no false ceiling

The four alternative full five-coordinate boxes intersect the stock on three axes and retain few of the old templates. For the first alternative [0,1,4,5,8] inserting all 26 old-pattern rows always pays all 48 rows of the three old tetrads it meets in at least three coordinates, since each projected sign pattern has a blocker as verified below. This alone rejects full-template transplantation into that box before paying for any old chamber losses. Partial signs on exposed support triples can still be useful in an actual new seam. With all original 104 chamber rows retained (in any of their signed orientations), each projected triple or quadruple of the stock template blocks every one of its sign patterns (2–4 and 2–8 blockers respectively). Checking both sizes matters: a fourth coordinate can cancel an inner product. Thus a pure signed four-support addition must meet each original chamber in at most two axes. Among all 210 four-supports, exact exterior checks yield 70 that admit any signs; each of these admits every sign. Of those 70, exactly 14 meet the four old boxes in at most two axes, and those are precisely the original 14 tetrads. Hence arbitrary partial signs and arbitrary independent orientations of the four stock chambers cannot create a new four-support ray with the exterior/four stock boxes fixed. This scoped conclusion doesn’t restrict new freely deformed chamber templates or other amplitude profiles. Thus the engine commits one guard and borrows the formerly invisible coordinates, instead of trying another 27-point deformation inside the old 5D box.

## Reproduction

`verify_bridge26.py` reads only the pinned upstream directory `version2/certificates`, rechecks hashes of its exact inputs, recreates the Leech shell, reconstructs all 2,601 normals, verifies all kernel norms and pairs including both bridge copies, confirms the four-stock-row price over all 3,840 orientations, and prints exact total 201211. The old exterior is the unmodified exterior of the certified upstream configuration. For an actual 27-coordinate candidate (not claimed), supply `--candidate file`, where the first line is the numerical search's diagnostics and subsequent lines are candidate rays, which the proof checker converts into exact integer directions then checks against every constraint. Shape counts or bootstrap numerical coordinates cannot replace that verification. The bundled exact replay establishes the 201,211-point control and verifies the conditional accounting, not the 27-point replacement.

**A strictly larger exact aperture for a future continuation.** The common-box bound is not fundamentally tied to 1/5. Write u=x0, b=x4, h=x8, v=x9 in the first mirrored chamber. The cross form on two local codewords is u b′+b u′+h h′+v v′. For any positive rational theta, bound it by the product of the norms of (sqrt(theta)u,b/sqrt(theta),h,v) and the swapped second vector. The single-row ellipsoid theta·u²+b²/theta+h²+v²≤1/2 guarantees every interchamber pair, without signs or equal amplitudes. Taking theta=2/5 includes the whole stated 1/5 box (its bound is 49/100) and also allows a substantially larger borrowed coordinate if the main tail is short. This is a proved sufficiency relaxation; a unit code with 27 rows satisfying the exterior, skeleton and old B/C in this larger ellipsoid is not claimed. In the relaxed regime the 32-label uniqueness proof need not apply. The bounded numerical test of the original 1/5 box did not certify a 27-point replacement; it supplies no upper bound.
Here “larger” refers to the algebraic sufficient cross-pair constraints; strict enlargement of the fully feasible intersection with the exterior/guard walls was not proved. In particular, the bounded integer-profile test found no four-sign quartet with a borrowed amplitude over 1/5; this is not a bound for individual rays or arbitrary varying profiles.
