# Exact coupled selection and competitive 14D trials

**No new lower bound was found.** The baseline remains 1,932 in dimension 14.
This work adds reusable exact coupled-selection operators and executes both
changed-alphabet isometric exchanges and genuinely joint point pools.

The new typed operators are `selection.isometric_exchange` and
`selection.coupled_union`; their interfaces, proofs, budgets and limits are in
[`discovery/docs/COUPLED_SELECTION.md`](../../../discovery/docs/COUPLED_SELECTION.md).
The engine is dimension independent. These experiments use the 14D baseline
freshly generated by `groups._build14()`, rather than loading record coordinates.
No commit, push, installation, Actions change or record registration was made.

## Two-copy exact campaign

| Run | Seed | Trials | Distinct-union optimum | New image points, maximum | Runtime |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [`run360`](run360/report.json) | 20261104 | 360 | 1932 in every trial | 1912 | 37.42 s |
| [`run2000`](run2000/report.json) | 20261105 | 2000 | 1932 in every trial | 1916 | 245.25 s |

The 2,360 trial executions contain 2,295 distinct rational isometry matrices.
They checked a cumulative 7,484,054,088 exact cross pairs in bounded row blocks.
The list includes structured new-alphabet Householder reflections, exact
rational plane rotations, sparse/dense normals in one or both blocks, two-map
words and four-map commutators. These transformations extend beyond permutations
of fixed E7 frames. Each union is deduplicated before point-level optimization;
all accessory points are included in that optimization.

Each search computes the full cross graph and checks a maximum matching and an
equal-size vertex cover. The separate solver-free [`replay.py`](replay.py) freshly
regenerates and verifies the baseline, proves each stored rational isometry,
rechecks exact overlaps, and verifies a saturating set of disjoint geometric
conflict pairs. These conflicting pairs partition the union into 1,932 cliques,
proving the finite-union upper bound. The baseline attains that bound.

Both replay runs passed: 570,998 matched conflicts for `run360`, and 3,302,736 for
`run2000`. Each also freshly checked all 1,865,346 pairs, norms, distinctness and
rank of the baseline and saved best artifact. See their `replay_report.json`.
Saved matching/cover witnesses are in compressed `certificates.jsonl.gz`; exact
parameters and counts are in `trials.jsonl`.

For five representative nontrivial reflectors, a further diagnostic found one
strongly connected component after contracting the perfect matching. For these
five graphs the only maximum subsets are the original two copies, after retaining
shared points. This observation motivated the joint pool experiments below; it
is not asserted for every tested map or all isometries.

## Joint heterogeneous masses and multiple images

[`joint_pool.py`](joint_pool.py) builds the entire finite candidate pool and
checks every pair with exact integer arithmetic. A numerical binary MILP selects
individual candidate points while deleting arbitrary baseline points, including
accessory points. It retains every base–candidate and candidate–candidate
conflict. The deletion limit was 128; each solve had a 30-second limit. Solver
status and bounds are numerical diagnostics, never exact exclusion proofs.

| Pool | Points after dedup | Exact pairs | Conflict edges | Best returned | Concrete mixed witness |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [`heterogeneous8988`](heterogeneous8988/inventory.json) | 8988 | 40387578 | 278124 | 1932 | 1897 |
| [`multi_isometry`](multi_isometry/inventory.json) | 6072 | 18431556 | 347646 | 1932 | 1860 |

The heterogeneous pool contains the 1,932 baseline points and four 1,764-point
product cohorts with exact block coefficients `(7,17)/13`, `(17,7)/13`,
`(359,439)/401`, and `(439,359)/401`. Each pair has squared coefficients summing
to two. A common integer scale gives norm squared 217402952. This is a joint
point search, with different weights allowed simultaneously; it does not retain
an entire common-unequal-weight product family. Candidate baseline-blocker
degrees are 19 for 6,272 points and 25 for 784 points. There are 138,768
base–candidate and 139,356 candidate–candidate conflict edges.

The unrestricted heterogeneous solve returned the baseline after 3.42 seconds,
with a numerical zero gain bound. Requiring a point from every weight cohort
timed out after 30 seconds without a MILP incumbent. A separate shared-deletion
greedy search produced a concrete mixed 1,897-point configuration: 1,893 baseline
points and one point from each weight cohort, deleting 39 baseline points,
including 12 accessory points. Its full exact geometry and rank 14 verified.

The multiple-image pool combines the baseline with three images under two
noncommuting Householders: normals `e0+2e7`, `2e1+e7`, and their ordered product.
All 168 original accessory points can be traded. There are 181,792
base–candidate and 165,854 candidate–candidate conflict edges. The unrestricted
solve timed out at 30 seconds with incumbent 1,932 and a numerical gain upper
bound of 174. That finite problem remains unresolved. Requiring all three image
cohorts also timed out without an incumbent. The shared-deletion greedy search
produced a concrete 1,860-point mixture using all three images and deleting 75
baseline points, including 35 accessory points. Its full exact geometry and
rank 14 verified.

Each pool includes compressed coordinates and graph edges, solver diagnostics,
selected indices, explicit configuration artifacts and independent verification
reports. `fresh_artifact_replay.json` records another saved-artifact replay.
These mixed configurations are search witnesses below the baseline, not records.

## Reproduction and remaining capability gap

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -m unittest discovery.tests.test_selection -v
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python research/discovery_campaign_20261104/coupled14/search.py --seed 20261105 --trials 2000 --out /tmp/coupled14-new
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python research/discovery_campaign_20261104/coupled14/replay.py /tmp/coupled14-new
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python research/discovery_campaign_20261104/coupled14/joint_pool.py --mode weights --seconds 30 --deleted-limit 128 --out /tmp/coupled14-weights
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python research/discovery_campaign_20261104/coupled14/joint_pool.py --mode isometries --seconds 30 --deleted-limit 128 --out /tmp/coupled14-images
```

The seven selection tests pass. A combined run with groups, gluing and the
scheduler passed all 39 tests. It includes a genuine exact 3-to-4-point coupled
exchange control and exhaustive comparisons on every 2-by-3 conflict graph.

The typed engine optimizes two independently valid explicit components. Joint
general-graph MILP exists here as a bounded research driver, rather than a
registered generic product-pool constructor. Automatic discovery of arbitrary
product alphabets, continuous or point-dependent masses, implicit massive-code
couplings, more general exact algebraic maps, and an effective optimizer that
finds a new 14–17D record remain open work. No finite campaign here establishes
a global kissing-number upper bound, universal search completeness or priority.
