# 21D: no gain with any pointwise real amplitudes on two fixed marked coordinates

This is an exact extension of the new single-coordinate amplitude obstruction, not a solver claim. Keep the 27,720-point odd sparse base of Cohn–Li, and take arbitrary subsets of the 4096 punctured-Golay sign words D used by [Kevin Li §3](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00248v1). Each word may choose its own arbitrary nonnegative real amplitudes a,b on two fixed distinguished coordinates; the other 19 amplitudes need only be common positive *within that word*, so rescale them to 1. Sign coordinates need not be nonzero. We prove **at most 2048 dense points** in this full continuous family. The live 31,560 uses wider profiles, so no numerical bound changes.

## A small geometric reason for the finite certificate

Each marked profile has norm squared N=19+a²+b². The shortened Witt octads form a 2-design with point replication 80 and pair replication 28 (inclusion–exclusion on the three discarded positions in the ambient 5-design). Thus retained octads include one through each mark alone and one through both marks. The old odd parity and D's even intersection require precisely one mismatch at a smallest amplitude for the maximal old dot. Necessary inequalities are

    max(6+|a−1|,6+|b−1|,4+a+b,6+|a−b|,
        2(a+b),2(1+a),2(1+b)) ≤ √(2N).

The first two come from single-mark octads, the next two from the octad through both, and the last three from old coordinate roots. Let s=a+b, d=|a−b|. Squaring the two joint octad walls gives 8s≤22+d² and 12d≤2+s²; the root wall gives 3s²−d²≤38. Since d≤s, first s²≤19, hence d≤7/4 and s≤401/128. Thus d<1 and s≤23/8, then d≤657/768, forcing a,b<2. Two different profiles on one sign word cannot both be admitted: ordinary dot 19 and norms ≤√27 give cosine≥19/27>1/2.

Let P be any one weight-five projective line avoiding both marks and Q the line through both. At difference P the two profiles' raw dot is 9+ac+bd (five ordinary sign disagreements, neither mark disagrees). At difference Q it is 13−ac−bd (three ordinary disagreements, both marks disagree). The key polar gate is **no physical profile (a,b) can have a legal partner of each of these two kinds**, even allowing unrelated (c,d) for each. This is stronger than testing two constant heights or named line profiles; it allows arbitrary point-by-point reoptimization across all 4096 words.

## Exact bounded rational proof of the polar gate

Cover each head square [0,2]² by closed dyadic boxes. A box that violates even a lower bound on one of the displayed linear or absolute-value walls versus the upper bound on √(2N) is discarded. For remaining boxes bound the three normalized values f=1/√N, A=a/√N, B=b/√N outwards by rational multiples of 2⁻¹⁸. With coordinate grid denominator D=2¹⁴, N's scaled integer numerator is 19D²+a_int²+b_int²; use independently checked integral lower and strict upper bounds on its square root and exact outward integer division. For two boxes the normalized product is bounded from below by

    P: 9 f_lo f'_lo + A_lo A'_lo + B_lo B'_lo,
    Q: 13 f_lo f'_lo − A_hi A'_hi − B_hi B'_hi.

Thus if either compatibility type has no possible partner box for a given outer box, discard that outer box. Split adaptively; an undecided partner leaf returns “possible” (only weakens pruning). The exact standalone replay visits 2,673 outer boxes, makes 462,334 interval comparisons, stops by outer depth 13, and leaves zero undecided boxes. No rounding is trusted: each floor or ceiling is checked by a small integer multiplication. All integers are <2⁵³. The interval outer algorithm exhausts the continuous domain, while ignoring any constraints beyond the necessary walls.

Given the gate, partition D into 4-cycles on the subgroup generated by the distinct nonzero line words P and Q. Any three occupied sites in a cycle would give one of them both neighbors, at those two differences, contrary to the gate. Hence at most two per cycle and at most 2048 dense points. This is a general continuous obstruction to a physically motivated low-rank profile deformation, not a bound on all 21D constructions. The surviving constructive mandate is a genuinely coupled shape with at least three variable coordinates (or a different sparse scaffold), not a more elaborate two-coordinate assignment.
