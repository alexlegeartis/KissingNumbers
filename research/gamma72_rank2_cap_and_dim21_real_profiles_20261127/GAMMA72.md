# Lower98: true rank-two phase cap and Γ72 Barnes palette shell-contact rigidity

Live 74D 6,218,995,830; 75D 6,219,816,924. The [published new Barnes MRD construction](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/commit/902483c2) ties 74D through 6×136,704 mixed plus six pure, not a numerical record. External Γ72 source remains [Nebe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862); the literal Γ72 shell count base is 6,218,175,600, and 75D live already has 12×136,704 mixed +864 thin +12 pure. No bound changed by the following proof.

## Pure-A rank-two palettes have exact maximum eight, including origin

Use the exact Barnes coefficient and 975 good-plane characterization from Lower97. A pure-A phase with coefficient rank two has depth exactly six iff its image plane is good. A rank-two good plane gives 42 distinct surjections from F₂³, so the neighborhood of zero under these permitted differences has 40,950 vertices. Identify the 12-bit A labels by sorted linear coordinates, and check pair adjacency by XOR of the three coefficient triples: the difference must span exactly a good two-plane. All permissible colors use distinct triples, so no self edges. The Barnes GL₃(F₂) action is transitive on the 42 rank-two matrices for each fixed image plane. Thus, after translating a clique to contain zero, a positive vertex can be normalized to one of **975** representatives. For each representative the induced neighborhood (inside the 40,950) has exactly 110 vertices. Exact bounded clique recursion, using certified greedy-color upper bounds in each candidate graph, gives maximum six in each induced neighborhood. Therefore the clique through the root has at most 7 nonzero vertices; including zero gives exactly 8, attained by the old F₈ MRD construction. This bounds arbitrary pure-A palettes whose **every nonzero difference has coefficient rank exactly two**; it does not classify rank-three depths or mixed A/B phases.

There is also a direct explanation why the original eight fixed-image MRD phases cannot be extended by *any* pure-A rank-two phase even before asking that the new image be a good plane. Let U be the MRD image plane, W the image of the purported new rank-two phase. If W=U, the rank-distance Singleton bound is eight (one row is injective across the code). If U∩W=0, rank of the sum remains two only if the two kernel lines in the domain agree, impossible over all seven different MRD kernels. If U∩W=<p>, choose row coordinates p and a complementary direction in each image. In the MRD family the non-p row α runs through F₂³, and the p row is Tα for an irreducible field multiplication T; the same remains true after any change of basis of U (a fractional linear transform of the multiplication slope). The new W phase has nonzero complementary row β, and fixed p row c. For α∉<β>, rank two in the difference means c+Tα∈<α,β>. Apply this to both α and α+β to conclude Tβ∈<α,β> for every α∉<β>, hence Tβ∈<β>. That is impossible by irreducibility. Rank-three or mixed A/B escape remains open.

## The complete seven MRD shells truly have cross minimum six

Each MRD nonzero phase contains 136,704 complete norm-six heads. In the integral phase labelled a=ξ, the raw three Leech norms 4+4+4 contribute exactly 110,592 heads (each is reconstructed from the literal rank-12 Leech frame classes with A-pair as in Lower97 and B-sum zero); this is sufficient to find exact finite witnesses. For each relative F₈ multiplier ξ, ξ², ξ³ we find a literal pair of norm-six heads in the corresponding phases whose horizontal displacement has squared norm six. Use the a=ξ integral head and target phases a=ξ², ξ³, ξ⁴, respectively. F₈* multiplication on the two coefficient rows a,ξa is simultaneous right action of a GL₃(F₂) matrix, hence a genuine Γ isometry. Interchange of the two phases gives inverse ratio. Thus the three checked ratios cover every unordered pair of distinct nonzero phases. The depth bound gives the converse, so the true full-shell cross minimum really is six on every pair. This excludes using all seven complete layers simultaneously in 74D by merely changing their tail angles.

The old within-shell depth-eight distance is attained: in an integral 4+4+4 head, flip the signs of any one full Leech block. The difference has raw block norm 16, and hence Γ squared norm eight; its parity is zero so it is in Γ. Such a pair x,x' has physical x·(x−x')=4, exposing a saturated Γ-equator contact. Isometry transfers this to every nonzero phase. Consequently even allowing independent uniform radial head factors λ, any full complete mixed shell attached at fixed tail t while retaining the Γ norm-eight equator must satisfy ||t||²=2λ². Indeed own-shell distance requires 8λ²≥6λ²+||t||²; the saturated equator contact reverses this via 4λ≤sqrt(8(6λ²+||t||²))/2. Pair minima for the attained cross contacts then reduce to the ordinary tail kissing condition at norm²2. Hence no change of angles **or uniform per-layer radii** beats six full MRD layers in 74D. This still leaves partial/pointwise contacts and truly new half-shift characters.

## New scope beyond rank two: no containing-plane rank-three append to the original MRD8

Let U be the original good image plane and suppose a pure-A coefficient triple Y has rank three with image a 3-space S containing U. This append is impossible **even if every rank-three coset were as deep as desired**. Write the seven original nonzero maps plus zero in S-coordinates as (a,Ta,0), where a runs through F₂³ and T is F₈ multiplication by ξ under the fixed two rows. Write Y=(c,d,b) with b≠0. Modulo the line <b> in the row space, the two rows (a+c,Ta+d) range over an affine codimension-one space of 2×2 matrices: the homogeneous map is injective because both a and Ta in <b> force a=0. If this affine space contains 0, one difference Y+(a,Ta,0) has rank one; such a pure-A coset is shallow by Barnes GL₃ reduction to an integral dependent phase.

Otherwise the affine space is a nonzero-level hyperplane. Each of the six 2-planes L⊂S other than U can be written as {ψ(u)=ε z} for a unique nonzero linear functional ψ on U and ε∈F₂, where z is the S/U coordinate. Since ψ₁ I+ψ₂ T is invertible, for each ψ and ε there is exactly one a solving ψ₁(a+c)+ψ₂(Ta+d)=ε b. For that a, the difference map has image contained in L; its rank is not one, so it is exactly two with image L. Thus the desired append forces all six other planes to be good in addition to U. But the seven planes are the lines of a Fano plane; every good line contains exactly one rank-six point. Each point lies on three lines, so a set of rank-six points cannot hit all seven lines exactly once. Contradiction. Other rank-three images meeting U only in a point or zero, and mixed A/B shifts, are still open.
