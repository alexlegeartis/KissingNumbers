# Lower98 low-band pivot: reciprocal two-amplitude parity split is impossible in Li's 21D carrier

The literal incumbent is Kevin Li, [*Improved Kissing Numbers in Nineteen and Twenty-One Dimensions*, §3](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00248v1), 31,560 = 27,720 fixed odd sparse +3,840 dense line-tagged. This is a separate amplitude model, not the previous 17-hole lower bound for Li tags.

A plausible way to remove all holes is to use one fixed positive amplitude profile on each parity of the punctured Golay [21,12,5] code D, so a difference along any weight-5 line switches profiles. In the simplest reciprocal two-amplitude split, choose any set S of k sites, make amplitude b>1 on S and 1 elsewhere in the even profile, and 1 on S and b elsewhere in the odd profile. Then coordinatewise products are exactly b; hence every weight-5 edge has raw dot 11b, independent of its line. This seems an ideal way to avoid same-line tags, but the full odd sparse base rules it out for all S and all real b.

By interchanging profiles take k≤10. For k=0 the normalized cross cosine is 11/21. Otherwise the squared norms are N_k=21+k(b²−1) and N_{21−k}; cross compatibility requires N_k N_{21−k}≥484b², equivalently k(21−k)(b²−1)²≥43b². Consequently b≥b_crit, the unique crossing of that monotone condition for b>1. Small exact rational lower bounds b₀≤b_crit for k=1,…,10 are (1973,1664,1541,1473,1431,1403,1384,1371,1364,1360)/1000.

For each shortened Witt octad O of the retained sparse base, let r=|S∩O|. All dense signs from D meet O evenly, and the old octad signs have odd parity. If 0<r<8, exact strongest raw octad dots for the two profiles are 6+(b−1)r and 6+(b−1)(8−r), by putting the one mandatory mismatch on the minimum-amplitude coordinate. For the first profile the necessary bound is r≤g_k(b)=(√(2N_k)−6)/(b−1), for the second 8−r≤g_{21−k}(b). If r=8 the maximum is instead 6b for the first profile; if r=0 it is 6b for the second. Coordinate-root checks can be dropped to strengthen this obstruction.

For all indices j used in the following bounds, g_j has no interior maximum on [b₀,∞). Its derivative sign is that of H(b)=6√(2N_j)−2jb−2(21−j); H(1)<0 and H'(b)=2j(6b/√(2N_j)−1) increases with b, so H can cross zero at most once after initially decreasing. Thus g_j≤max(g_j(b₀),√(2j)). Exact rational comparisons at b₀ and the limit give:
* k=1: an octad through the selected coordinate has r=1. In the second profile it has dot 7b−1, which violates the squared bound 2(1+20b²) for all b≥b₀=1.973 (the difference 9b²−14b−1 is positive and increasing there).
* 2≤k≤7: g_k<min(k,4) (for k=2 the limit equals 2, but direct squaring gives g₂(b)<2 whenever b>11/8), and r=8 is disallowed because 36 b²>2N_k. But any choice of min(k,4) selected positions lies in a retained octad. For any four first-21 positions, five Witt octads contain them, and each of three omitted coordinates can kill at most one. Extend smaller subsets to four if needed. Contradiction.
* k=8,9: g_k<5 and g_{21−k}<6. Both extremes r=0,8 are disallowed by 36b²>2N_j on the corresponding all-heavy profile. Hence r∈{3,4} for all 210 octads. Their exact shortened 2-design replications are 80 per point and 28 per pair. Therefore Σ_O(r−3)(r−4)=28k(k−1)−6×80k+12×210, equal to 248 or 216, not zero.
* k=10: g_k,g_{21−k}<5 and the extremes are disallowed, so all r=4. Yet Σr=80k=800≠4×210.

Thus this reciprocal split cannot carry all 4096 dense words over the unchanged Cohn–Li odd sparse base, even though it perfectly equalizes the close-edge products. It does not rule out genuinely real nonreciprocal profiles or an additional phase code. The exact verification code has no optimizer or floating-point inputs.

## Exact broader one-coordinate amplitude obstruction

Fix any marked coordinate, give the other 20 coordinates any one common positive magnitude per vector (normalize it to 1), and let the marked magnitude a be arbitrary and can depend on the whole Golay word, not merely parity. Preserve Golay signs. Against a retained octad containing the mark, the strongest product is 7−a if a≤1, and 5+a if a≥1. Raw squared norm is 20+a². Thus a≥√58−7>3/5. Combining the second inequality with the old marked coordinate-root comparison a≤√22−2 (ruling out the extraneous branch a≥5+√10) gives a≤5−√10<15/8. All such profiles therefore have their marked amplitude in [3/5,15/8].

For any weight-five projective line P avoiding the mark, two words at difference P agree on their mark and disagree on five ordinary positions. Their cosine is (10+ab)/√((20+a²)(20+b²)). Fix b: the derivative in a has sign 20b−10a, so there is no interior minimum. Apply the same argument with the variables reversed: the minimum on the containing rectangle is at a corner. At all corners the squared inequality points strictly the wrong way, 4(10+ab)²>(20+a²)(20+b²), verified with integer numerators in the replay. Thus every such line P is an actual forbidden difference **regardless of the real pointwise assigned one-coordinate magnitudes**. One such line partitions all 4096 words into 2048 disjoint pairs, so at most 2048 dense points in this scope. This says nothing about multiple marked coordinates with coherent nonuniform shape; the actual Li 21D construction uses five light coordinates per vector.
