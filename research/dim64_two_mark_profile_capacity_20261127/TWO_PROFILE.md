# 64D: sharp polarized two-profile bank in one RM(2,6) translate

Baseline: https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/blob/934d082034d5bc6c64a5ec5d7b5e3f8576c29962/README.md, 422,673,152. External historical comparator: Edel–Rains–Sloane (1998), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0207291, 331,737,984. The linked predecessor `verifications/improved/dim64-65-dg-split-amplitudes/` already doubles exactly 131,072 owners per pair using Delsarte–Goethals. This is a scoped new sharp upper bound for a relaxed doubling scheme, not an upper on the actual kissing number.

For every selected sign word u in one fixed cubic translate of RM(2,6), assign its two weighted profiles (-a,b),(b,-a) on positions p,q, leaving all other magnitudes 1. Put a=√12+√5,b=√12−√5, so a²+b²=34, ab=7, a>b>1 and every norm squared is 96. An affine coordinate change sets p=0,q=e_1. For two distinct owners their binary difference lies in RM(2,6), so has weight at least 16; the only weight below 24 is 16 (quadratic Walsh values from rank 0,2,4,6 imply minimum distances 16,24,28,32). Within the **same profile** (in either orientation), d≥24 works since all coordinate magnitudes are ≥1. At d=16 both orientations work iff both p and q disagree: a weight-16 difference missing the large coordinate in at least one orientation gives weighted disagreement sum at most 15+b²<24, so dot >48. If both marks disagree, the weighted sum is (16−2)+34=48. Across **swapped** profiles the exact dot is 64−2d−8(ε_p+ε_q), where ε_j=±1 is the product of the owner signs; hence it is ≤48 for all d≥16, and exactly 48 at d=0. No numerical slack is used at equality.

Thus a selected bank is compatible iff its indicator is independent in the Cayley graph on the 22-dimensional binary coefficient space with generators all weight-16 words except the affine 4-flats containing both marks. Indeed, a rank-2 binary quadratic may be written as (s(x)+A)(t(x)+B) plus an affine function; its four weight-16 affine translates are exactly the four affine-flat indicators. There are [6 choose 2]_2=651 codimension-two normal spaces and 2604 flats. The 155 allowable flats contain both marks ([5 choose 2]_2 choices of normal space in e_1^⊥, one translation), leaving degree 2449. Companion exact 22-bit Walsh transform checks least eigenvalue −79, multiplicity 13,888. Hoffman's ratio bound gives 2^22·79/(2449+79)=131,072, attained by the established distance-24 DG bank. In particular allowing these short distances cannot add a single doubled owner, even nonlinearly. Across 32 disjoint pairs no gain is possible by enlarging those banks at this profile. Different cubic carriers, profile multiplicity ≥3, general nonlinear coordinates remain open.

The extreme coefficients retain the existing outside interfaces: bounds 32+(a+b) with dense owners, 32+2(a+b) across pairs, and sparse with even whole parity 12+a+b,13+a,15+b,16; four-sites 2+a+b. These all have strict slack against norm products (a+b=√48; a≈5.70017,b≈1.22803). This shows the theorem concerns actual geometrically usable amplitudes, not a nonphysical alphabet. Old dense selected owners must be retired as in the predecessor.
