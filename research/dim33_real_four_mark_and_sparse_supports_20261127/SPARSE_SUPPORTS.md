## I. Real 9-sparse replacement under all signed roots and all 1,837 complete octad banks

Keep all roots with two coordinates ±4 and all 1,837 old complete even octad banks at ±2. A new real vector x has norm²32, and root compatibility forces `|x_i|+|x_j|≤4` for each distinct pair of axes. Write m=max|x_i|≤4. On at most eight nonzeros, either m≤2 and norm32 forces exactly eight ±2 entries, or 2<m≤4 and m²+7(4−m)²<32 by strict convexity and the endpoint values 32 and 16. Thus a genuinely different vector with at most 9 nonzeros has **exactly nine**.

For such a row write u_i=|x_i|/2>0 on its support; then Σu_i²=8. It is within strict ℓ₁ distance 1 of its signed nine-cube ray:
- If max u_i≤1, then ℓ₁ deviation is Σ(1−u_i)<Σ(1−u_i²)=1, since all nine entries are positive and some are strictly below 1.
- Otherwise let its unique maximum be 1+e. All other u_i≤1−e, so 0≤1−14e+9e² from fitting squared norm 8. As 0<e≤1, this gives e≤(7−2√10)/9<1/12. For given sum of squares of the other eight, their sum is smallest when as many as possible are at 1−e and at most one is between 0 and 1−e (move squared mass to an extremal point of its capped simplex, using concavity of sqrt). Here this means seven at 1−e and one at sqrt(12e−8e²); at the endpoint all eight can reach 1−e. Therefore Σ|u_i−1|≤1+8e−sqrt(12e−8e²)≤1−e; the last inequality follows by squaring sqrt(12e−8e²)≥9e for 0<e≤12/89. In particular the deviation is again strictly below 1.

For any old signed octad that has unweighted *signed overlap* q≥5 with this nine-ray sign pattern (number of sign agreements minus disagreements on shared nonzero coordinates), raw dot is greater than 4(q−1)≥16. This is independent of the nine real amplitudes. For a complete even bank whose support intersects this ray in five to seven coordinates, match all shared signs and fix parity on an unused slot. If it intersects in all eight slots, matching apart from at most one slot gives signed overlap at least 6. Thus an admissible nine support cannot contain any covered 5-subset of an old full support. Monotone exact enumeration gives precisely **18** such 9-supports, each including coordinate 32 (the extra axis); all 9,216 sign patterns can be generated without numerical geometry. Each sign pattern has **at least 8** literal old signed octads that it must pay for, and the total distinct pool of these old signed blockers is 1,864.

Two new nine rays with unweighted signed overlap q≥6 conflict regardless of their real amplitudes. Indeed denote their possible maximal excesses over 1 by e,f (zero if none), and their `L1` deviations by L_u,L_v. On a same-sign shared axis, loss of normalized dot relative to 1 is at most the sum of the two low deficits; on an opposite-sign shared axis, gain in the magnitude of the product is at most the sum of high excesses plus their product. Each row has at most one high coordinate, so the total possible loss relative to signed overlap q is ≤L_u+L_v+ef. If e=f=0 this is strictly below 2; otherwise it is ≤2−e−f+ef<2. Hence raw dot >4(q−2)≥16 as asserted. Among other consequences, at most one ray can occupy a sign pattern, even with arbitrary varying real radius allocation.

**Paid trade theorem:** With the entire root guard and all 1,837 complete banks fixed, deleting any number p≤23 of the 2,216 literal individual signed octads does not permit more than p new, genuinely 9-support rays. This is a no-net-gain cut for the all-roots scaffold (370,536 before stars) and this specific type of donor. Higher support, unguarded roots, moving whole full banks, p≥24, replacements by other shapes, or combined trades are not covered. There is no claim that every compatible *candidate* in the relaxed graph translates to feasible amplitudes.

Proof of finite step: Give each sign pattern its exact proved blocker set B (possibly a subset of its true blockers), and for fixed p form a graph on patterns with |B|≤p. An edge requires q≤5 and |B_i∪B_j|≤p. Any p+1 coexisting additions paid from p slots give a clique in the p-core. All p from 8 to 17 have empty p-core; p≤7 is immediate. For p=18–23 the p-core sizes are 35,151,295,1030,1236,1740. Enumerate any potential p+1 clique by vertex branching, filtering the next vertices by neighbors and checking the union of the actual unavoidable B sets never exceeds p; the independent cardinality bound prunes when there are too few remaining vertices. Exhaustion completes at each p; there is no solver-dependent answer, computed relaxed maximum, or time-bound claim for p≥24. ## II. Independent signed two-heavy carrier wall, even if all roots and stars are released

Test the radius-32 integer profile (3,3,2,2,2,1,1), with two heavies, three mediums and two lights, in all signed orientations. This was a candidate for many orientations with fewer common signed-root payments than ordinary stars. Dense compatibility of any orientation is automatic from ℓ₁=14. A full old octad intersects its seven support positions in at most seven sites, so parity can be corrected freely at an unused site; exact compatibility with that bank is that the sum of intersected absolute amplitudes is at most 8.

An elementary exhaustive carrier derivation avoids iterating all `33 choose 7` arrangements: a heavy pair cannot coexist with two of the mediums on any old full support, leaving 1,417 triples for old-heavy pairs as counted on the **actual** live witness. No one heavy can coexist with all three mediums, leaving 40. Every light is excluded from any full support containing the two heavies and at least one medium; only three remaining heavy-medium choices have ≥2 allowed light positions. Finally one heavy plus two mediums plus both lights is also forbidden. These cuts are exhaustive: with no heavy, the total weight is ≤8; with both heavies at most one medium and no light is legal when any medium is present; with one heavy, at most two mediums and not both lights if two mediums are present. The two surviving supports by labeled partition H/T/L are
`(0,4)/(8,16,28)/(20,32)` and
`(0,4)/(8,20,28)/(16,32)`.
All 128 orientations of each support do clear all full banks. Each of the 256 candidates has exactly 14 greater-than-16 raw dots with the 2,216 actual individual octads. The plural blocked rows number 448, and the bipartite incidence graph has an explicit saturating matching, produced and independently rechecked by `lower99_33d_two_heavy.cjs`. Hence deleting at least as many individual octads as new rays is necessary for this two-heavy family, regardless of the root/star choices or mutual donor checks. This family alone cannot increase the dense-plus-octad population.

## III. Portable, independent guard on any zero-charge flat dense redesign

Any 33D norm²32 code of size M with all |x_i|≤1, whether or not it is organized in Cheng–Sloane cosets or uses flat sign tails, yields an ordinary binary **(33,M,8)** code by rounding each coordinate to its sign (assign signs to zeros arbitrarily). Each row obeys Σ(1−|x_i|)≤Σ(1−x_i²)=1. For two rows x,y, the uniform sign dot minus the actual dot is at most Σ(1−|x_i y_i|)≤2. Hamming distance ≤7 would make rounded dot ≥19 and actual dot ≥17, contradicting ≤16. Therefore a gain by an entirely bounded-amplitude dense layer is also a major binary-code construction problem; this does **not** assert a numerical upper bound on A(33,8). To escape without solving that binary problem, one needs components >1 with an actually priced complete/signed-octad interface, or a different count balance.

## IV. Complete real ten-support geometry before individual octad prices

Under the same full-root guard and actual complete 1,837 banks, a 10-coordinate support can occur **if and only if** it is an extension of one of the 18 uncovered nine-supports. All 420 distinct extensions include padding axis 32. The sufficiency remains true with all 131,072 old dense rows and arbitrary signs on the 10 positions, but not asserted with the old individual octads, stars or a simultaneous family of new rows.

Proof of necessity. Write u_i=|x_i|/2>0 and d_i=1−u_i on the ten positions, so Σu_i²=8 and Σ(2d_i−d_i²)=2. If all u_i≤1 then Σ|d_i|<2 by positivity, as above. If exactly one u_i=1+e>1, all remaining nine u_j≤1−e. Here 0<e<1 since u_i=2 forces all other u_j=0. Their sum is at least (7−2e−e²)/(1−e), and hence Σ|d_i|≤2−4e/(1−e)<2. Consequently any full bank intersection of 6, 7 or 8 is incompatible, exactly as before: parity-adjusted unweighted signed overlap can reach at least 6. Each covered 5-subset F of this ten-set can therefore be fully sign-matched in some bank, requiring Σ_{i∈F} d_i≥1.

Let j be a coordinate of largest deficit m=d_j∈(0,1). Every such F contains j. If there is no high coordinate and F misses j, its cost is at least (2−m)Σ_F d_i≥2−m; including j gives cost at least 2+m−m²>2, contradiction. If there is a high coordinate 1+e and F misses j, its positive deficits still sum to at least 1, so those cost at least 2−m. At least three of the nine positive-deficit coordinates are outside F and different from j, each costing at least 2e−e² by the pair cap. The total positive cost is then ≥2+m−m²+3(2e−e²)>2+2e+e², contradicting the positive cost available after excluding the unique negative deficit −e. Thus deleting j leaves no covered five-subset. The supported nine-set is one of the 18 in Part I.

For sufficiency take any such nine-set S and extra site j, put u_i=√199/15 on S and u_j=1/5. This has squared norm 8 and individual u<1, so the full roots are safe. A full bank meets S in at most four positions and the sum is ≤4√199/15+1/5<4 (square 4√199<57). Since S includes padding axis 32, the absolute dot against a dense ordinary sign vector, before multiplication by 2, is ≤8√199/15+1/5<8. These checks hold for **every** choice of signs and every new support by the same formula. The geometric carrier count is exactly 420 (414 have one uncovered nine-subset, two each have 2, 3 or 4 such subsets). This is a rigorous reduction of the next 10-support sparse switch to projections onto only 18 actual anchors, not a huge free-orientation scan; no improved kissing configuration yet.
