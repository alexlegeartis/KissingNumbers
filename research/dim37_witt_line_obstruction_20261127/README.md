# A Witt-design obstruction to extending two fixed 37D coordinate lines

This is a theorem about two fixed 12-coordinate lines in the published 37D construction, not an upper bound for the 37D kissing number. No lower-bound table entry changes. The obstruction applies to arbitrary real amplitudes on those lines, and the final counting argument also rules out the specific batch of uniform eight-support replacements.

Live 37D project comparator **544,666**, named outside comparison 500,008 from [Olivier using Lin's code](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23569153). The project 2,742 octad support scaffold derives from [Brouwer's support list](https://aeb.win.tue.nl/codes/cwc/d8/a36.8.8.2742H), regenerated by diagonal PSL(2,11) orbits. The known free refill uses the middle projective 12-site line. On either OTHER projective line, 132 old octad banks meet in six sites, exactly one per hexad of the Witt \(S(5,6,12)\) design. This follows by rechecking all 792 five-subsets and complement symmetry from the pinned generator. Root stock and all signs come from the public [37D exact replay](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/main/verifications/improved/dim37-qr-flat-repack).

A new unit ray x supported on one protected 12-flat under fixed D36 roots has \(x_{(1)}+x_{(2)}\le1/\sqrt2\), where \(x_{(k)}\) are descending absolute coordinates. Each full old hexad bank gives \(\sum_{i\in H}|x_i|\le\sqrt2\): all restrictions of its even 8-sign words to six coordinates occur. Given the top four coordinates, the four hexads through them partition the remaining eight coordinates into four pairs. Even after deleting ANY ONE entire old octad bank, one of the three largest remaining coordinates still completes that four-set in a retained hexad. Thus \(\sum_{i\le4}x_{(i)}+x_{(7)}\le\sqrt2\). With \(u_i=\sqrt2 x_{(i)}\), take gaps \(y_k=u_k-u_{k+1}\ge0\); these two walls are \(\sum\min(k,2)y_k\le1,\ \sum (\min(k,4)+1_{\{k\ge7\}})y_k\le2\). Every polytope vertex has at most two nonzero gaps, and checking all exact rational single/two-gap vertices gives max \(\|u\|²=48/25\), hence \(\|x\|²\le24/25<1\). This is a real amplitude obstruction, not just fixed signs.

More generally, every sphere ray respecting fixed roots blocks at least 10 old rows on at least five hexad banks. One has \(\sum u_i\ge4\) for \(\sum u_i²=2\) and \(u_1+u_2\le1\): obvious if \(u_1\le1/2\); otherwise \(2\le u_1^2+(1-u_1)(\sum u_i-u_1)\) and \((2+u_1-2u_1^2)/(1-u_1)\ge4\). The Witt 2-design identities give
\(\sum_H(\sum_{i\in H}|x_i|)^2=36+30(\sum |x_i|)^2\ge276\).
Unblocked banks contribute ≤2 each, while all bank contributions are ≤9/2 because six absolute coordinates are at most three times the sum of the largest two. Relative to \(132\cdot2=264\), a 12 deficit needs at least five offending hexads. Each forbidden optimal six-sign restriction has two old phase extensions, hence ≥10 individual blockers. In the specific uniform signed eight-support family, every new ray blocks exactly 136 rows of these banks: 32 hexads at overlap5 with four exterior completions, plus four at overlap6 with two completions. An old hexad point can block at most 26 compatible new uniform eight-support rays: for each of six omitted hexad coordinates, common fixed signs on five force the remaining raw norm-three tails to have mutual dot≤−1, so at most four; fully aligned six supports allow at most two by antipodality. Double counting therefore makes this entire equal-eight batch unprofitable even if individual old signs may be deleted and blockers shared. This does not rule out a changed root stock, head motion outside the protected coordinate flat, or longer-support redesigned donors. It explains exactly why the middle refill cannot simply be copied to either outer line.
