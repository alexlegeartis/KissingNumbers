## 171 exact charts and a genuinely new 128-ray bank

Each of the 420 true 10-supports is in the Lower99 list, and the old binary dense code surjects on its 9 nonpadding positions (literal rank check all 420). So if padding is high there is no extra dense wall (six highs plus the three lows have total ≤6+√3<8); if padding is one of the lows, the other two lows must sum ≤1. A covered 5-face is impossible with four or five high positions; if it contains exactly two low positions, they must sum ≤1; if it contains all three their norm-one sum is automatically ≤√3<2, so it clears. Full banks have at most five overlapping support positions, so parity is free and these are exact walls. There are 171 admissible choices of physical high set H and low triple L on 13 physical ten-supports: 10 have no pair walls; 130 have one; 31 have two. Of the last two families, 20 and 8 respectively have padding low. Three pair walls on positive u,v,w imply u²+v²+w²<1: if maximum ≤1/2 bound 3/4; otherwise use m²+2(1−m)²<1 for 1/2<m<1. For one/two pair walls put 1/3 on one site common to the demanded pairs and 2/3 on the other two, giving an explicit norm-one positive solution. The unconstrained charts allow any norm-one positive lows.

Choose any fixed admissible low-amplitude triple and any initial low-sign pattern. For every signed word h on the 7 high sites, give the three lows those signs times the parity sign of h. This is a full 128-ray bank: if high Hamming distance is 1, their high dot is 5 and the exactly antipodal low dot is −1; if it is ≥2, high dot≤3 and low dot≤1. Thus a huge algebraic bank exists before prices, not merely 128 unrelated numerical candidates. However the best of 321 explicit rational amplitude choices × eight low phases needs 228 old individual octads before counting any star price. Among 714 inspected zero-star-price phases with ≤400 old octad deletions, even if every immediately available additional signed octad were inserted with no mutual checks, the best net is −88. These latter numbers are exploratory exact finite counts, not the proof that no other joint rephasing trade works.

## Complete exact price for arbitrary real low magnitudes

Sort the three positive lows u≤v≤w and write them as (a,b,1)/sqrt(1+a²+b²) with 0<a≤b≤1. Absorb low signs into the coefficient n of the projected old signed octad. Each low dot that matters has n∈{−1,0,1}³ and threshold in {−1,0,1}; if a high signed dot is ≥6 the old octad necessarily blocks, and if it is ≤2 it cannot block. The other cases are decided by:
- Pair sums u+v, u+w versus 1: b versus p=1/(2a), and b versus q=sqrt(2a) with reversed sign. The largest pair v+w is always >1, since 2b>a².
- One-minus sums vs 0: only u+v−w can change sign, at b=T=1−a. Vs 1: only v+w−u can change sign, at b=Z=a/(1−a). Remaining comparisons are automatic from order, sign, and unit norm (all three sum>1, individual <1).
- Mixed differences with only two terms have sign by ordering and absolute value <1. Negating deals with threshold −1 and other sign cases.

The four curves and boundaries b=a,1 only change vertical order at a in {0, 2−√3, (3−√5)/2, 1/2, 1/√2, 1}. This follows by solving their pairwise equalities (inside 0<a≤b≤1) and using the two boundaries. The replay lists all curve orders in the five open intervals and at each positive breakpoint, and enumerates every vertical singleton or open interval between consecutive curves with b∈[a,1]. The resulting signed-dot trichotomies under three-coordinate permutations deduplicate to **127 exact states**, including ties. Padding-low and full-bank pair walls are read directly from their pair-sum state. On all 171×128=21,888 chart/high prefixes, minimizing over the admissible states and eight low sign choices gives exact minimum individual-octad price **at least six**. Exactly 21 prefixes attain six, 137 attain seven, and larger minima are reported by the replay. These prices do not include stars. This rules out uncharged append and a move paying up to five old individually signed octads; it does not imply no gain when larger joint trades share costs.

## Exact global no-gain for the ten unconstrained three-low charts

The ten charts with no pair wall have a stronger certificate covering **any mixture of arbitrary strictly positive real low magnitudes**, not just full banks, common uniform lows, or a fixed parity choice. Every high sign prefix is used at most once (same seven signed unit coordinates already contribute 7 and low vectors can subtract at most 1). An old signed octad is a universal unavoidable cost for a prefix if its raw high contribution a≥12, or a=10 and it has at most one overlapping low coordinate: low term has magnitude at most 2√3 in the first case and strictly <2 in the second. Thus each of the 1280 labels has 2, 3 or 4 universal costs; assign uniform fractional weight over them so each new ray needs total 1.

For two prefixes the signed shared-high dot h is fixed. Let k1,k2 count the two high/low overlap sides, and l the shared low coordinates. Put s,t equal to norms of the two low vectors' projection to the shared lows. The worst negative auxiliary term has absolute value at most sqrt(k1)sqrt(1−s²)+sqrt(k2)sqrt(1−t²)+st, with s=t=0 if l=0. For k1,k2≥1 use sqrt(1−s²)≤1−s²/2 and st≤(s²+t²)/2 to bound by sqrt(k1)+sqrt(k2), strict when l>0. For l=0 the bound is strict if either own nonempty matched subset has fewer than 3 positions. If l>0 and exactly one k is zero, optimize the two orthogonal components by Cauchy–Schwarz to get sqrt(k+1), strict because the opposite low vector cannot be entirely in the shared coordinates. If both k=0, the bound is 1 (strict when l<3). Whenever h minus this bound exceeds 4, or equals 4 for a strict estimate, the two labels cannot coexist. All tests square only nonnegative sides.

For each old sparse owner the at-most-eight incident labels are exhaustively checked under these forced incompatibilities. Measured in twelfths, the counts (number of incident labels, maximum total weight) are (2,8):384; (6,12):320; (6,8):72; (8,12):64. Thus every compatible new set weighs at most one per paid old octad, proving added≤removed even if all old stars are released as constraints. Nothing in this discharging check excludes the other 161 pair-wall charts with extensive joint exchanges, a different number of high axes, or moving the complete banks.
