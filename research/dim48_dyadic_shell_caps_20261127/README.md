# 48D: dyadic periodic selections of the norm-8, norm-10 and norm-12 shells

Lower70, 2026-11-27, nominal 05:00–05:30 UTC. **No new kissing lower bound.** All norms below are squared. The established 48D construction has 52,416,000 points, from the classical extremal even unimodular lattices; [Boyvalenkov–Cherkashin](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.05121) is the source for the reference configuration and its restricted optimality, not an unconditional 48D upper bound.

## Statements and scope

Let L be any extremal even unimodular rank-48 lattice (minimum 6), and K a sublattice with 2L contained in K contained in L. Associate to any 2L-periodic set P in L the subset of its residues A in V=L/2L; its relative density is |A|/2^48. Choose an entire shell from a coset or from a union of cosets. Requiring the **ambient periodic set**, not just the selected shell, to have minimum distance squared at least m is sufficient for the norm-m shell to be a kissing code: two vectors on that shell at squared distance at least m have inner product at most m/2. The following results concern precisely this sufficient periodic mechanism, and do not exclude shell-specific phases whose ambient periodic set contains shorter differences, nonperiodic subsets, mixtures of shells, or off-lattice constructions.

* If min(K)≥8, every affine coset of K contains at most **9,828,000** norm-8 vectors; a nonzero coset contains at most **9,523,200**. These are sharp in P48n. The relative density of any 2L-periodic min-8 set is ≤2^-12, sharp. Even arbitrary nonlinear unions of full cosets of K stay below 52,416,000 in their norm-8 shell whenever [L:K]≤2^23 and the full periodic union has minimum distance squared at least 8.
* If min(K)≥10, every affine coset of K contains fewer than **13,000,000** norm-10 vectors. The relative density of any 2L-periodic min-10 set is ≤89/61,865,984. Arbitrary nonlinear unions with [L:K]≤2^26 and actual periodic minimum ≥10 still have fewer than **47,000,000** norm-10 vectors.
* If min(K)≥12, **every affine coset, at any index, contains at most 52,416,000 norm-12 vectors**. The relative density of any 2L-periodic min-12 set is ≤2^-24, sharp. In particular passing to dyadic norm-12 sections with a complete affine period cannot beat the 48D lower bound.

The full norm-8, 10, 12 shells have 39,007,332,000; 6,609,020,221,440; 437,824,977,408,000 vectors respectively. Naive pigeonhole targets would be binary indices ≤2^9,2^16,2^22. The exact spectral thresholds for attaining the corresponding ambient minimum are instead ≥2^12,2^20,2^24, and the affine-coset bounds above also exclude exceptional oversized bins.

## I. Exact dyadic theta character table

Thus at cusp zero in the variable Q=exp(πiτ) each coefficient is m_j(t) at exponent j/4, since ||(t+2x)/2||²=j/4. Under T² this cusp expansion has multiplier ε_t, whereas chi_t is invariant under T. The group Γ0(2) is generated by T and ST²S^−1 and −I, so for q(t)=0 chi_t is a weight-24 form on Γ0(2); for q(t)=1 it has the character taking ST²S^−1 to −1. In the latter case divide by D=θ4(2τ)^4. Jacobi's transformation gives D|_2 S=−θ2(τ/2)^4/4, with leading term −4Q^{1/2}, and D|T=D. This supplies exactly the same multiplier, D has no zero in the upper half-plane by its product formula, and there is no pole at either cusp in the ratio. Thus chi_t/D is holomorphic of weight 22 on Γ0(2).

Use A=2E2(2τ)−E2(τ), B=E4(τ), and the basis A^{w/2−2j}B^j for 0≤j≤floor(w/4). Its size is justified by the Γ0(2) valence bound k/4, and independence follows from nonconstancy of B/A². If two vectors x,y represent the same residue and are not ± of each other, the nonzero lattice vectors (x±y)/2 have norm sum (||x||²+||y||²)/2≥12. ## II. Three spectral density gates

For a vector f on V use normalized Fourier coefficients (so Parseval holds with average over V). Convolution by m_j with no averaging has eigenvalues F_j(t). If a periodic indicator f=1_A has ambient minimum ≥m, then its quadratic form vanishes for m_j for 0<j<m. Indeed a nonzero short difference between residues lifts to both points of this full periodic set. A shell of norm j<24 has m_j(0)=0. In particular the root-line graph has ordinary adjacency given by m_6/2; distinct lines give distinct residues because of the same half-sum argument above.

Use the operators:
  H8=F6;
  H10=F6+(11/312)F8;
  H12=F6+(8/135)F8+(1/540)F10.
The constant-mode degrees are N6, 1427674500, 14602887936. The lowest nonconstant eigenvalues are −γ with γ=12800, 26700/13, 4352/5 respectively. This follows directly from the table; for the last operator the a-slope is zero. Thus density ρ≤γ/(degree+γ)=1/M0 with M0 respectively 4096, 61865984/89, 2^24. Explicitly use
  sum_{t≠0}(H(t)+γ)|f̂(t)|²=γρ(1−M0ρ).                        (1)
For a period K, put U=(K/2L)^⊥ and M=|U|=[L:K]; f̂ is supported on U. Since K itself has minimum≥m, Fourier inversion in its origin coset gives the extra identity
  sum_{t∈U, t≠0}(H(t)+γ)=γ(M−M0).                              (2)
Consequently errors bounded by C(H+γ) give one-coset errors ≤Cγ(1−M0/M) by inversion, or general-union errors ≤Cγ sqrt{(M/M0−1)x(1−x)} by (1),(2), x=M0ρ, via Cauchy–Schwarz.

Sharpness: Dursthoff, [Extremal Lattices and Hilbert Modular Forms, §7.5, p.108](https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~David.Dursthoff/DursthoffThesis.pdf), supplies a ζ8-automorphism of P48n for which the Gram (2+ζ8+ζ8^−1)G gives minimum 8. This is (1+ζ8)L isometric under its ordinary norm; real rank 48 means 12 copies of the cyclotomic field, so index 2^12, and 2=(1+ζ8)(1−ζ8+ζ8²−ζ8³) ensures 2L containment. Likewise (1+ζ8²)L is similar to √2 L since ζ8² is skew orthogonal, has index 2^24, contains 2L since (1+i)(1−i)=2, and has minimum 12. These sharpness inputs are published lattices, not new records.

## III. Shell weights for norms 8 and 10

For t≠0, the table gives |F8(t)−304800|≤154(H8(t)+γ8). By (2) the upper bound for the shell in a+K is
  N8/M + 304800(1_{a∈K}−1/M) + 154 γ8 (1−4096/M).
As a function of M≥4096 this decreases for either position of a because N8 >304800+154γ8·4096. At M=4096 all nonzero U characters have s=16, so the origin bound 9,828,000 and nonorigin bound 9,523,200 are actual values. For nonlinear unions the bound is
  304800 + 9523200 x + 1971200 sqrt{(M/4096−1)x(1−x)}.
For M≤2^23, maximize by setting x=(1+u)/2 and taking the Euclidean norm of the coefficient pair. This gives <50,000,000. At exact maximum density equation (1) also shows any nonlinear independent period-residue set has exactly the same two shell weights on its translates, depending on whether the translated set contains zero.

For norm 10 let β10=−1241088, δ10=−2070528. The table gives the uniform bound |F10(t)−β10−δ10 ε_t|≤1482(H10(t)+γ10), including the affine s=12 row. For one coset M≥2^20, the crude sufficient bound N10/M+3311616+1482γ10 is <13,000,000. For arbitrary unions, the quadratic baseline contributes at most |δ10| sqrt{Mρ}, by Parseval. The total upper bound is

If M≤2^26, both square roots are <10 and the displayed expression is <47,000,000.

## IV. Every affine norm-12 coset

Put M0=2^24, N=2^48=M0², C=3670016=(7/32)M0, β=4558848, δ=33226752, and P=N12−β−δ. For all nonzero t the table gives the **reciprocal Fourier relation**
  F12(t)=β+δ ε_t−C m_12(t)+E(t),     |E(t)|≤1755(H12(t)+γ12).
It is zero on the right error term in types 12,14,16; for types 6,8,10 the respective errors are 58,888,028,160; −1,962,934,272; 109,051,904. Set E(0)=0; the discrepancy of the displayed identity at t=0 is P.

The quadratic Gauss sum on V has plus sign: sum_x ε_x=+2^24. It follows e.g. by Poisson summation (equivalently the even unimodular lattice Gauss sum exp(2πi·48/8)=1) applied to √2 L, whose discriminant group identifies with V and whose discriminant sign is ε. Thus the Fourier transform of ε at t is M0 ε_t. With W=K/2L, U=W^⊥ and Q_U(a)=M^−1 sum_{u∈U}ε_u e_u(a), the average of ε on a+W is (M/M0)Q_U(a). If the restriction of b to U has rank r, squaring the character sum shows |Q_U(a)|≤2^(−r/2). In particular r≥2 dim U−48; if M≥2M0 then |Q_U(a)|≤M0/M≤1/2.

Write n(a)=sum_{x∈a+W}m_12(x) and R_U(a)=sum_{u∈U}m_12(u)e_u(a). The two exact identities are
  n(a)=P/M + β 1_{a∈W} + δ Q_U(a) − (C/M)R_U(a)
         + (1/M)sum_{u∈U}E(u)e_u(a);
  R_U(a)=|W|^−1 sum_{x∈a+W}F12(x).
They follow from Fourier inversion and duality. If M=M0, equation (2) forces every nonzero u in U to be one of types 12,14,16. Therefore the lattice over U also has minimum 12. The origin formula for W and U, with Q_U(0)=Q_W(0), gives equal origin shell populations
  n(0)=(P/M0+β+δ Q_U(0))/(1+C/M0) ≤52,416,000.
If b|_U has positive rank, then each |Q_U(a)|≤1/2 and n(0)≤38,784,512, so for a outside W the first formula and |R_U(a)|≤n(0) give 51,193,888. If b|_U has rank zero then U=W, and K/√2 is integral unimodular of minimum 6. By [Gaulter, Minima of Odd Unimodular Lattices in Dimension 24m](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.2001.2670), it is even. Therefore differences in K have norm divisible by four. Any affine norm-12 shell is antipodal (2L⊂K), so its normalized off-diagonal dots belong to {-1,−1/2,−1/3,−1/6,0,1/6,1/3,1/2}. [Boyvalenkov–Cherkashin, Thm 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.05121#S5.Thmtheorem1) bounds it by 52,416,000.

For M≥2M0, the origin formula has R_U(0)≥0 and yields ≤N12/M + 37785600 + 1527552(1−M0/M). This decreases with M; at 2M0 it is 422687351817/8192<52,416,000. For a outside W, substitute the reciprocal pointwise formula into the second identity (there is no x=0 term), and eliminate R_U(a) from the first. It gives
  (1−C²/N)n(a)= (P−Cβ)/M + δ(1−C/M0)Q_U(a)
      + M^−1 sum_{u∈U}E(u)e_u(a) − (C/N)sum_{x∈a+W}E(x).
The third term is bounded by 1,527,552 by (2). Only norm-eight labels can contribute positively to the last term. Within a+K the norm-eight vectors have squared minimum distance twelve and are antipodal; off-antipodal normalized absolute dots are in {0,1/8,1/4}. For projective lines use the even polynomial z²(z²−1/64)(z²−1/16); its normalized Gegenbauer coefficients in dimension 48 are nonnegative with positive constant and f(1)/f0=2948400/137<21522. Each type-8 residue has exactly two such reps, so at most 21,521 residues of type 8 in the coset. Substituting these bounds, P−Cβ≤N12, M≥2M0, and |Q_U(a)|≤1/2 gives n(a)≤5903650127/200<30,000,000. ## Reproduction and the remaining direction

Run the supplied `verify.mjs` with Node; it has no dependencies and performs only rational arithmetic on small matrices and polynomials, not lattice enumeration. This is a structurally meaningful failure of the entire affine binary route, not a verdict on arbitrary norm-8/10/12 shells. In particular a root difference r of norm 6 is a conflict between norm-eight vectors only when y·r=3 (odd); period-level rejection of every root difference is stronger than necessary. Thus the actual constructive next problem is a **shell-sensitive** phase join: permit a period root only where every selected phase excludes its y·r=3 latitude, or abandon dyadic characters (e.g. mixed prime quotient or non-affine signed phases). The whole-lattice spectral ceiling should not be reused as a spherical-code bound.
