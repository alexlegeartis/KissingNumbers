## Exact interface and perfect-matching veto

For a difference u∈y+C, define g_σi(0)=1, g_σi(σ_i)=-1, and g_σi(-σ_i)=0. Before normalizing, the cross dot is (√3/2) Σ_i g_σi(u_i). Because raw squared norm is 45 and 26²·3>45²>25²·3, safety with global antipodes is exactly |Σg|≤25. Equivalently the positive-side weighted loss L=w(u)+#{i:u_i=σ_i} must be ≥20 for all u. Negative-score symmetry is −g_σ(u)=g_{−σ}(u−σ), coordinate by coordinate. Any single violating difference is a full perfect matching between one sign sheet of the old and one of the new, and simultaneous negation gives the other perfect matching. Thus **arbitrary thinning of these two antipodal orbit families cannot exceed the old dense count** whenever their full interface fails. This statement does not cap an arbitrary third torus orbit or arbitrary sparse survivors.

## Different mathematical follow-up, not a wider blind shift scan

Three fully C-invariant torus orbits with a common character ℓ:C→F3 would allow a profitable partial-layer mechanism. If all positive conflicting offsets on each pair of shape orbits lie in a single ℓ-bin and no negative conflicts occur, then the nine affine rank-20 layers induce a 9-cycle when the sum of the three directed offset bins is nonzero. Choosing four independent buckets and doubling by antipodes would give 8·3²⁰=27,894,275,208, about 27% over the live total even before any sparse family. The precise gate is to find a common nonzero character annihilating all *within-interface offset differences*, then compute the quotient conflict graph including sign bits if necessary. This gate is a prospective design, not a verified palette. Positive-offset span already has rank 21 for every nonzero mark class with zero relative rotation and both shifts in P; the checker confirms this independently in systematic coordinates. Thus the original unrotated marked-Q family cannot supply the required unique bins or a three-orbit rank-one gain. To make the new quotient mechanism useful, choose a different parent or choose phase shapes/representatives truly beyond the small P-defect region; don't brute-force more incumbent shifts.
