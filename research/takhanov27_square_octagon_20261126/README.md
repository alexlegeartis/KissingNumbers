# An obstruction for real deformations of four linked squares in Takhanov’s 27D code

**Certified scope.** The incumbent is Takhanov’s 210,108, not our old 209,884. All 3,248 single native square carriers have capacity exactly eight with all other incumbent points fixed. More strongly, for each of 64 specific overlapping-blocker quartets, no simultaneous change of the four squares can improve the source even if arbitrary other incumbent points outside them are deleted, provided the 12 pure-tail poles are kept and all new points are placed in those four local real five-dimensional carriers. The deletions and continuous replacements need not be antipodal, small, or paired. This does not exclude additions outside those four carriers, the octagons, cross-square hinges, a changed pole set, or different sources. No new kissing number.

**Input.** Takhanov, version 1 within upstream release 77f0860bf93a745140e6e25481d3f60773b11441, version1/certificates/data/d27_certificate.npz, Git blob 2f5a163b2a0a8320864493fc510c6ceb43658076, SHA256 8b704bb49503e3a4f6ad6b567690a7a2a9abfb30600f453313eb0510e6d29a94. Both upstream and KissingNumbers/main at 9cdc476b45beeeca3e5d8fc46756c8019f5c887f contain the identical blob; the matching full-coordinate blob is a609b442deeb2ec6152157bc0e10f9eec40dc001. Complete squared norm 128, dot bound 64. Numeric comparator unchanged. All new finite checks require only the much smaller addition/endpoint table, without recomputing the entire packing. Companion script: [verify_square27.py](verify_square27.py).

### Why squares are useful
Use every unordered pair of equatorial Leech endpoints whose midpoint supports an incumbent opposite-tail pair (M,±t). There are 13,248 different midpoint bodies, and 13,248 endpoints of degree two. Their graph has 3,248 four-cycles and 32 eight-cycles. For each four-cycle, its four cyclic midpoint bodies satisfy M0+M2=M1+M3=2O, with |O|²=64. Put A=M0+M3−2O and B=M0+M1−2O. O,A,B are pairwise orthogonal, all with squared norm 64, and the four Leech endpoints are O±A, O±B. Define the carrier to contain EVERY (O+x A+y B,8z), with w=(x,y,z1,z2,z3) on the unit four-sphere. This includes the whole four folded arcs, all four Leech refunds, and positions far off those arcs. Exactly eight incumbent midpoint rows, four orthogonal antipodal residual lines, form the base. Any two points in the same carrier satisfy the kissing bound iff their residuals have nonpositive dot product.

**Isotropy lemma.** Nine unit vectors in R⁵ with pairwise nonpositive dot products admit nonnegative weights μ, summing to 5, with Σμ wwᵀ=I and |Σμ w|≤2. To see this, take the components of the graph of strictly negative dots. Components span orthogonal spaces. Each connected Gram block has nullity ≤1 by Perron–Frobenius applied to a sufficiently large scalar shift of its negative; if singular, its kernel has a strictly positive vector. Nine vectors force ≥4 singular blocks, each of size ≥2; rank below 5 is impossible by the same dimension count (in general n≤2d). Thus either four antipodal lines plus their orthogonal singleton, or three antipodal lines plus three nonpositive directions in the residual plane with positive linear dependence. For each line give its two signs weight 1/2; a singleton takes weight one. For the planar triple, its oriented angular gaps are each in [π/2,π); its induced projective gaps are their supplements. Doubling the projective angles therefore places the origin inside the convex hull of the three unit circle points (possibly on an edge), which yields the planar identity after multiplying the corresponding convex weights by two. The only potentially unbalanced part has total weight at most two. Also μ≤1 at every row by the positive semidefinite identity. Ten rows are exactly an antipodal orthonormal basis, with weights 1/2 each. This is the point at which continuous alternatives become controllable; there is no assumption about previously tested rays or switches.

### First two exact gates
An actual unmodified pair P=(m,±t) outside the square constrains all candidates by |a·w|≤h−b·w where h=64−O·m≥0 (average the exterior inequality over the old opposite residuals), a=(0,0,8t), b=(A·m,B·m,0,0,0). If b=0 and |a|²>5h², a nine-set is impossible by summing squared inequalities against the isotropy weights. This finds direct blockers on 3,024 squares. Counts by number of actual source pairs: 0:224, 1:16, 2:256, 4:1408, 6:512, 8:384, 10:256, 12:128, 16:64. For b≠0, sum the squared inequalities to get |a|² ≤ 5h²+|b|²+4|h||b|. This closes another 64 of the 224. Example after scaling and signed permutation: |4z₁|≤1−x+y, which would imply 16≤7+4√2. Both signs of each blocking pair are literal source rows; a grid or floating optimization is not involved.

### Tail-energy lemma, including the 160 remaining squares
Write s=max(|x|,|y|) and u,v,w′ for tail magnitudes, with u the tail coordinate missing from the old square's midpoint pair. The poles give every pairwise tail sum ≤1. Suppose at the actual signs and body coordinate attaining s, the exterior gives EITHER u+v,u+w′≤1−s, OR u+v≤1−s and w′≤1/2 (allow interchanging v,w′). Every feasible non-pure row then has s≥1/2 and tail energy ≤1/2.

Indeed, for 0<s<1/2 let r=1−s. In the first case, fix u. If r−u≥1/2 the largest possible v²+w′² is (r−u)²+(1−r+u)²; otherwise it is 2(r−u)². These two convex pieces bound the total tail energy by max(r²+s²,1/2+(1/2−s)²) using u=0,1/2−s,r. In the second case, fix w′≤1/2. Now u,v≤1−w′ and u+v≤r. Its maximal sum of squares concentrates as much as possible in one coordinate: for w′≤s it is r², and for w′≥s it is (1−w′)²+(w′−s)². Adding w′² and maximizing the convex pieces gives the same upper bound at w′=s,1/2. Adding the body energy bound 2s² gives <1 in both cases (1−2s+4s²<1 and 3/4−s+3s²<1). For s≥1/2 the first case gives u²+v²+w′²≤max_{0≤u≤r}(u²+2(r−u)²)≤2r²≤1/2; the second gives (u+v)²+(w′)²≤r²+1/4≤1/2. At s=0 the pole polytope intersects the unit sphere only at the six signed coordinate axes (if the largest magnitude is a≤1/2, norm²≤3/4; otherwise norm²≤a²+2(1−a)², <1 for 1/2≤a<1). These exceptional points are pure axial *residuals*: full 27D points (O,±8eᵢ), which are axial heads, not pure-tail poles.

For all remaining 160 the checker finds the indicated signed facets from actual midpoint and head rows, deleting all eight rows of that square. With the incumbent exterior intact an additional pure axial residual is impossible: on each signed tail ±8eᵢ all 48 incumbent head bodies of norm eight form a full 24D orthoplex; a new norm-eight body cannot have nonpositive dot with each. Summing tail energy of the nine-frame would then give 3≤5/2. This proves the individual claim on every square, not merely 'fresh hinges tested'.

### Which shared deletions actually matter?
Only 16 squares have one direct guard, and each has a different axial head pair. In contrast, the 512 midpoint guard bodies (384 on squares, 128 on octagons) each constrain exactly 34 squares. The 256 squares with two guard bodies form EXACTLY 64 groups of four: each group's four squares have the same set of two physical guarding midpoint bodies. For example, sorting cycles by least Leech label as in replay, square IDs 8,137,1808,2406 share signed-pair rows (9746,9747) and (12272,12273) in added_integer; their Leech graph endpoint pairs are (70736,86383) and (86383,96686). This superficially favorable 4-for-2 exchange is the concrete test, not a hypothetical independent deletion budget.

For each of the 64 quartets, first omit from the guard lists all 32 local source rows AND both signs of both common bodies (36 freely omitted). For each square, each signed choice of x or y as the saturated body coordinate, each tail sign, and each other tail j, the actual remaining exterior still contains ≥4 DISTINCT rows realizing the signed inequality ±xᵢ±z_m±z_j≤1. Moreover each of the four signed individual head inequalities ±z_j≤1/2 for the two other coordinates has ≥4 head witnesses. To see that seven arbitrary additional nonpole deletions do not defeat the pointwise tail-energy lemma, fix the signs of a hypothetical donor. The witnesses for its two different mixed facets are disjoint (different nonzero tail supports), and the witnesses for either axial cap are disjoint from all midpoint witnesses. If one mixed facet disappears, at least four rows were spent on it, so neither the other mixed facet nor the axial cap on the unguarded coordinate can have disappeared. The lemma now applies, point by point; no common orientation or fixed signs are assumed.

In any local block of nine, if no point is pure, the trace 3 contradicts 5/2. In a block of ten the same trace and weights 1/2 force at least two pure axial residuals. Thus each block size is at most 8 plus its number of new axial heads. This remains true for smaller blocks trivially. For each signed axial tail there is capacity 48 in all of R²⁴, so all new pure axial heads together number at most the deleted old axial heads. Count all four blocks: with ≤7 exterior deletions no gain follows even giving the common guards' removals for free for the geometric test. With ≥8 exterior deletions, no gain is possible since the four unrestricted R⁵ orthoplex bounds allow at most 4·(10−8)=8 new places in total. (Here exterior means outside the 32 old local midpoint rows; an actual removal of a common guard is paid exactly once in cardinality accounting.) If carriers intersect, assign each new row to just one; each assigned subset remains pairwise compatible. This proof allows no undocumented outside insertions. There is no global conclusion about 27D.

### Next honest positive route
The 32 eight-cycles are not square carriers, and the 896 other strong midpoint adjacencies are not all native-square folds. In the first eight-cycle the body Gram for the four independent midpoint bodies is cyclic tridiagonal with diagonal 96, magnitude 64 on adjacent entries and a sign twist on the wraparound; opposite midpoint bodies negate. The four independent original Leech roots, normalized to squared norm two, have the analogous diagonal 2, off-diagonal ±1 matrix of determinant four (a D4 root sublattice). This antipodal D4 type comprises 16 cycles. The other 16 have cyclic midpoint Gram row (96,64,16,0,0,0,16,64); its constant component has center of norm squared 32, the nonconstant body span has dimension six, and including three tail coordinates gives a 9D residual with normalized inner-product limit +1/3. The D4 type accesses four body plus three tail coordinates. Neither obeys the square-carrier rank-five reduction. The first nonantipodal octagon construction and exact one-carrier obstruction are in [OCTAGON.md](OCTAGON.md). Cross-carrier and arbitrary continuous tail architectures remain open.
