# Exact angular budget for paid tetrads in Takhanov's 26D nucleus

Source: R. Takhanov v0.2, [commit 77f0860](https://github.com/k-nic/KissingNumbers/tree/77f0860bf93a745140e6e25481d3f60773b11441/version2). The baseline 201,212 belongs to Takhanov. No improvement is claimed. Use the exact 2,601-normal exterior chart certified by Lower42. In one chart write a_1,...,a_4 for unsigned regular coordinates, o for the original tail, and h for the alternate tail. We retain all 200,864 exterior points and all 20 signed axes. Necessary and sufficient for exterior safety in this chart, conditional on all component caps 1/2, is sum(a_i)+2h<=2. Each original mixed signed cube intersects this chart on its own a_i and the two tails, so retaining all four cubes adds four 'tubes' a_i+o+h<=1. Breaking one such inequality requires exactly two signed skeleton rows for the individual donor. Other tetrads meet the chart at most twice and are automatically safe. These are not constraints on all possible 10D interior replacements.

## One paid tube: sharp alternate-tail excursion
Allow one tube to fail. Then h<=(21-9 sqrt(3))/44, attained. With zero paid tubes Lower42 gives 1/9. First o>=r=sqrt(h(1-h)), since each a_i^2<=a_i/2. For h>=1/4, three regular coordinates are <=1-o-h. If h in [1/4,1/3] these are <1/3, so energy <=1/4+1/4+1/9+3/9<1; if h in [1/3,1/2] they are <1/4, so energy <=3/4+3/16<1. For h<1/4 set c=min(1/2,1-o-h), L=2-2h. By transferring mass to the largest eligible coordinate, the maximal regular energy under the caps and sum is achieved at (1/2,c,c,min(c,L-1/2-2c)); L>=3/2 makes the last cap nonnegative. For o<=1/2-h this maximal total energy increases with o. Above 1/2-h it is convex between successive breakpoints 1/2-h, 1/2-h/3, and 1/2. The three endpoint energies respectively are 5/4-3h+6h^2, 5/4-7h/3+22h^2/9, 5/4-3h+4h^2. All decrease on the relevant interval (the first is nonincreasing to 1/4); at the claimed maximum only the middle reaches one. The sharp unit profile is (1/2,c,c,c;o,h) with c=1/2-2h/3, o=1/2-h/3.

## Two paid tubes: a forbidden open interval
With two protected tubes retained and two allowed to fail, either h<=1/6 or h=1/2. The latter consists only of the unsigned profiles (1/2,1/2,0,0;1/2,1/2) with the two hot regular positions allowed to fail. For 1/6<=h<=1/2, o>=r>=h and r>1/2-h. Maximizing regular energy under caps (1/2,1/2,c,c), c=1-o-h, and the sum cap gives (1/2,1/2,c,o-h). This follows by the same transferring argument since 0<=o-h<=c. Its total energy is convex in o on [r,1/2]. At o=1/2 it is 5/4-2h+3h^2, strictly less than 1 inside (1/6,1/2). At o=r, a unit solution requires equality in every a_i^2<=a_i/2 and in the sum cap. Thus all a_i in {0,1/2} and sum(a_i)=2-2h. The only interior candidate h=1/4 requires three 1/2 entries when the protected caps are <1/2. Both endpoints are achieved: (3,3,2,2;3,1)/6 and the pure tetrads above. These claims concern exterior plus skeleton, not the 104 inner-template points.

## Exact coupled prices: the 2513 quartet and the 36 block
On chamber B=(0,1,2,3,9), use indices 8 for alternate and 9 for original tail. Fix x0=25,x8=6,x9=23; choose the four signings of x1,x2,x3 of even parity and magnitude 21, divide by sqrt(2513). The exterior inequality is strict (100^2<4*2513). Internal pair inner product 749/2513<1/2. Of all fourteen 16-row tetrads only two rows, signing bit masks 0,2 of tetrad #5=[0,4,8,9], must leave. Independently seek the best of all 3840 signed coordinate permutations of each 26-row template. For the four quartets' cardinalities k=1,2,3,4 (prefix of +++,--+,-+-,+--, representing all subsets up to symmetry), the minimum main-template deletions are 1,3,5,7. The other three templates jointly require zero via their independent orientations. For all four donors one main minimizer is coordinate order [4,0,1,2,3], sign mask 16; template rows [1,3,7,15,19,23,25] must leave. Full quartet net =4-2-7=-5. Best nonempty prefix net =-2. No positive exchange. The threshold before the unrestricted obstruction below was **25** points in a redesigned clipped chamber: exterior 200864 + 20 axes + 224 original tetrad points - 2 + 3*26 other chambers + 4 donors + 25 new main =201213. All other templates can be oriented to avoid donors; every new main row has coordinates <=1/2 and support intersection <=2 with them.

For the second block fix hot positions 0,1,9 at all eight signs of magnitude 3. For each hot sign triple append a low triple on 2,3,8 consisting of an arbitrary signing of (2,2,1) and its simultaneous negative; divide by 6. The resulting 16 rows coexist: different hot signs give distance squared >=1; the opposite low triples have squared distance exactly 1. For the canonical all-positive low triple and its negative, all 32 rows of the two hit mixed tetrads must leave. All 26 main incumbent rows must leave for all 3840 orientations, and the three remaining templates can all stay. Net -42. For arbitrary low-phase choices and any nonempty subcollection from one hot-position pair: the best possible net is exactly -4. Holding i sign and both tail signs fixes two compulsory removed rows in tetrad i. At most two donors can charge this pair (two choices of the j sign, and the fixed alternate tail sign fixes the low-triple phase). Similarly for j in a different tetrad. Therefore the skeleton price is >=2k for k donors. For k=1 four removals are forced, for k=2 at least six (the two fixed groups cannot coincide in both i and j). Every singleton additionally hits at least one main row, by the complete signed permutations of the template and signed-permutation equivalence of all singleton projections. Thus net<=-4 for all k; singleton all positive achieves it with main identity orientation and sign mask 11. Different hot-position pairs or different chambers can share payments outside this theorem.

## Reshaping the main chamber: full tetrahedral symmetry is too restrictive
The main domain additionally requires
25u+23v+21 max_{even s} (s1*x1+s2*x2+s3*x3) <= sqrt(2513)/2,
where u=x0, v=x9. Under the full group permuting the three regular positions and flipping an even number of their signs, a code has at most 24 points. If two absolute regular components differ, an element swaps them while restoring signs by even sign changes, giving a distinct row at distance <=sqrt(2)/2, impossible. All regular magnitudes are therefore equal to some r>0 per orbit. The signed orbit has size 4 and one of two parities. For orbits of one parity, choose matching signs: z=(u,v,sqrt(3)r) have mutual inner products <=1/2 and z3>=1/sqrt(2) by the coordinate caps. No horizontal projection is zero (otherwise r=1/sqrt(3)>1/2). The planar projections are pairwise nonpositively correlated. Four would have to be two orthogonal antipodal pairs of rays (successive angles >=90 degrees). Orthogonal neighbors force z3=1/sqrt(2) for both hence |u|=|v|=1/2. One orbit then has u=v=1/2; even the more favorable parity gives 24+21/sqrt(6)>sqrt(2513)/2. Hence at most three orbits per parity. The bound is not asserted sharp. This first argument alone would leave nonsymmetric codes open; the next result excludes them as well. Under only the even sign flips, unequal absolute regular coordinates a,b,c are allowed when every pair has sum of squares >=1/4. Between such orbits the exact worst body product is aa'+bb'+cc' for matching parity and this minus 2 min(aa',bb',cc') for opposite parity; add uu'+vv'. The weaker subgroup does not rescue the quartet: the next argument uses no symmetry of the main code.

## Stronger obstruction: at most 24 for the 2513 quartet with *no* group invariance
This supersedes the tentative general target of 25 above, which is impossible for this quartet. Arbitrarily assign a sign to any zero coordinate of any candidate point. For all nonnegative s,t<=1/2 one has st>=(s+t)/2-1/4, and for unit clipped vectors sum |x_i|>=2. Hence aligned dot products of the five magnitudes are >=3/4. At most one row can occur in any given sign orthant. Moreover, if a row has |x_i|<1/4, then the adjacent orthant with only its i-th sign reversed must be empty: the worst dot is >=3/4-2|x_i y_i|>=3/4-|x_i|>1/2.

For the 2513 tetrahedral sign quartet, the four even sign orthants on x1,x2,x3 with u,v nonnegative are impossible: the left side of the quartet constraint there is at least 21 times sum |x_i|>=42>sqrt(2513)/2. Take instead any of the four odd regular orthants with nonnegative u,v. On an odd sign orthant Psi=max over its four even donor signs of sum(s_i*x_i) is the sum of the three magnitudes less twice their minimum. Thus Psi>=max_i |x_i|>=sqrt((1-u^2-v^2)/3). If both u,v>=1/4 then u^2+v^2>=1/4 from the three remaining coordinate caps, and u,v<=1/2. The bound F=25u+23v+21 sqrt((1-u^2-v^2)/3) is strictly increasing in both arguments on this region (each negative derivative term is <=21/sqrt(6)<23). Its minimum is therefore on u^2+v^2=1/4; there u+v>=(1+sqrt(3))/4. Consequently F>=23(1+sqrt(3))/4+21/2>sqrt(2513)/2, violating the quartet constraint. An occupied odd orthant of this type must therefore have either u<1/4 or v<1/4, forcing its corresponding immediately adjacent orthant empty. Each of the four odd orthants pays either itself, if empty, or its own neighbor with one changed hot sign. These four missing words are distinct and different from the first four blocked even words, irrespective of how signs of zeros were assigned. Thus at most 32-8=24 points fit; letting the other three 26-point templates be unchanged, the quartet cannot improve the external record even under a completely new main template. The maximum 24 need not be attained. This is a local impossibility result for these four donors and this 5D main support, not an upper bound on the kissing number or on donors in multiple chambers.

## How to reject other four-for-two candidates before searching
Normalize the donor coefficients to unit length. More generally let a four-donor parity orbit have positive fixed amplitudes A,B on the fixed coordinates and magnitudes c1,c2,c3 on the three parity-flipped ones; its alternate-tail component is invisible to the tested 5D main rows. Put L=(sum_i c_i^(-2))^(-1/2). If min(A,B,c1,c2,c3)>1/4 then the four aligned positive-hot even orthants are excluded. In an odd orthant the worst residual signed sum is at least max(c_i*|x_i|)>=L sqrt(1-u^2-v^2). If min(A,B)>=L/sqrt(2) then Au+Bv+L sqrt(1-u^2-v^2) is nondecreasing in both variables on the clipped square. On its inner quarter circle u^2+v^2=1/4, u,v>=1/4, the minimum is [max(A,B)+sqrt(3)min(A,B)]/4 + sqrt(3)L/2. If this is strictly >1/2, the very same four disjoint neighbor exclusions apply. These conditions are sufficient for a no-go; failure of any one is not a construction. Any new 26D attempt on this external skeleton should change amplitudes far enough to escape this broader certificate, or share the paid rows across *different chambers* with an asymmetric donor cluster. Do not rerun an angular search for a 25th point under the 2513 quartet. Source alternative for independent near-term attacks remains 29D.
