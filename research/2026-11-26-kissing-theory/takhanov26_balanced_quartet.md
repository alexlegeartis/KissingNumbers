# Balanced quartet: a different exact 26D +1 reduction

**Conditional, not a new lower bound.** The [2513-amplitude route is closed](takhanov26_2513_23.md). Its skeleton and two-chamber bookkeeping survive a change of geometry that removes the decisive weak-regular-axis inference.

For each of the two complementary four-coordinate heads, take the four rows
\[
(500,\pm405,\pm405,\pm405;500,94)/\sqrt{1000911}
\]
with even regular sign parity. The last two coordinates are original tail 9 and alternate tail 8. Unit norm, the axis caps, the exterior gate \(500+3(405)+2(94)<2\sqrt{1000911}\), internal separation and joint separation are immediate. With pins [as before](takhanov26_two_chamber.md), the only old skeleton losses are three rows of support \((0,4,8,9)\). Here both unchanged templates can keep every row under **any** signed orientation, since even a three-coordinate overlap sums at most \(500+405+94=999<\sqrt{1000911}\) in donor numerator. The exact replay verifies these finite statements.

The common 5D chamber now has constraint \(500u+500v+405\Psi(r)\leq\sqrt{1000911}/2\), with \(\Psi\) the maximum over four even regular signs. As before a 24-row unit code in this chamber with coordinate caps \(1/2\) gives two copies, eight donors, and exactly **201,213** points. The former obstruction starts by forcing a weak regular coordinate in each parity-even mixed-tail orthant. Here the point \((u,v)=(1/2,-1/2)\) with all regular signs positive and magnitudes \((1/2,1/\sqrt8,1/\sqrt8)\) is unit and allowed with no such weak coordinate: its mixed-tail contribution is zero and \(405(1/2+1/\sqrt2)<\sqrt{1000911}/2\). The swapped mixed-tail point has the same property. This rules out recycling that necessary step, not every possible obstruction. The universal orthant test from [the earlier note](takhanov26_paid_tetrads.md) still bounds the common chamber by 24. No 24-row witness is known in this geometry.
