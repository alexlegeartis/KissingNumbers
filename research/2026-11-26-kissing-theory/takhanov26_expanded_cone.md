# Opening both negative axes does not rescue the six fixed donors with a 22-row chamber

Keep the same six positive-hot donors in each 26D chamber, but delete both signed axes −e8 and −e9. Extend the old-tail variable v below −1/2 while retaining the four body caps. When t=−v>1/2 impose |x_i|+t≤1 on every body coordinate, guaranteeing compatibility with every signed mixed tetrad. The expanded candidate lies on the unit sphere and therefore 1≤t²+4(1−t)², hence t≤3/5 (the isolated possibility t=1 is not part of this cone of unit rows). All points of the cone are safe against the original exterior: the old Leech exterior has zero tail projection, and for the 8,960 other exterior rows their 328 distinct projections satisfy Σ_body|p_i|+|p_tail|≤4 and |p_tail|≤2 on each chart. Thus Σp_i x_i≤(1−t)(4−|p_tail|)+t|p_tail|≤2. These caps also follow geometrically by convex interpolation of the safe full base (four magnitudes≤1/2 at old tail −1/2) with the removed axis. Interactions between opposite expanded charts (one shared old tail only) have dot≤9/25; adjacent expanded charts share just two body coordinates. Negative tail extension cannot increase the dot with another chamber's fixed donor because both donor tail signs are positive. The original within-chart six donor cuts are retained.

Even in this enlarged region no 22-row code exists. The general obstruction and degeneracy-safe integer-box machinery are explained in the companion 21-row proof. Here each row has coordinatewise maximum C=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,3/5). In a fixed sign orthant we have a·b≥Σ_i(C_i(a_i+b_i)−C_i²)≥2−||C||²=16/25>1/2. Positive hot and positive old-tail labels remain forbidden by the original argument, as the expansion is exclusively southern. Thus 22 would give two missing labels out of 24. The twelve exact symmetries give 48 representatives of the 276 choices. Use the same lower bound t>3/10 on aligned mixed triples when applicable: if the negative entry is already below −1/2 the claim is automatic, else all entries obey the original caps. The old directed 1/(2√2) lower bound is used only on edges whose endpoints BOTH have positive old-tail sign (and hence original caps). No such rule is assumed on the expanded negative-tail half. For that half encode |x_i|+|v|≤1 and v≥−3/5, allowing all admissible ordinary rows as well.

The standalone checker keeps all 48 after weak propagation, then eliminates 47 using the clipped-simplex real-box test. The sole missing-label pair [8,6] is eliminated by two children of a single bisection, three nodes total. It follows that the fixed positive-hot six-donor scheme, even giving all four rebuilt charts this enlarged negative-axis cone, produces at most 201129−2+4·21=201211. This is NOT an upper bound on arbitrary 26D codes. In particular the positive alternative of removing only one negative pole, using no fixed six-donor block, and installing two uncapped 27-row codes while keeping the other two old 26-row templates is not addressed. Nor does the cone restriction rule out candidates taking advantage of an individually removed signed tetrad beyond these pairwise strip guards.
