# Exact local obstructions in Takhanov’s 26-dimensional replacement chambers

No new numerical bound. The comparison is Takhanov’s [v0.2 release](https://github.com/k-nic/KissingNumbers/tree/77f0860bf93a745140e6e25481d3f60773b11441/version2) at 201,212. His exterior has 200,864 points; he installs 20 signed axes, 14 complete four-coordinate signed cubes, and four independent 26-point clipped five-dimensional codes. Comparable results and all algebra for this note use that pinned external construction, not a newly owned witness. Prior Lower17/30/34 covered a different question: the fixed 26-template, exchanges through two removals, whole-support permutations, and repeated numerical searches for a 27-code. We did not repeat a fixed 5D starting chart. Full comparison source: https://github.com/k-nic/KissingNumbers/tree/77f0860bf93a745140e6e25481d3f60773b11441/version2 .

**Sharp transverse excursion in the mixed six-dimensional chart.** For each original support split four regular magnitudes a1,…,a4 and original tail o; freely introduce magnitude h on the alternate tail. Include both signs and allow arbitrary real components. A mistaken opening hypothesis that all eight alternatives were full safe five-supports was rejected by native exact replay; only 16 of each proposed 32-point parity lift actually survive exterior. From all 2,601 true projected normals divided by 32, the only inequality not implied by coordinatewise caps, after fixing any signs and dropping dominated inequalities, is Σa_i+2h≤2. All four blocks and 64 signed chambers were independently checked. The 14 retained four-cubes give a_i+o+h≤1 separately by the four mixed tetrads. Coordinates have cap 1/2.

For unit vectors, h≤1/9 exactly. First, Σa_i≥2Σa_i² gives o²≥h(1−h). Thus if h≥1/4, o≥√3/4 and a_i≤1−o−h<1/3; even allowing both tails at 1/2, the norm is strictly too small. If h<1/4, let c=min(1/2,1−o−h) and L=2−2h. Since L≥3c, for fixed o,h the squared energy of the four regular entries is maximized by (c,c,c,min(c,L−3c)). On o≤1/2−h it is largest at 1/2−h; on the successive intervals cut by 1/2−h/2 and 1/2 the total squared energy is convex in o. Its three possible maxima are 5/4−3h+6h², 5(1−h)²/4+h², and 5/4−4h+5h². On [1/9,1/4] all decrease, and only the middle reaches 1 at h=1/9. Profile (4,4,4,4;4,1)/9 is an exact exterior- and tetrad-safe unit vector, for every sign, with two regular supported coordinates at most on any non-mixed tetrad. With o=0 the true exterior alone plus the signed axes force h=0. Thus an alternate-tail-only proposal cannot be saved by arbitrary angular optimization.

**Full continuous flat-ray stock, four chambers at once.** For unequal tilt amplitudes h∈[0,1/9] but equal five main amplitudes c=√((1−h²)/5)∈[4/9,1/√5], measure gain against all 26 source points in each chamber, allowing all 3,840 independent signed permutations of each template. At least two of the five main signs must differ between selected donors: with zero or one difference, the dot is at least 47/81>1/2. For a given main sign word s, any template row with positive signed sum has smallest possible donor dot at h=1/9. If the template row is t/d, its unavoidably removed status is tested by 8Σs_it_i>9d. Exact enumeration of all 254,475 independent sets in the five-cube (all distinct compatible main words) gives max (number of donors minus number of distinct incumbents removed) = 0 achieved ONLY by empty; the best nonempty value is −1, uniquely. Counts at −3,−2,−1,0 are 12,4,1,1. The extremal representatives for signs with Hamming distance at least two coexist, so the local statement is sharp. Independent orientations permute the cube and preserve the calculation. Active and lost source sets in different chambers are disjoint, so even coordinating these trades cannot improve 201,212 in this enlarged stock. This is not optimality for less symmetric amplitudes or a different exterior.

**Trying two for one instead of just asking for a free donor.** Delete a signed tetrad v and seek any unit w=c v+b with b supported outside its four coordinates. Keeping the other 13 complete signed tetrads and the real exterior already forces either w=v or c≤1/2. For ten of the fourteen supports there is a partition into three pairs of the six outside coordinates, each supplied by another tetrad sharing two inner positions; taking matching signs gives |b_j|+|b_k|≤1−c. For the remaining four supports the four regular outside coordinates make a K4 of such pair inequalities; their total squared norm is ≤(1−c)². To see this, given max coordinate x, if it is ≤(1−c)/2 the claim follows, otherwise bound by x²+3(1−c−x)² and use convexity between the midpoint and endpoint. Each remaining tail magnitude is ≤1−c by a genuine exterior row with signed inner sum 4, outside coefficient ±2 on that tail and no other outside component; checked all 4×16×2×2 exact tests. Consequently 1−c²≤3(1−c)²; no 1/2<c<1 is possible. This prevents any one actively blocked coaxial donor, regardless of transverse direction or latitude and even before imposing the old five-block templates. To profit locally one must change the four relative magnitudes or relax neighbouring constraints, not just split an old point symmetrically.

Follow-up pricing of unequal-amplitude donors was an exact bounded grid check, not a general theorem. For all unsigned ten-coordinate amplitudes in {0,1,2,3,4} then row-wise normalization, 632 distinct integer tuples obeyed all axis caps and had exactly one unsigned four-cube with exactly one full signing to drop (the other 13 cubes and the other 15 signings of the same cube must remain). Of these 416 were noncoaxial. On all 64 source-exterior sign cosets (16-element true independent sign group), only 1,536 (tuple, sign-coset) incidences survived the full exterior. For each of these, one of the current five-coordinate templates saw the projection (4,4,4,4,4)/√82 in absolute values; none of its 3,840 signed orientations admitted that donor, by direct integer dot checks. Thus in the tested grid even the two-for-one direction needs a larger amplitude alphabet, a prior multi-template trade, or a release of additional skeleton points. More broadly, sampled true signed-orthant LP and freely reoriented templates gave no unconditional free donor (91,358 linear programs, 7,862 different unsigned-amplitude/sign states; best objective squared radius ~0.750134, heuristic only). Short six-dimensional nonlinear 27-point searches also yielded no exact solution, not an impossibility proof.
