# The fixed 24 rotated-octahedron donors leave at most 20 rows in each original chamber

This is a continuum obstruction, not a new kissing bound. In [four_octahedra_21.md](four_octahedra_21.md), the fixed exterior, axes, surviving tetrads and 24 donors contribute 201129. Even allowing four distinct real chamber codes with no symmetry whatsoever, they contribute at most 80, giving at most **201209**. Rustem Takhanov's v0.2 construction at 201212 is not improved or bounded by this result. This proof closes only the specified new donor architecture with rebuilt rows confined to their original five coordinates and magnitudes at most 1/2.

For a hypothetical 21-row chamber label each row by signs in the order (u,r1,r2,r3,v), assigning a sign to zero arbitrarily. At most one row per orthant: since a,b∈[0,1/2], ab≥(a+b)/2−1/4, and each vector's sum of magnitudes is at least twice its squared norm, i.e. 2. A same-orthant dot is thus at least 3/4. Eight (++ on u,v) labels are forbidden: u+v≥1/2 and the three parallelism forms in four_octahedra_21 form orthogonal rows of length 3, hence M(r)≥√3||r||≥√(3/2). The score exceeds √10089/2. A 21-row configuration therefore has exactly 3 omitted labels among 24.

Two elementary starting lower bounds shorten the certificate. If another occupied orthant differs at exactly i, then |x_i|≥1/(2√2): write d=|x_i| and minimize x·y on that adjacent capped orthant. Giving |y_i| its maximum 1/2 can only help. By concavity of the square root, the other magnitudes have minimum weighted sum with three at 1/2 and one at 0; the sum of the three smallest other magnitudes of x is at least 1+√(1/4−d²), by concentrating square mass subject to the cap. The dot is thus ≥(1+√(1/4−d²)−d)/2. In either mixed hot/tail quadrant let t be the magnitude of the negative entry. Each other magnitude is ≥√(1/4−t²). In any of the six regular sign triples aligning up to overall sign with one of the cut's tripod forms, the score is at least 169√(1/4−t²)−50t, which at t=3/10 equals 52.6>√10089/2 and is decreasing. Thus those t exceed 3/10.

There are 2024 = binom(24,3) choices. The tripod matrix is conjugate by diag(−1,1,1) to 3I−2J, with J the all-ones matrix. All signed permutations obtained by this conjugation from S3 preserve M, as does simultaneous negation of the three regular coordinates. Using just these 12 exact symmetries gives 246 classes; no symmetry is imposed on the rows themselves. The script takes one representative of every orbit.

**Continuous elimination, not a point sample.** Enclose each nonnegative row magnitude in [l_i,h_i]. A box can only be removed via a necessary condition. Squared-norm interval bounds and all six donor inequalities narrow intervals. For an occupied adjacent pair flipping i, use both x·y≤1/2 and ||x−y||²≥1: the concordant contribution to the dot is at least Σ_{j≠i}l_j l'_j, and the concordant contribution to squared separation is at most Σ_{j≠i}max(h_j−l'_j,h'_j−l_j)². These inequalities propagate oriented lower bounds for the flipped magnitudes.

The key stronger test uses **clipped simplex vertices**. For any signwise dot with disagreeing positions D, bound each negative contribution below by −h_i h'_i. On the agreeing set P, the squared amplitudes s_i of x lie in the rectangle [l_i²,h_i²] and satisfy Σs_i≥1−Σ_{D}h_i². Since the remaining dot is nondecreasing, its minimum is at the lower corner if this already satisfies the constraint, or on the equality slice otherwise. For fixed other row, it is a nonnegative linear combination of square roots of the s_i, hence concave on that polytope and minimized at a vertex. Applying the same argument to the second row reduces a lower bound for the joint dot to the finite set of vertex pairs. At a slice vertex all but at most one coordinate are box endpoints. The same reasoning applies to a single donor inequality, with negative coefficients paid at their upper endpoints and remaining coefficients positive. If **all** relaxed completions on the resulting vertices violate the cap, no real row(s) in the original boxes are possible. Using only Hamming distance one and two pairs is a legitimate relaxation.

Implementation: integer box endpoints in units of 1/32768; they enclose real values and are not assumed point coordinates. Every square root is rounded outward or, when lower-bounding a positive dot, downward. The corrected integer square root rt enforces t²≤n<(t+1)²; all integer products/sums used for decisions are below 2^53. The donor threshold is rounded *up*, never down. Excluded endpoint intervals are discarded, retaining the common boundary, or the surviving interval is bisected into two closed children. Any unresolved leaf triggers failure rather than a proof. Search-order scores do not enter exclusion tests.

Run `node verify_one_tripod_21.js`. Of the 246 representatives, 71 survive the first propagation, 17 survive clipped-simplex pruning with slice width 400 units, and 8 survive width 75. The remaining eight missing triples, in bitmask convention where + is 1 at bit positions 0 through 4, and their real-box tree sizes are:
`[8,6,3]:7; [8,7,6]:5; [10,6,0]:11; [16,14,1]:373; [16,14,3]:43; [18,8,7]:71; [18,9,6]:143; [18,12,3]:3`.
All 656 nodes of these eight trees resolve, with no unresolved boxes. Hence 21 is impossible. The argument does not say 20 is attainable or exclude another tripod, asymmetric donors or genuinely six-dimensional chamber rows.

## A genuinely different constructive target

The certificate fixes the six positive-hot donors from one tripod in each original chart. A different exact class is to add three of the six reflected negative-hot donors from the [compatible 48-donor carrier](takhanov26_48_donors.md) in each chart, not all six. This adds twelve donors but pays one more shared tetrad row globally, not twelve. All retained exterior and cross-chart conditions follow by restriction from the certified full carrier. Its fixed count is therefore 201140. A common 19-row clipped code adapted to the chosen three negative-hot signs would give **201216**, or an asymmetric total of 73 rebuilt rows would give 201213. No such chamber is supplied. A search over signed permutations of the old template alone cannot realize this sufficient target; try co-designing different chamber profiles or another genuinely new carrier. The cap-15 result for the full doubled carrier does not apply to this partial one, and this paragraph makes no claim that partial doubling enables enough rows.
