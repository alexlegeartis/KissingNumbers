# Binary quotients for line-labelled Golay additions in 21D



In [Kevin Li’s 31,560-point construction](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2610.00248v1), one component is the 1024-word Cayley group generated by 21 punctured Golay lines. A sufficient promotion is an independent dominating omitted set with <64 words. Any possible line labelling (not necessarily produced by that sufficient condition) requires the omitted set to dominate. If omitted words are a union of r cosets of a translation subgroup and the quotient is F2^q, then O+B=F2^q, where O are the r quotient centres and B are the images of zero and the 21 lines, so |B|<=22. General lemma: for |O|=m+1>=2, |B| >= 2^(q-m) K(m,1), where K is the minimum cardinality of a radius-one binary covering code. Translate one centre to 0, and map the m unit vectors onto the other centres by a linear map pi:F2^m -> W=span(O). The image of the closed Hamming unit ball is O. For each W-coset c+W the full preimage of (B−c)∩W covers the m-cube by radius-one balls. Its cardinality is 2^(m−dim W) times the size of B∩(c+W). Sum over cosets. For q=7, <=7 centres (enlarge to 7), K(6,1)=12, so |B|>=24. For q=6 and <=3 centres K(2,1)=2 so |B|>=32. For higher periods use volume directly. Therefore no improving line-centre construction can have translation stabilizer of rank >=3. Citation: G. Cohen, A. Lobstein, N.J.A. Sloane, Further Results on the Covering Radius of Codes (1986), https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me127.pdf. For rank8 a 15-centre quotient is not ruled out. Exact bounded rank8 exchanges leaving >=3 of Li's eight even centres in each of 155 two-plane quotients H⊂Li's even kernel did not find a 7-even plus <=8-odd independent dominating complement. This search is not a general no-go.
