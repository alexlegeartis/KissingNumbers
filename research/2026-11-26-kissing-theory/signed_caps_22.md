# Sharp signed caps and two non-antipodal 22D ties

The named 49,896-point benchmark is Leech’s, as reported in [Cohn–Li, Table 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04916v3). The following two configurations tie it, not improve it.



## Explicit parents

Use 24-bit extended binary Golay G. Its generators are cyclic shifts i=0,...,11 of g(X)=1+X+X^5+X^6+X^7+X^9+X^11 on the first 23 positions, with the parity bit at position 23. Keep 21 low bits, with D the other three. Write G_d for the 512 projected words with deleted triple equal to d. Write O for the 210 weight-8 supports outside D and L for the 21 projected five-sets from octads containing D. Distinct L meet in one coordinate. Every O meets each L in at most four. Normalize every following vector only at the end; its squared norm is 32 and acceptable dot is at most 16. Coordinates are encoded as 21 ordinary entries and one head coefficient H whose actual coordinate is sqrt(3) H.

* Equator: all 840 two-coordinate roots (entries ±4) and 210 × 128 signed octads (entries ±2 on O, even minus parity): 27,720.
* For each hemisphere σ = ±1 choose deleted triple d_σ, with d_+ = 000 and d_- distinct. For every c in G_d and i=1,...,21 use body entries (-1)^c_j, except at i multiply by -3, and H=σ. This gives 10,752 per hemisphere.
* For each L and σ use body entries ±2 on L, minus parity wt(d_σ) mod 2, H=2σ. This gives 336 per hemisphere. Total 49,896.

Within one sign coset distinct projected words differ on at least eight coordinates. Across distinct deleted triples their distance is at least five. Marked-body dot for two respective codewords at distance d is at most 21-2d+8; heads contribute +3 on the same side and -3 across. Identical word with two distinct marked coordinates has body dot 13. The parity of c on any line L equals wt(d_σ) mod 2 by Golay self-duality; therefore in a same-side mark-cap pair with the mark in L the restriction has a disagreement and body dot ≤10. If the mark is outside L then body dot ≤10 without parity. For off-side mark-cap pairs body dot ≤14 and heads contribute -6. Cap-cap: on one side bodies have unit-signed overlap at most 1, actual body dot ≤4, and heads contribute +12. On opposite sides body dot ≤20 and heads contribute -12. All full-base interactions follow from even octad parity, intersection ≤4, and root entries. This certifies all classes without lookup. For d_-=111 the configuration is antipodal and reproduces the layered model. For 001 vs 011, no marked vector has an antipode (complementation toggles 111, not either chosen triple). For 001 every cap has an antipode; for 011 none does. Thus the skew parents have respectively 28,392 and 27,720 points whose antipodes occur, versus 49,896 in the standard parent. Their nonisometry is intrinsic, independent of coordinate relabeling. Altogether puncture differences of weight 1, 2, 3 give three distinct classes, with no global priority claim.

## Sharp cap theorem, no lattice assumption

Let C be arbitrary signed weight-5 vectors in {0,±1}^21 with pairwise dot v≤1. Then |C|≤336. This classification does not assume restrictions on intersections of supports. For a pair put t=|supp(x)∩supp(y)|, v=x·y. Use F=(3/4)(v−1)(v+2)(2v+t+1). On the diagonal F=336; off-diagonal F≤0 using t≤5, |v|≤t and integral v. Put E2=(v²−t)/2, E3=(v³−3vt+2v)/6, the signed subset monomial Gram kernels. Direct expansion gives (4/3)F=12E3+7(vt−v)+2tE2+6E2+t²+t−2. First four kernels are positive semidefinite; vt−v sums Gram features x_i x_j² with i≠j. For M=|C| we have Σt≥25M²/21 and Σbinom(t,2)≥100M²/210 by Cauchy on single-coordinate and pair frequencies. Hence Σ(t²+t−2)≥(4/3)M² and ΣF≥M². Now 336M≥M². The explicit 336-point cap attained the bound by exact independent replay of all 56,280 pairs: dot histogram {1:28560, -1:26880, -3:840}.

At equality each coordinate occurs 80 times and each pair 16 times. Every Gram feature-sum appearing with a positive coefficient vanishes, hence so does the row sum of each corresponding kernel. The signed first-moment vector vanishes by summing the vt−v features over j≠i (four per vector with x_i nonzero). For any fixed x the row moments of (1,t,binom(t,2),v,E2,E3,tE2,tv) including diagonal are then (336,400,160,0,0,0,0,0). Offdiagonal F=0 allows (t,v) in [(1,1),(1,-1),(2,-2),(3,1),(4,-2),(5,1),(5,-3)]. Subtract the diagonal, solve the resulting linear system: (160,160,0,0,0,10,5). The first seven equations have determinant 672. Every support is occupied 16 times by a whole parity (the allowed nonzero same-support Hamming distances are 2 and 4), and the 21 distinct supports meet pairwise exactly once; their pair incidence is a 2-(21,5,1) projective plane. All independent choices of parity on each line conversely attain 336. The graph joining dot=-3 decomposes into 21 connected components, one per full parity class (the five weight-4 vectors generate the even length-5 code). Their spans recover the support planes and their intersections recover coordinate lines. Thus isometries are signed coordinate permutations. The incidence rank over F₂ is 10. This is not a claim of optimality among arbitrary real 21-dimensional spherical codes. Compare Cohn–Kumar, pp. 153–154, https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2007/13-9p.pdf: they derive the 21 supports/336 within a lattice using a support-distance premise, not the unrestricted signed-ternary theorem proved here.

## Universal ceiling for this family

Keep all 210 equatorial octad supports with a full sign parity per support (arbitrary, chosen independently), and all two-coordinate roots. Allow arbitrary selections of marked vectors of the above magnitudes (arbitrary 21-bit signs, arbitrary mark, either hemisphere) and signed 5-support caps (arbitrary supports/signs). For each mark i, the projected sign word must obey the sign-parity restriction on every octad containing i: every fully matching restriction would achieve forbidden dot 20, whereas a mismatch suffices. The octads containing i have F2-rank 9 (80 such octads, exhaustive for each i), so allowed sign words are empty or a coset of punctured G, of size 4,096. A 3-dimensional octad simplex with every basis word zero at i and having independent deleted triples gives a subgroup H of size 8. All its seven nonzero words puncture to weights 5,6,7; signs at i agree, yielding conflicting same-side pairs within each coset of H. Each mark and hemisphere thus contributes ≤512. Four explicit triples in the above 24-bit encoding cover every possible i among their common zero positions: (2b2230,532026,820336), (2ad060,564460,9e8048), (216f00,542e04,850d14), (2b2230,42a294,8120f4), all hexadecimal. The projected Golay and all seven nonzero words for each triple were verified independently by a second generation over the 4,096 coefficient masks. Therefore at most 27,720+21×2×512+2×336=49,896 even allowing arbitrary mutual trades across these layers. To beat the comparator change at least one magnitude/height, or the equator itself.
