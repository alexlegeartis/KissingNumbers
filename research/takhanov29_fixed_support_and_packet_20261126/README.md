## Exact saturation of the fixed-support D5 phase mechanism

The external witness has 164,362 equatorial Leech rows, 2,963 added non-midpoint rows, and 64,905 midpoint rows. There are 32,549 distinct midpoint bodies of squared norm 96; 32,356 have two rows with opposite root tails of squared norm 32, and 193 have one. A double necessarily has antipodal tails; all old doubles use a norm-32 D5 root, and relabelling any of these same bodies does not change its interaction with any equatorial row.

For each of the ten supports {a,b}, the two projective root directions 4(e_a+e_b) and 4(e_a−e_b) have the same absolute inner product with every line on any other support (16 if the supports intersect once, 0 if disjoint). At a fixed support they are orthogonal and have self product 32. For two double bodies of a fixed support, join them if their body product exceeds 32; their original phases are opposite, and phase choices remain legal iff a whole connected component is switched or unchanged. There are exactly 542,785 such edges in ten graphs and 122 connected components. Each actual added non-midpoint row can set a unary lock if the flipped line would violate the 64 dot threshold; there are 11 directly locked double bodies. This is an exact iff description, not a heuristic.

A proposed double on one of the 193 bodies has to (i) survive its own external and other-single constraints, (ii) not conflict structurally with any other support of the doubled bodies, (iii) request consistent phase bits from every adjacent component, and (iv) not require an impossible flip under unary locks. Cross-compatibility uses the maximum absolute tail dot (both tail signs admitted for each double). There are 448 trials after (i), 94 after (ii). All 94 fail (iii) or (iv): 18 parity only, 18 parity + another fixed singleton, 28 only another fixed singleton, 30 auxiliary plus another fixed singleton.

The second exact result then unlocks **all** 193 singleton signs and supports simultaneously. Every one of those 193 bodies can choose any oriented D5 root, or one antipodal pair; the 32,356 double bodies keep their two-coordinate supports, but all 122 component phases can switch. The 2,963 non-midpoint rows remain fixed. For each of the 193 bodies one evaluates all 20 projective roots and both signs, using real integer inner products with every fixed row. At any other doubled support the interaction is sign independent, while a matching support fixes the neighboring component bit when the body dot exceeds 32; conflicting requests in a component rule that local mode out. Allowed modes store their phase requests. Two modes are compatible exactly if their tails pass the actual pair threshold and their requests for every shared component agree. Global consistency of component bits is equivalent to pairwise consistency of these requests (they are unary assignments). This exhausts 853 surviving modes, 240 of them double candidates. To detect any strict gain it is enough to promote exactly one such body because additional doubles can each be demoted to one of their two signs, which cannot create a new conflict. Starting from each of the 240 promotions, exact binary-relation arc propagation removes 204 targets outright; the remaining 36 are all eliminated by splitting one variable into its two surviving choices and repeating propagation. The certificate has 312 nodes; no optimization solver is required. Thus this precise family cannot exceed 232,230. It does not allow moving body coordinates, moving auxiliary rows, changing the support of an already doubled body, or demoting an old double in a coordinated trade.

## A physical six-head packet

Four of the easiest proposed promotions share the same two double blockers. Their six heads in first-occurrence midpoint order are [31061,32169,31680,31719,30909,32319]. Direct integer checks show they are m±v₁, m±v₂, m±v₃, with ||m||²=64, ||vᵢ||²=32, and all four axes pairwise orthogonal. Body products are 32 within opposite pairs and 64 across the three pairs. Set t₊=(0,4,4,0,0), t₋=(0,4,−4,0,0). The old A four rows have residuals ±v₁−t₊ or ±v₂+t₊; the old B four have residuals ±v₃±t₋ independently. There are 8 local rows, 232222 elsewhere. This is an actual packet present in the comparator, not invented coordinates detached from its exterior.

**All six heads fixed, arbitrary real tails:** remove all eight packet rows and keep the others. Each of the six heads has the same exact six allowed unit-tail directions at squared norm 32. The true walls contain |t₀±t₄|≤0, |tᵢ|≤4 for i=1,2,3, |t₂|+|t₃|≤4, and |t₁+t₃|≤4. Equality in squared norm forces |t₁|=4 and exactly one of |t₂|,|t₃| to be 4. The latter case requires opposite signs. The six vectors are checked against every remaining projected normal. Their three projective lines are t₊, t₋, and (0,4,0,−4,0); only the first two are mutually orthogonal. If 9 points fit over six sites, at least three sites must double, so doubles occur in at least two of the opposite-head groups. Their lines must be orthogonal because cross-group body dot is 64 and both signs are present. Only t₊ and t₋ can be those lines; the third group would require a tail orthogonal to both, which the exact six directions do not provide. But a packet using only two groups has at most 8 points. This is a continuous tail result, independent of a finite grid.

**Move the A bodies, keep all B and the other 232222 rows:** take any new raw point with common center m and residual in span(v₁,v₂,v₃) plus all five tail coordinates. The four retained B signings imply exactly that the v₃ component vanishes and t₁=t₂. Two actual exterior normals further force t₀+t₄=0. Write the residual in coordinates (x₁,x₂,z,w,u) as x₁v₁+x₂v₂ and physical tail 4(z,w,w,u,−z). Its squared norm must be 64, equivalently x₁²+x₂²+z²+w²+u²/2=2. Actual projected normals give |x₁|+|x₂|≤1, |z|+|w|≤1, |xᵢ|+|z|≤1 for i=1,2, and |u|≤1. For w≥0 they also give |z|+2w+|u|≤2; for w≤0 they give |x₁|+|x₂|−w+|u|≤2. The exact replayer checks each signed version directly against normals reconstructed from all 232226 retained rows. On each w-sign translate to five nonnegative magnitudes, with the six displayed upper walls and five nonnegative lower walls. There are 19 distinct basic feasible vertices per sign. Cramer's rule and integer comparisons show the positive definite quadratic is at most 2, and its only attaining vertices are (1,0,0,1,0) and (0,1,0,1,0). Strict convexity excludes equality at any nonvertex. At each sign of w, points from different v directions conflict (residual dot=32); at most two per sign, at most four in total. Thus a real affine rebuild with common center does not help if all B remain. This says nothing about rebuilding all eight jointly or moving the center coefficient.

## Open construction pursued here

Allow replacement of all eight local rows and arbitrary independent center coefficients in the full 9D linear span of m,v₁,v₂,v₃ and the five independent tails. Keep every other one of the 232222 rows. Search for nine rows of squared norm 128 with mutual dot≤64 in this space. Each nonnegative center coefficient allows compression of all exterior halfspaces to 1426 exact projected normals; 1416 can be active on the unit sphere. The exact checker reconstructs these constraints, checks the full input and Gram metric, and can verify any candidate rounded to integers in the orthogonal basis by squaring positive raw dot products. Such a successful check would give 232222+9=232231. Moving center coefficients makes all the preceding fixed-center exclusions inapplicable. Numerical results are archived separately.

## Replay

- `verify_phase29.mjs /path/to/d29_certificate.npz` uses Node built-ins only, rehashes the NPZ, constructs the support graphs and 853 true modes, and verifies the 312-node refutation without SAT/DFS search. The full hosted readback reproduced the advertised counts.
- `verify_packet29.mjs /path/to/d29_certificate.npz [candidate_file]` reconstructs the Leech shell, validates both rigorous packet scopes, and optionally checks a numeric 9-row candidate after integer normalization. Hosted readback reproduced 164362 retained shell rows, 576 walls when B stays, and 1426 when all eight are moved.

**Degeneracy and a different omission branch.** Using the original signs of all 193 singletons, exactly 104 of the 122 phase components carry no external or singleton lock. Their independent flips give 2^104 distinct labelled coordinate sets of size 232,230, not claimed non-isometric and not a size improvement. The allowed old mode at each singleton is unique, and its requested component values are all zero; this counting check is included in the phase checker. By contrast, once an old singleton may be omitted, compensating with two promotions could improve by one, and the 240-target full-occupancy certificate no longer applies. A bounded hosted branch search of that variant hit its 150,000-node ceiling without producing a witness before the cap; this does not close it and must not be advertised as an exhaustion.


**Decision for future 29D work.** There is no new bound beyond Takhanov's 232,230. Do not rerun already exhausted fixed-support phase switching. The meaningful next mechanisms are (a) allow a profitable support change of an already doubled midpoint, or (b) replace a larger packet sharing actual frozen wall owners. The one-omitted-singleton branch remains open with a weighted mode/occupation solver, not another fixed-degree or fixed-center sweep. There is no reason to repeat the same 9D minimization longer before adding physically justified degrees of freedom.
