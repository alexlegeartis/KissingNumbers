## Four is the true maximum size of a mutually depth-six integral phase palette

The [Nebe Γ72 Γ model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862) on three Leech blocks uses half the direct-sum squared norm. Decompose each local Leech parity label into its A and B coordinates in the polarization, both 12-dimensional. Use the [pinned comparator and exact half-coset proof](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/a0875c99/verifications/improved/dim73-82-gamma72-depth6-lattice), especially its integer glue and theta count (each isotropic depth-six integral coset has exactly 136,704 norm-six heads). An integral shift is encoded by two A-label differences a,b relative to the third block, and the total B label t. Integral means represented by a raw integer Leech-block triple; such shifts are a 36-dimensional subgroup of (1/2)Γ/Γ. This does not speak to other half-shifts, quarter shifts, restricted shells, or actual shell contacts independently of the minimum-depth sufficient interface.

For each nonzero A-label u, let R_u be the 24 B-labels of norm-four Leech roots at A-label u, each with two signs. Reconstruct these from the literal integer Leech frame and the Nebe Gram file. Lemma 3.1 in Nebe gives exact other minima: anisotropic labels have norm-six reps, other nonzero isotropic labels norm-eight reps, zero norm-zero. The quadratic value of a local parity label (u,r) is u pairing r, since both halves are singular.

* For dependent a,b, one can use at most one nonzero block with raw norm ≤8, so depth ≤4.
* For independent a,b, put U={a,b,a+b}. A norm below six horizontally means either raw 4+4 or 4+6 in two blocks; any three occupied blocks cost at least raw 12. The A labels of the occupied blocks are any two distinct u,v in U. Rule out 4+6 iff, for every distinct u,v, the pairing v with t+r vanishes for every r in R_u. This first forces v to annihilate every difference in R_u, then gives one affine equation on t. The two values demanded by the two other members u must agree, and the three values must be linear on their two-dimensional plane. A raw 4+4 is exactly t ∈ R_u+R_v. Finally depth≤6 for every independent a,b,t: for any distinct u,v, the two simultaneous affine equations u pairing r=1 and v pairing (t+r)=1 on B have 1024 solutions, which produce raw 6+6.
* Enumerating all nonzero A labels yields ranks 6/8/10 for the R_u difference span, with 65/650/3380 counts. Among 2,794,155 planes, 975 pass the 4+6 gate for some t and those all already pass it at t=0. In each of the 975 planes, t∈U^⊥ has 1024 choices, exactly 686 hit a 4+4 sum, leaving **338** depth-six values t per plane. Thus there are exactly **1,977,300** integral depth-six cosets, all 975 good planes of rank type (6,8,8). This adds exact B-label feasibility/counts to the earlier necessary link constraints; the previous necessary projected link alone would not certify this.
* A pair-depth-six phase clique projects injectively to ordered A pairs: a repeated or dependent difference has depth≤4. Translate one phase to 0. Every other projection must be one of the 5,850 ordered bases of the good planes. Join two such pairs iff their XOR is also in that list. The literal graph has 5,850 vertices, degree14, 13,650 triangles and no 4-clique, verified by neighborhood intersections. Therefore at most 4 original phases. Conversely a triangle in the induced origin-neighborhood graph together with 0 gives exactly four phases, since every good projected displacement is valid with B-total 0.
* A whole norm-six shifted shell topped by a tail of norm²2 gives total norm²8. For two such tails of inner product μ, the crude certifiable horizontal squared separation required is 4+2μ. In the Zinoviev–Ericson 1,154-point tail data, the first eight vectors contain a literal acute clique: entries −2 on positions 0 and 1, plus positions (4,9),(5,6),(7,8),(11,12), each with first −2 and second ±2. Raw norm²16, raw pair dot8, so after scaling each pair has μ=1 and demands depth6 with this certification scheme. Thus their full transfer is impossible in these integral phases *with the minimum-depth interface*. Not a claim about the true cross distances of independently filtered or rigid shells. The complete transfer would have given 6,375,932,016, but that total is not a bound. Tail source: [Cohn's original data](https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/153312), with [literal extracted 13D block](https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/kissing-number-13-zinoviev-ericson-reproduction/blob/main/Ze99%201154%20data.md).

## Design averaging forbids any coordinate scaling of the full 85D eleven-chord QR48 section

Compare the extant [86D fixed-site/eight-chord QR–Pless variant](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/a0875c99/verifications/improved/dim86-87-axial-pless). One apparent 85D shortcut is to retain all non-chord coordinates and compress 11 selected triangle coordinates to centered chords. Raw doubled dense population would be 2 × 2^11 × 3^13 = 6,530,347,008, 233,025,898 above our 85D entry. This is not a bound.

The ternary code C is [48,24,15] and self-dual, e.g. [Harada et al., *On Extremal Self-Dual Ternary Codes of Length 48*](https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/154281), and [Leech–Sloane §1.6–1.7](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1971-081-3) gives the extremal weight counts including A15=415104 and A48=96. The supports of weight-15 words form the classical 5-(48,15,364) design, [Assmus–Mattson (1969), *New 5-Designs*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9800(69)80115-8). Alternatively their design criterion applies since self-duality gives at most 10 nonzero dual weights ≤43 (15,18,…,42), and 10=d−5. Weight divisibility by 3 follows from self-orthogonality; both QR48 and Pless48 have the same applicable parameters, regardless of their inequivalence. Only 4-design is needed to rule out 11 chords, but 5-design gives the exact 12-chord rigidity below.

Choose any marked set M, positive weights w_i on the 48 mutually orthogonal local blocks, arbitrary allowed pair of trits at each of the marked sites, and ordinary unit triangles at the other sites. Each mark is represented as the literal chord with midpoint removed: raw norm contribution 3w_i/4 in a marked block or w_i in an ordinary block. Each differing local trit contributes squared distance 3w_i in either case. Thus everything in one sign-copy has squared norm N=W−T/4, W=Σ_i w_i, T=Σ_{i in M}w_i. The kissing condition after normalization is raw squared distance≥N. With ≤12 marks, every min-word difference is realized by an actual pair: any two allowed trits realize both nonzero F3 differences in the two orientations, while the zero difference allows either; the projection of C on the marked positions is surjective because no dual word is supported on ≤12 positions. Thus for every minimum-weight support S, necessarily 3Σ_{i in S}w_i≥W−T/4.

Average over the 1-design: (45/48)W≥W−T/4, so T≥W/4>0. Now condition on S avoiding any three distinct positions Q. The 4-design and inclusion–exclusion ensure every other position has conditional inclusion probability 15/(48−3); hence averaging gives W−Σ_Q w_i≥W−T/4 and Σ_Q w_i≤T/4. When |M|≥3, average over triples in M: 3T/|M|≤T/4, so at least 12 chords are necessary. For 1–2 marks, choose Q containing the full marked set; T≤T/4 impossible; for no marks the first average suffices.

At exactly 12 marks, all marked triple sums must achieve T/4, so all marked weights equal a=T/12. The triple bound on two marked sites and any unmarked site gives each w_i≤a, hence W≤48a. Now condition on avoiding four marked sites (5-design): 45(W−4a)/44≥W−3a requires W≥48a, so *all* coordinate weights equal a. This is sharp for a single copy: with uniform weights, its norm²45a and the weight15 distance is 45a. The two antipodal copies need their separate far-distance check; no optimum or stronger classification is claimed for coupled metric, nonlinear tag, a partial-label bank, or a different external carrier.

## A generic size bound for the strict positive-dot palette

The same exact graph has a deterministic proper seven-coloring, verified on every adjacency; the class sizes are (1149,1145,1103,1018,913,504,18). If a complete family of tail directions uses integral depth-six source phases and reserves pairwise depth six for **every strictly positive tail dot**, rather than only if dot>1/2, coloring the projected phases partitions its tails into seven nonpositive-dot codes. Repeated projected phases also have difference depth≤4, so any tails assigned to the same projection must be nonpositive in this auxiliary interface. Rankin's orthoplex bound permits ≤2m tails per color in m dimensions; hence at most 14m tails, only 182 in 13D (24,880,128 norm-six heads). This closes every m=13 tail approach of that stricter type, not a scheme that uses depth five in the angular gap 0<dot≤1/2, not exact cross-shell screening, and not any globally unrestricted kissing bound. The right bypass is to use that angular gap or a substantially different character lattice, rather than guessing more depth-six palettes.

## Constructive escape: eight mutually depth-six phases in the full half-lattice, seven actual depth-six fibers

The four-phase limit applies only to integral raw block representatives. In the full (1/2)Γ/Γ there is an **eight-phase palette containing zero**, with all nonzero pair differences at depth exactly six. This construction does not itself improve the live 85D count. It gives an independently derived **tie at 74D** against live 6,218,995,830, and new half-shift architecture.

Write the Barnes basis b₁=(1,1,α), b₂=(0,β,β), b₃=(0,0,2), where β=1−α, αβ=2 and α²−α+2=0. These are literal [Nebe coordinates](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862), §2.1. A half-shift is given by its three Leech module coefficient classes (x,y,z)∈(Λ/2Λ)^3: a representative of 2r is the raw triple (x, x+βy, αx+βy+2z). Let A be the 12-dimensional α-image over F₂, on which α=1, β=0. Take coefficient triples all in A. If x=0 as a class, pick its representative 0; choose y=αλ with λ parity in A, and z parity in A. Since βα=2, the half-shift r has integral raw representative (0,λ,λ+z), B-total zero and projection plane span(λ_A,z_A)=span(y,z). Thus every rank-two coefficient triple in this chart with good plane <y,z> has exact depth 6 by the checker above.

The full Barnes automorphism group is ±PSL₂(7), as in Nebe §2. Reducing its 3×3 matrices on the A factor gives a homomorphism into GL₃(F₂) factoring out −I. It is nontrivial: the unitary reflection in b₁, with h(b₁,b₁)=2, acts by r↦r−h(r,b₁)b₁. The Hermitian form is integral on Barnes, so this is a Barnes automorphism (its own inverse); explicitly b₁↦−b₁, b₂↦b₂+αb₁, b₃↦b₃−βb₁, so the A reduction sends b₂ to b₂+b₁. Simplicity of PSL₂(7) and equality of the two group orders (=168) give all GL₃(F₂). By Hermitian tensoring, these are Γ isometries. They act by coordinate-row mixing over F₂ on pure-A coefficient triples, preserving the spanned A-plane. Hence any rank-two coefficient triple in A can be taken to one with x=0 and the same A-plane. All triples of this kind whose plane is good have exact depth 6.

Use the literal plane with u=720, v=8359937 from the reconstructed 24-bit A-label list. Let F₈=F₂[ξ]/(ξ³+ξ+1). For a=a₀+a₁ξ+a₂ξ², define the three A coefficient vectors
    (a₀u+a₂v,  a₁u+(a₀+a₂)v,  a₂u+a₁v).
The two coefficient rows are a and ξa in the fixed basis. Their rank is two for each nonzero a, because ξ≠0,1 and the field has no zero divisors. Every nonzero pair difference therefore has rank two and spans the good plane <u,v>. The eight literal triples, in a=0,1,ξ,1+ξ,ξ²,1+ξ²,ξ+ξ²,1+ξ+ξ² order, are:
    (0,0,0); (u,v,0); (0,u,v); (u,u+v,v);
    (v,v,u); (u+v,0,u); (v,u+v,u+v); (u+v,u,u+v).
For six of the seven nonzero phases the first coefficient is nonzero, so the raw first block of the half-shift is not integral; adding elements of Γ cannot make it integral. Only the zero and (0,u,v) phases are in the original integral chart.

The depth-six norm-six shell count 136,704 transfers to all seven nonzero phases by isometry, not by extending a theta argument to unverified characters. For any seven (or fewer) norm²2 tails forming a kissing code in m dimensions, attach a separate nonzero phase's complete shell to each. Every distinct-layer distance is at least 6+2=8, and each norm-eight equatorial comparison is at least 6+2=8; same-layer difference is an element of Γ of minimum squared norm eight. Any independent pure norm⁸ tail kissing code is compatible because its dot with the norm²2 tails is ≤√(8·2)=4. In 74D use six mixed tails and six pure tails aligned to a regular hexagon, obtaining 6,218,175,600+6×136,704+6=6,218,995,830, exactly the published live count by a different phase palette. In 75D seven mixed tails from the 12 cuboctahedral directions and all 12 pure produce only 6,219,132,540, below live 6,219,816,924. The origin phase contributes no mixed shell. Thus this is a verified palette and tied construction, **not** a new numerical record, not a transfer of the whole Zinoviev–Ericson 13D code, and not an extension retaining all existing non-Γ72 rows.
