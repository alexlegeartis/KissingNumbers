## 64-coordinate BCH: genuinely large bank, but no whole parent-cleared embedding in the affine GL class

For sign body of norm²64 and tail squared H, the old donor-safe min parent distance 14 implies 36 ≤ 4√(64+H), i.e. H≥17. A fixed tail raises the within-bank Hamming minimum required to (64+H)/4: if H in [17,24], distance 22 works. The classical extended BCH [64,18,22] binary code gives 262,144 bodies per fixed tail, twice the live 131,072 DG body bank. Truth-table trace representation over GF(64), polynomial u⁶+u+1: RM(1,6) plus traces of powers 5,9,21, with respective ranks 6,3,2. The replay checks all 2^18 words and exact minimum22. Its rank16 quadratic kernel gives 65,536 points per cubic quarter, with within-quarter minimum24. The cubic plane is {0,384604,573223,876923}; all three nonzero cubic differences have whole RM2 coset distance 18.

The six GL(6,2) orbits on cubic coefficient forms (lower terms stripped) have representatives [0,1,20,1064,1536,12640], sizes [1,1395,54684,468720,357120,166656], and exact closest RM(2,6) weights [0,8,12,16,14,18]. A coordinate cycle and elementary transvection generate GL(6,2): conjugation gives directed edge transvections of the coordinate cycle, and commutators give every elementary transvection along a path. Permuting/substituting coordinates preserves Hamming length and maps RM2 to itself. Enumerate the two basis operations on the 20-bit top coefficient space, then actual exact 2^22 quadratic+affine words per orbit reference. The orbit of the BCH cubic plane has 55,552 elements; the three directions of these planes partition all 166,656 type-18 labels. That statement is checked explicitly, not assumed from the field norm analogy.

Against the fixed 80 mothers, just 4,400 cubic labels clear every mother at distance at least14. There is no new label clearing at ≥16. Enumerating each possible eligible pair among the 4,400 labels lets one locate the unique BCH plane orientation when its difference is type18 and check the two remaining corners. No affine BCH plane has all four cleared corners under any invertible binary affine coordinate change. There are exactly 2,912 affine transformed planes with three cleared corners, supporting 196,608 bodies per tail *before* sparse/atlas compatibility. These counts concern this BCH embedding and fixed parent palette, not arbitrary length-64 binary codes or coordinate permutations.

For a fixed embedding, exact distance to any nonzero purely quadratic translate of the full BCH body code is 16, all 63 nonzero quadratic phases modulo the rank16 kernel. The three cleared quarters already realize each of the three nonzero cubic differences, so the same min translates to the cleared stock. Distinct phases still need heads at half-angle at most .5 in the same transformed BCH plane; there is no hidden quadratic phase with clearance18 to multiply heads.

## Why the three-quarter bank does not pay in the old D4 total-parity proof

The live two-coordinate heads are 3.1 times D4 roots (norm²19.22), and a dangerous predecessor sparse bank is a signed length-16 bank with precisely that pair of tail coordinates. One sign disagreement suffices for the angle; the current certificate ensures it via a shared *total-parity* inequality. For any fixed head and chosen three cubic quarters this same parity can apply only where their three cubic evaluations against the 14-body support agree. Every type-14 seeded mask is in RM3 and the parity pairing against cubic phase c is, exactly, the bit product c·rev(s), where rev maps every triple of variables to its complement and s is the mask's top cubic class. This follows because a polynomial sums to odd on all 64 points only through its full degree-6 coefficient; lower terms drop out. For the six tags [3,12,5,10,6,9], the base uses cubic shifts [822310,1025326,213640,27520,779738,1041562] of the 80 labels and exact weight14 shell. Each type-14 relevant cubic label contributes exactly64 masks, by orbit weight enumerators. The unavoidable **seeded masks with varying parity** minima over all 2,912 triples are [576,832,704,576,704,704] for these tags, independent of existing sign assignment. Each rejected bank costs 2,048; even all four heads over a single tag gross at most 786,432, below 576×2,048. Thus upgrading a tag by this old total-parity argument loses even before atlas/old donor charges. This is not proof of actual angular collision where extra guard rows happen to separate; use local common dual projection to attempt a stronger separator.

## Dense carrier redesign: fixed full period cannot beat 80

The 80 labels are five cosets of a translation period V of size16 (rank4); all 3,160 pair differences are type16. Exhaustively quotient the 20-bit space by V. A pair of full period blocks is compatible iff its difference coset lies entirely in types 16/18. Of the 65,535 nonzero quotient classes, 299 pass; distribution of minima is 1,395 at 8, 37,981 at 12, 25,860 at 14, 299 at 16. Translation fixes one block as 0; its neighbor graph has 299 vertices and max degree26. Exact clique search needs five more blocks to attain six (96 mothers); every such anchored clique is ruled out in 1,243 explored nodes. This proves 80 maximum **if the full old period is fixed**. A relaxed rank-three period scout was capped at 100,000 search nodes per selected seed and did not yield a candidate; it is not a rank-three certificate. Among all external-to-parent cubic labels, smallest old block count is five (64 labels), so naive one-label low-cost insertion is ruled out.

## 32-coordinate atlas: fixed-bank closure and a real alternative kernel

The published length-32 atlas bank is the [32,11,12] code generated by six affine functions and the five traces Tr(a x³) over GF(32). For two banks on one same head, body magnitudes √2 and capital tail norm²8 require Hamming distance at least9; at even parity it is at least10. Exact multi-source syndrome BFS over **the entire** F2^32 quotient of size 2^21 proves covering radius8, histogram [1,32,496,4960,35960,201376,691920,842208,320199]. Thus no additional binary body (even outside RM3) can fit on the same head while keeping the full existing bank. More broadly, within a fixed RM2(5) coset, the graph of weight-eight differences on 65,536 vertices has degree620 and least Walsh eigenvalue −20. The Hoffman bound is exactly2,048, attained by the bank: no replacement inside the same cubic phase, linear or nonlinear, can improve it.

But thinning one trace direction gives a nested [32,10,12] kernel. In its RM3(5) quotient (16 syndrome bits), there are 722 nonzero directions with coset distance≥10. Exactly 6,400 two-planes have all three nonzero such directions and all four distinct cubic phases; taking all four cosets gives literal [32,12,10], **4,096-point** atlas bank replacements. No three-plane exists with all directions good over this fixed thinner kernel. One witness is quotient indices [0,608,22944,23488] with the monomial order of the portable checker. This is still an isolated bank code, not a kissing result. Different atlas hyperplanes at the same head can use an affine 3-flat separating the old restricted cubic from the whole new affine phase span; restriction to their 4D intersection makes every nonzero cubic coefficient functional a permissible 3-flat test. To turn the four-phase bank into a gain one must choose compatible full palettes on its own hyperplane, preserve angles to the tagged Hamming banks with shared dual guards, and count only independently certified old survivors.

Backup considered: [Harada, arXiv:2611.29304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29304), dim50 odd min-norm5, q⁷ shell 828,921,600. Norm-six differences are the precise conflicts in this shell after an added unit tail; simple signed-axis coloring would use at most 36 colors by 68D. No such coloring/subset achieving a live threshold was found. Do not equate bare norm-seven population with a valid kissing bound.

## Late verified positive intermediate atlas result

The 6,400 four-quarter banks induce 70 distinct cubic two-space directions. The same 80 restricted dense mothers and the literal 46 selected hyperplanes of the diagonal-triples predecessor were tested. For each candidate hyperplane H and phase c, evaluate cubic ANF of restrictions (coordinate order is induced by deleting the lowest-index nonzero normal bit). First require distance ≥4 from every old restricted cubic coset of RM2(5). Then for each other retained hyperplane J intersecting H, require c restricted to their affine 4-flat I to be different modulo degree2 from J's frozen cubic on I. If all four phases of a bank pass, the old restricted cubic lies outside the affine restricted phase group. A separating binary functional on the four cubic coefficients exists, and every nonzero functional is the oriented 3-volume of some 3-flat since ∧³ F2⁴ is dual to F2⁴. Therefore one common weight-eight parity check certifies a body mismatch: enough for all cross-hyperplane same-tail angles. Other 24-cell tails have dot≤4 and are already safe at overlap≤16. This checks the full conservatively retained 46-atlas geometry **between different hyperplanes**, not the other lower-stock interfaces.

Exactly 32 of 46 H have at least one such four-phase bank. On zero-based hyperplane 72, for instance, one exact four-phase set is [560,621,805,888], with outer cubic plane [0,93,277,328]. The 4,096-point bank remains a component, not a new kissing bound. Prioritize a coupled 24-tail/64-quadratic-shift optimization on one H; distinct body banks with shift difference0 collide, nonzero min shifts of 6 can only accompany negative tail dots, shifts of 8 are universally sufficient in that same H. Or reframe subset of higher-dimensional sparse tags with a common dual map.
