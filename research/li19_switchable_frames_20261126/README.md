# Switchable frames and a new cuboctahedral body in dimension 19

**Attribution and target.** The 19D comparison is Kevin Li's 12,460 (https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00248v1); strict target 12,461. The checked repository's rational Li control uses marked amplitude 747/1000 and diagonal tail 817/1000 and checks all pairs (research/character_families_20261104/build19.py in alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers). The 17D comparison is Cohn–Li 5,730 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04916v3). The 18D project bound is 8,358 (verifications/improved/dim18-bent-hexagon/); the fixed hexagonal carrier already has stronger continuous caps; we did not repeat it. Our new 19D work is a co-reconstruction of the sparse and dense layers, not another search of the old 5,360 rational directions with fixed body.

**A switchable record-sized skeleton.** Li’s norm-eight sparse 10,668 points include 192 vectors with axial tail ±2f_i and four head entries ±1 on one of four pairwise disjoint tetrads T, with odd sign parity. For each of the 24 (T, signed axis) cells independently replace its eight heads by the coordinate orthoplex {±2e_j : j in T}. Every switch preserves all Li’s 1,792 added rays and all other cells. Against the nonaxial sparse body this is immediate: the 16-head roots have dot ≤4, the equator octads have dot ≤2, and the shoulder sextads have head dot ≤2 plus tail dot ≤2. Pure tail roots have dot ≤4. Against rational Li additions the coordinate type has raw dot ≤2·1000+2·2000=6000 on an axis word (square strictly less than 2·19,558,009); diagonal words give at most 2·1000+2·817=3634 (square strictly less than 2·18,002,467). Within an axial slot distinct cells have disjoint support; across distinct nonopposite slots tail dot zero and head dot ≤4; at opposite slots tail dot −4. This gives 2^24 distinct labelled realizations at Li’s cardinality. Not a new lower bound. The all-coordinate realization has the full D19 root system and is valid for the independent cuboctahedral body below.

There are at least 16 *different numbers of contacts in the sparse core*, a stronger distinction than the already trivial continuous perturbability of the strict dense rows. Each old tetrad head makes 6 equator-root and 24 equator-octad contacts (three octads contain the tetrad and eight complementary odd sign choices remain); a coordinate head makes 30 root and 0 octad contacts. In a direction with positive contribution 2 against a sextad, every support intersects the tetrad in 0 or 2 cells; in the two-cell case each whole 8-head basis contributes exactly 64 cross contacts against its 32 signed rows. No contribution at other shoulder angles (intersection ≤2). Pure tail, other-block and internal contact counts do not depend on phase. For two orthogonal signed axes over the same T, eight head coincidences in equal phases give 8 contacts, and differing phases give none. Thus total contacts drop by 8 times the cut of the graph K2,2,2 on the 6 signed axes for each T. One block allows cut sizes {0,4,6,8}. Four blocks allow totals {0,4,6,...,32}: 16 distinct contact counts with maximum drop 256. The 8+8 same-slot graph of strictly incompatible pairs between old and new bases is a connected 4-regular bipartite graph (each odd sign vector adjacent to its four matching signed axes; any two coordinates can be extended to such a sign word). Every maximum independent 8-set is therefore an entire basis. For one extra point x=(h,p) all 2^24 full phases collapse to 16 exact choices: on each T choose the smaller support function of the old and coordinate bases; this works simultaneously at every signed tail. Explicit scalar condition: for every T, min(max over odd signs s of h_T·s, 2 ||h_T||_infty)+2 ||p||_infty ≤4 in the norm-eight system. For multiple new points, the phases need not agree; the exact cost of a submitted compatible family is the sum of deficits computed by maximum bipartite matching on surviving 8+8 conflict graphs. This gives a positive follow-up to search *many* collectively freed holes, rather than claiming fewer contacts imply any free hole.

**Freeing the 256 absent heads: an entirely different 10,668-point body.** Use the odd 16D Barnes–Wall equator (4,320); the 12 pure D3 tail roots; the 192 mixed coordinate roots; and 12 times 512 sextad shoulder points whose 3-tail is a cuboctahedral root. View the latter as ordered edges of four tetrahedral vertices (±1,±1,±1) with even number of minus signs: v_ij=(w_i-w_j)/2. Assign each oriented edge its own bent coset of RM(2,4)/RM(1,4), indexed by z, and parity bit p. Its 16 distinct weight-six supports use all sign patterns of parity 1-p. State 2z+p in ordered-edge row-major order:
[27,67,88,27,89,66,44,54,26,71,45,26].
The replay fixes the exact monomial and coordinate indexing; it directly checks all 19,968 pairs of shoulder support categories and all equator incidence restrictions. Standard norm-eight normalization and threshold four. In particular all 12 banks are mutually compatible.

An added flat sign word c has head (2/3)(−1)^c and tail (sqrt(8)/3)d/|d|. For every positive-dot shoulder bank it must have parity p against the bank; then maximum head contribution 8/3 plus at most 4/3, while a wrong bank has tail dot nonpositive. Equator and D19 root constraints hold immediately. Among 64 affine RM(1,4) cosets any two different members of one coset have Hamming distance ≥8. Two cosets at bent difference have min distance 6, hence all pairs compatible regardless of tails. At any other distinct difference min distance 4 and all pairs are compatible iff tail angle ≥60 degrees; compare positive integer scalars by 4(d·e)^2≤|d|²|e|². Using all nonzero primitive integer d in [−2,2]^3, each of all 64 cosets has a possible placement; every one of the 41,664 triples of distinct entire cosets can be placed simultaneously. Thus the redesigned body permits every one of the 2,048 heads, even three full sign classes at a time; Li’s sparse body excluded the 256 zero-tail heads even for arbitrary real tails at a flat head. The new conclusion is not 2,048 simultaneous additions. In the specified finite direction menu 280 of the 635,376 quadruples fail, including [0,1,2,17]; the count 280 uses only the finite menu, and the final section separately excludes this particular quartet on the continuous tail sphere. An explicit joint 38-coset realization with a somewhat larger direction menu gives 1,216 dense additions or 11,884 overall, strictly below Li. A 57-coset witness would give 12,492; a truly asymmetric 1,793-point layer would give the first +1. Do not claim this body nearly meets either threshold. An initially found 64/64 singleton-eligibility design was discarded because matched pairs of cosets were jointly incompatible; the three-wise certificate is for the corrected displayed state.

**Why the dense and shoulder layers must change together.** With Li’s actual 1,792 rational auxiliaries fixed, for each directed slot all possible 448 equator-compatible six-supports were tested, in both full sign parities. Exactly the original 16 supports (even parity) pass. Holding also the *other* 11 full shoulders, allowing partial signs: per slot 1,536 individual alternatives survive the other shoulders; 512 original rays have zero dense blockers, 512 other rays conflict with exactly eight dense rows, and 512 conflict with exactly sixteen. No change of just this one bank can beat the record. Future coupled searches can start by pooling these precise 8-owner neighborhoods across different shoulders rather than repeatedly trying one missing head.

No improvement of the known 19D lower bound is claimed.
**Analytic four-class obstruction (not a grid artefact).** For full sign classes 0,1,2,17, all pairs except (2,17) contain word pairs at Hamming distance 4. Write x,y,z for an arbitrary tail (at the established flat-head amplitude). The unfavorable directed sextads enforce homogeneous inequalities v·d≤0. Direct substitution of the twelve displayed labels gives: class 2 only the direction (1,1,−1), class 17 only (1,−1,1), class 1 the line ±(1,−1,1), class 0 the cone (x,y,−y), |x|≤y. To avoid class 17, class 1 must take (−1,1,−1). Class 0 is then confined to the short great-circle arc between classes 1 and 2; it must separate by at least 60° from each end. But the ends are separated by only arccos(1/3)<120°, contradiction. This specific quartet fails for the entire continuous tail sphere. We do not assert the same for the remaining 279 finite-menu failures or allow changing the amplitudes in this proof. Reading back and executing the stable threewise and contact replayers reproduced all counts, including the joint 38-coset witness.

The four-class obstruction also permits independent tail directions for the 128 individual heads, still at the stated common head/tail amplitudes. The full classes 2 and 17 force their described single directions for every word. Each word in class 1 has a Hamming-distance-four neighbor in class 17, since translating an attaining pair by an affine word preserves the difference and traverses the entire first class. Thus the sign of its line is individually forced. Likewise each word in class 0 has a distance-four neighbor in each of classes 1 and 2, so the same short-arc contradiction applies to each. This does not treat varying the amplitudes, or deleting words rather than inserting the full four classes.
