# QR weight-18 marked-pencil continuation in 90D

**Verified bound: 21,956,420,880 in 90 dimensions, adding 758 rays to a construction of the preceding size.** This is a new sparse shell, not a larger length-15 phase code. The preceding 90D bound was **21,956,420,122** in [QR–Pless phase7906](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/main/verifications/improved/dim86-90-qr-pless-phase7906), retaining the [rotated five- and three-plane layers](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/main/verifications/improved/dim86-87-90-rotated-plane-trades). Externally named Γ72 baseline is [Nebe 2010/2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.2862v3) plus an orthogonal 18D tail, not claimed best worldwide. The primary sources for ternary QR48 minimum 15 and the classical 415,104 / 20,167,136 counts at weights 15/18 are [Nebe, *On extremal self-dual ternary codes of length 48*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.1681) and [Leech–Sloane 1971](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1971-081-3). The new construction uses those established source-code facts; it does not claim to determine the optimum or certify worldwide priority.

## Explicit new point and why it clears both dense signs

Let Q be the same ternary self-dual QR48 parent in the pinned certificate; let M={0,1,2} be the three shortened coordinates. Call its length-45 shortening Q°. The dense code is the 2·3²¹ global-sign triangle images of Q°, each norm squared 45. In each plane define unit triangle vectors t_j and A2 roots \(h_j=t_j-t_{j-1}\), indices mod 3, norm squared 3. The explicit 48-trit words are:
- u = 000120000220020000000120000000000011102021111220,
- v = 122210000100020000000100000000000010201021001100.

Both belong to Q; u has weight18 and zeros on M, while v and v±u have weight15 and nonzero trits on all three marks. Let S be the support of u. Put h₀ at u_i=1 and −h₀ at u_i=2, and zero outside S. Its squared norm is 54. Restricted to S, the orthogonal complement of the projection of Q° is exactly the rank-two span of u and v restricted to S; the explicit checker verifies rank16. Every check column has first component u_i≠0. The trit pattern maximizing the dot with each dense sign has syndrome (0,1) under these two rows. Indeed the plus and minus maximizing patterns are \(\phi_i-1_{\{u_i=2\}}\) and \(\phi_i-1_{\{u_i=1\}}\) respectively with \(\phi=0\), and differ by −u. Thus neither syndrome can be repaired by zero or one changed trit. In each of at least two mismatched slots the contribution is ≤0 instead of +3/2, so the total absolute mixed raw dot is ≤24, strictly less than \(\sqrt{45\cdot54}/2\).

This phenomenon is exhaustive for the full-equal-amplitude 18-slot, signed A2-root family against both dense signs. Any dense projection of rank defect ≤1 has covering radius ≤1. With 18 occupied ordinary and 3 marks, defect≥3 is impossible by ternary Griesmer \(15+5+2>21\). For a 2D QR subcode on the S∪M support, its four projective lines have weights summing to three times the size of the union. The only options are \((15,15,15,15)\) on union20 or \((15,15,15,18)\) on union21. In the first case every direction has five check columns and shortening three cannot make one direction missing. In the second case the projective direction sizes are 6,6,6,3; the missing direction exists exactly when M is the last triple, the zeros of the unique weight18 line inside the pencil. On each such S, the two nonzero syndromes on the missing line yield 2·3¹⁶ dense-compatible phase seeds for each sign of the QR18 outer line (only seed pool, not all mutually compatible). The complete hosted enumeration of the two systematic halves certifies 2,244 such pencils, 6,732 pairs of marked weight15 words, and 312–406 old weight15 near neighbors per normalized QR18 outer line; together 800,172 oriented-incidence edges.

## Exact replacement of old sparse stock and zero-cost old translations

Do not union QR15 and Pless15. Replace the entire live weight15 outer set by the 130,944 weight15 words of Q°; they carry the same 7,906 known phase code words of Hamming minimum5 as before. The count of 130,944 is explicitly reproduced via the systematic [I|R] enumeration: any QR word of weight15 has precisely one half of weight at most7; forward uses R and inverse uses −Rᵀ. The complete unshortened min-15 shell is 415,104. This count can also be independently obtained from 3-homogeneity of the classical QR automorphism group on coordinates: PSL₂(47) has index two in sharply 3-transitive PGL₂(47), and transposing two points of a standard triple has determinant −1, nonsquare, so every 3-subset orbit is still transitive. Hence the zero-triple count is \(415104\binom{33}{3}/\binom{48}{3}=130944\). No Pless assumption is transferred to QR.

Different QR15 outer words differ in ≥15 slots, each costing at least squared distance3, independent of all phases. Within one outer word the Hamming-5 phase code has squared separation≥45, invariant under translating the entire phase code in F₃¹⁵. Dense–QR15 dots still use fifteen times 3/2. These statements preserve every old point in count (coordinates of the sparse layer intentionally change), including independently rotated phase code per outer word. All old small layers remain compatible with arbitrary QR15 phases exactly as in the existing bound.

For the given u18, if an old w15 has Hamming distance≥18 then each mismatched outer position contributes squared distance≥3, sufficient for the mixed squared-distance cutoff \(99-\sqrt{2430}\). Only d=15 matters, since the outer words lie in Q and each weight is divisible by3. Put b = number of occupied old slots outside S. Then same signed overlap is b+3, opposite signed overlap 12−2b. The upper b≤6 is immediate. For b≥3, the rank-two span of u,w has one line of weight18 and its three other lines all have weight≥15. The sum of weights of those four lines is three times the union size \(18+b\), so \(3(18+b)\ge18+3\cdot15\). At d=15 the only bad phase pattern is equality in every common same-signed slot and inequality in every opposite-signed slot: then distance45; if either condition fails, at least 9 is added and the mixed angle is safe. For a uniform translation of K, the expected number of bad phases is
\[
 |K|\,3^{-(b+3)}(2/3)^{12-2b}
 \ \le\ 7906\cdot 64/3^{12}\ <\ 1.
\]
An avoiding translate therefore exists for every old w independently. This is an exact, deterministic finite construction: for d≥18 use zero shift; for d=15 take the lexicographically first achieving zero bad phases. The strict expectation proves termination. More efficiently, expose the 15 shift coordinates in order and choose the smallest trit minimizing the number of as-yet-compatible bad old phases. Every still-compatible old phase has the same probability of becoming bad on the remaining coordinates (fixed equality and inequality counts), so the conditional expectation never increases; at the end it is an integer below 1. This is an exact algorithm with no trials and no loss of old count. It respects the prescribed sorted-coordinate phase convention of the live code. This replacement does not assume old antipodality after independently translating each phase bank.

## Packed witness and exact replay

Run `python3 verify.py` with a C++20 compiler (set `CXX` to override g++). The wrapper verifies SHA-256, decodes the same 7,906-word phase fixture, compiles `verify.cpp` in a temporary directory, and checks the reported exact count. No optimizer or network is used. The validator independently reconstructs the canonical QR48 code from the two degree-23 factors of \(x^{47}-1\), checks the multiplicative order, reduces the cyclic QR generator into \([I|R]\) with unchanged coordinates, and verifies the inverse half map is exactly \(IR=-R^\mathsf T\). The elementary \(RIR=I\) check verifies that the inverse census branch is exact. The published minimum-15 theorem is a named input, not inferred from self-duality.

The compressed witness SHA-256 is `b8b3c0060a5b7b51f4dd1aeef5e735e8dde6d25c3d375043d7027b4f43070209`; uncompressed `ba1695bee7fa7a3a3c3fc5b600e580b65f720579e17383d4545167600ed18c41`. The decompressed bytes are:
1. ASCII magic `QR1890v1`, then a little-endian uint32 point count (758).
2. For each new point, six little-endian 48-bit masks: the positions of trits 1 and 2 in u, then in v, then in the phase word. A coordinate at bit i is coordinate i of the original 48-space.
3. For each of the 130,944 shortened QR15 words ordered by their pair of full 48-bit masks (ones mask first, both numeric increasing), one little-endian 24-bit integer. Read it as a base-3 word with 15 digits (including leading zeros) which translates the old phase code in the increasing order of occupied physical planes. The input has no trailing bytes.

The new/new replay evaluates the exact signed raw root dot, with \(2\langle x,y\rangle/3=3(C_0-O_0)+O-C\), where \(C,O\) are same- and opposite-outer overlaps and the zero-suffixed counts further restrict to equal phases. The cutoff is 18 in these integer units. Dense compatibility of every new point uses its two explicit syndrome tests and QR membership; the check rows' restriction to the occupied support has the two required independent rows. For every new/old outer pair at Hamming15 the relevant old phase codes are compared by exact bitset intersections: equal phase on each same-signed overlap and unequal on each opposite-signed overlap. An empty intersection proves there is no short pair. The wrapper rechecks all \(\binom{7906}{2}\) old intra-code distances. The resulting file is a finite exact witness for the stated lower bound, not an assertion that the insertion is maximal.
