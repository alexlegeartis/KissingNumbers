SOURCE DATA AND ATTRIBUTION

All three source files are copied unchanged from Qihong Lin's public
certificate, as fetched into the workspace on 24 October 2026:
https://github.com/QH-Lin/kissing-numbers-33-39
Qihong Lin, New lower bounds for kissing numbers in dimensions 33-39,
22 October 2026, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23554808 .
SHA-256 hashes are pinned in base_certificate.json.

supports_3424.txt is Lin's 3424-support constant-weight code, CC BY 4.0.
The four changes to its use are specified separately in base_certificate.json;
the original source file itself has not been edited.

dense_kernel.txt and dense_representatives.txt originate with Yang et al.,
Large-Scale Autonomous Discovery of Kissing Number Constructions,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35051 and
https://github.com/Oxelra-AI/Qiushi-Engine-Kissing-Number-Research .
They retain their original CC BY 4.0 attribution, also recorded in the
file headers supplied by Lin.

CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

The dense code and original support family are existing results. The new
work consists of the four support substitutions and the 64-for-56 root
replacement, with a new exact verification program.

PRESENT ORBIT CONSTRUCTION

The original binary dense data are retained for provenance and replay of
the base verifier. The present result replaces all original dense vectors
by the exact normalized integer group orbits in orbit_certificate.json.
The new orbit representatives and complete integer orbit verifier are
additional work. Lin's eight-support data remain in use, with the four
previously certified replacements. The original CC BY 4.0 attribution
and notices above continue to apply to the supplied source data.
