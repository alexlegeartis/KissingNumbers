A KISSING CONFIGURATION OF 768924 POINTS IN DIMENSION 39
25 October 2026

THE RESULT

The supplied data and exact verifier establish

                         tau_39 >= 768924.

This improves Qihong Lin's 768916-point configuration by eight points.
The mechanism is a root replacement: four neutral support changes allow
64 new vectors to replace 56 roots. No enlargement of the binary dense
code or of the constant-weight support family is required.

THE RETAINED LAYERS

The dense layer has 327680 vectors of shape (+/-1)^39, obtained from the
ten cosets of the supplied [39,15,12] binary kernel. The verifier enumerates
the kernel and all coset differences and establishes minimum distance 10.
Thus distinct dense vectors have inner product at most 39-2*10=19<39/2.
Normalize this layer by sqrt(39).

The middle layer consists of 128 vectors on each of 3424 eight-coordinate
supports: coordinates +/-2, an even number of minus signs, and zeros
elsewhere. Normalize by sqrt(32). Begin with Lin's support family, making
these four changes (all coordinate and support indices are zero-based):

 index   old support                         new support
    4    0,2,14,15,26,29,33,34               0,14,15,26,29,33,34,35
  769    4,10,14,21,26,31,34,35              0,4,10,21,26,31,34,35
 1898    14,15,18,21,31,32,35,38             14,15,18,21,25,31,32,35
 2155    1,2,16,18,25,28,31,38               1,2,16,18,25,28,31,35

The updated supports still intersect pairwise in at most four coordinates.
The verifier proves this by checking that no five-subset occurs twice.
Within one support, even sign parity gives Hamming distance at least two.
Both facts yield middle-middle inner products at most 16.

Initially the root layer consists of all +/-4 e_i +/-4 e_j, i<j, also
of squared norm 32, for 2964 roots. Root-root and root-middle products
are at most 16. Against the dense layer, absolute products are at most
8 for roots and 16 for middle points. Both are below sqrt(32*39)/2.

THE NEW VECTORS

For a center a and a four-element leaf set B not containing a, define
H(a,B) to contain the sixteen vectors

                 +/-4 e_a + 2 sum_(b in B) s_b e_b,

where s_b is +/-1 and the number of negative leaf signs is even. The
center sign is independent. These vectors have squared norm 32.
Use the following four stars:

                center a       leaves B
                    0           2,16,26,29
                    2           0,6,20,36
                   31           10,14,21,38
                   38           1,9,18,31.

Delete all four roots on each center-leaf coordinate pair. There are
four times four incidences but only fourteen distinct pairs: (0,2) and
(31,38) are each counted twice. Thus 56 roots are deleted, and the four
stars add 64 vectors, for a net gain of eight.

THE ANGLE CHECKS

Within one star, points with equal center sign and different leaf signs
differ in at least two leaves. Their product is at most 16+4*(4-4)=16.
Opposite center signs give product at most -16+16=0.

For different stars (a,B) and (c,F), an upper bound valid for all signs is

             8 [a in F] + 8 [c in B] + 4 |B intersect F|.

For all six pairs of listed stars this is at most 16. The verifier also
checks every pair of the actual 64 new vectors directly.

A new vector can conflict with a root only on a center-leaf pair; all
those roots have been deleted. For each updated middle support S,
the certificate checks that either a is absent from S or |B intersect S|
is at most two. This gives maximum absolute product at most 16. The
independent verifier directly maximizes over all even sign patterns for
every new-vector/support pair, so this check is not an assumed property
of the support search.

Finally a new vector has l1 norm 12. Its absolute product with every
dense +/-1 vector is therefore at most 12, and

                           4*12^2 <= 32*39.

Thus the differently normalized layers are also compatible.

COUNT AND DISTINCTNESS

The dense layer has full support. Middle vectors have eight coordinates
of magnitude 2, roots have two coordinates of magnitude 4, and new
vectors have one coordinate of magnitude 4 and four of magnitude 2.
These shapes distinguish the four families. Within-family distinctness
is verified. The final count is

               327680 + 438272 + (2964-56) + 64 = 768924.

REPRODUCTION

Run python verify.py from this directory, or pass its full path.
Only Python's standard library is required. All mathematical checks use
integer arithmetic. The program checks source hashes, enumerates the
dense code differences, verifies the updated support family, constructs
the 64 new vectors, and checks every affected pair type. It requires no
solver, saved numerical distance table, or floating-point tolerance.

The support and dense data are copied from Lin's public certificate.
Attribution and licenses are in source/THIRD_PARTY.txt. The new contribution
is the compatible support changes and root replacements. This is a local
exact certificate; external submission or acceptance is not claimed.
