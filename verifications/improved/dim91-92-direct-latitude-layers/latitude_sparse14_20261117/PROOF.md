# Direct latitude augmentation in dimensions 91 and 92

This package adds entire latitude layers, not merely points on new axes. It is conditional on the already established minimum distance 15 of the explicit ternary Pless [48,24,15] code generated by `generator.npy`, and on the independently audited equatorial configuration of 21,943,640,934 points in R90 from `hexagon_sparse15_phase7812_20261117`. No publication is performed by these files.

## Exact finite input

The generator is systematic. Each of six supplied outer arrays has 31,680 distinct length45 ternary words of weight14. Prepending its indicated prefix (100,200,010,020,001,002) produces a word in the full Pless code. This requires no shell completeness premise.

The phase code H is the subset of the supplied 7,812-word length15 code with last symbol1, with that coordinate deleted. Its 2,609 words are distinct, and all 3,402,136 unordered pairs have Hamming distance at least5. The source code is Laaksonen–Ostergard's explicitly published ternary (15,7812,5) code, already independently audited; direct verification of H makes its provenance irrelevant to validity.

Let t0=(1,0), t1=(-1/2,sqrt(3)/2), t2=(-1/2,-sqrt(3)/2). Define h0=t0-t2, h1=t1-t0, h2=t2-t1. These roots have squared norm3 and mutual inner product -3/2. Every root has absolute inner product at most3/2 with every triangle vertex. The positive and negative root alphabets are disjoint. Roots of opposite signs have squared distance at least3, and distinct roots of the same sign have squared distance9.

For outer word c, replace its fourteen nonzero symbols, in increasing support order, by h_(H_j) for symbol1 and -h_(H_j) for symbol2. The resulting body vector b is in R90 and has squared norm42. Append a head of squared norm3, giving total squared norm45.

## Dimension 91

Use prefix100 with scalar head sqrt(3), and prefix200 with head -sqrt(3). Each layer contributes31,680*2,609=82,653,120 points, hence165,306,240 new points.

## Dimension 92

Use prefixes100,200,010,020 at heads (sqrt(3),0),(-sqrt(3),0),(0,sqrt(3)),(0,-sqrt(3)), respectively. Any distinct heads have squared distance at least6. Four layers contribute330,612,480 points.

## Pair verification

All vectors have norm squared45, so it suffices to prove squared distances at least45, equivalently inner products at most22.5.

1. Same layer, different outer words: their full Pless codewords have identical deleted prefixes, so the body Hamming distance is at least15. Each differing outer symbol contributes squared distance at least3, regardless of phase. The body squared distance is at least45.
2. Same layer, same outer word, different phases: at least five positions differ; each contributes squared distance9, hence distance at least45.
3. Distinct layers: their weight1 prefixes differ in at most two positions, so body outer distance is at least13, giving squared body distance at least39. In92D head distance is at least6; in91D it is12. Thus total distance is at least45.
4. Against the old sparse15 equator: the old outer word has deleted prefix000. Full-code distance15 implies body outer distance at least14. Thus squared body distance is at least42. The old and new body squared norms are45 and42, respectively, so their inner product is at most(45+42-42)/2=22.5. Equatorial heads vanish. This proof uses that the old sparse layer is built from the same Pless parent, as explicitly certified in its package.
5. Against the dense equator: each of fourteen roots has absolute inner product at most3/2 with a unit triangle vertex, hence total absolute inner product at most21. This argument is independent of the dense parent and includes its antipodal completion.
6. Optional old orthogonal poles (91D) or regular hexagon (92D): the new heads have norm sqrt(3), while these orthogonal points have norm sqrt(45). Cauchy gives absolute inner product at most sqrt(135)<22.5. The inequality is exact since135<2025/4.

Injectivity within a layer follows from disjoint signed root alphabets and distinct phases; distinct layers have distinct heads, and every new head is nonzero. Thus the new layers are disjoint from the equator. The optional orthogonal points have zero bodies and are also distinct.
