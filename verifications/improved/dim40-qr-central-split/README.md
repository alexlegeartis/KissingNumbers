The earlier project construction 1,126,365 is not the relevant comparison. ## Construction

Take the extended binary quadratic-residue [48,24,12] code. Keep the nine patterns 0,e1,…,e8 on eight marked coordinates and project to the remaining forty as sign vectors ±1. Every prefix occurs exactly 65,536 times (direct enumeration, or self-duality and d_min=12). Distinct retained projected words have d≥10 by triangle inequality. From the selected central word all-plus, every other projected word has d≥11: its source prefix has weight at most one. The 589,824 count reproduces ERS's dense-component bound rather than attributing their 40D record to us. Retain all 3,120 signed D40 roots and all 128 even sign rows on each of the 4,267 Smart weight-eight supports.

Remove the single all-plus dense word. Put a=√41−2, D=39+a²=2(a+1)². Add 40 daughters u_i with entry −a at position i and +1 in all other positions. Normalize each vector by its own norm; supports, and unnormalized daughter norm, distinguish the classes. Different daughters have raw product 38−2a=D/2 since a²+4a=37. Any signed root gives raw product at most a+1=√(2D)/2. For any even signed octad not containing i, dot≤8. For an octad containing i, the donor restriction has odd parity: if the hot sign agrees, at least one light sign must disagree, so dot≤a+5; if the hot sign disagrees, dot≤7−a. Both 8 and a+5 are ≤√(8D)/2=2(a+1) since a>3. Any remaining dense sign has Hamming weight d≥11 relative to all-plus. Flipping the hot coordinate maximizes raw product to 39−2(d−1)+a ≤19+a. Then 10D−(a+19)^2=362−74a>0, hence normalized dot<1/2. This covers all new/new, new/old and inherited old/old pairs without numerics, missing sources, or an assumed binary code with unknown central distance.

Total 589,823 + 40 + 546,176 + 3,120 = **1,139,159**. A generic all-plus split in n dimensions has the same sharp root/daughter balance with a=√(n+1)−2; actual use requires an independently checked retained dense-distance and a compatible signed-support parity. For multiple parents at central QR distance ≥12, distinct hot coordinates are automatically mutually legal, but on the same hot coordinate agreeing parents at distance 12 are not; all octad parity changes must be paid or coherently shared. No multi-parent gain is claimed here.
