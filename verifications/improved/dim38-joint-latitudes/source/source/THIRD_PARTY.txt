SOURCE DATA AND ATTRIBUTION

equator_lines_6855.txt:
Qihong Lin, New lower bounds for kissing numbers in dimensions 33-39,
22 October 2026. CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
https://github.com/QH-Lin/kissing-numbers-33-39
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23554808
The file is copied unchanged; its SHA-256 is pinned in certificate.json.

golay_basis.txt, dim14_directions.txt, triples.txt, class_labels.txt:
Alexey Kravatskiy's 38D cap construction, from the shared workspace's
research/dim38-leech/data. The base construction is also distributed at
https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers
and is credited by Lin's certificate and by Yang et al.,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35051 . The four files are copied unchanged and
their hashes are pinned. The 14D direction configuration is the existing
1932-point configuration; it is not a new 14D bound claimed here.

The idea of adding norm-48 Leech vectors to the empty equator is due to
Yang et al. Lin supplied the 6855-line equator used here. The new work
changes the axis selection, adds 28 coordinate-axis points, and adds
5408 points at a different latitude, for a net gain of 5072 points.
