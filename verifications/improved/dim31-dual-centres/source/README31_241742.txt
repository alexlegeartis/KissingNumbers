The count
---------

Insert 86,528 midpoint points and 1,792 fibre-centre points, and append

  196560 - 43264 + 86528 + 1792 + 126 = 241742.

Equivalently, there are 338 uniform midpoint blocks, each gaining 128,

Geometric normalization and midpoint blocks
-------------------------------------------
Every vector has squared norm 32; every distinct pair has inner product at
most 16. Divide all coordinates by sqrt(32) for a unit-sphere code.

Choose an octad normal space of dimension 8 and its orthogonal 16-dimensional
residual space. There are 4320 residual fibres of 16 Leech points. Their
owners are r +/- p_i, where ||r||^2=||p_i||^2=16 and the eight p_i are
orthogonal. The residuals form 135 groups of 32. Within a group they form
an orthogonal cross-polytope; between groups their maximum products are 4 or 8.

For two normal axes p,q and orthogonal tail roots a,b of squared norm 8,
delete r +/- p and r +/- q and insert

  (r +/- (p+q)/2, +/- a),
  (r +/- (p-q)/2, +/- b).

The signs in each line are independent. This gains four per fibre, or 128
for all 32 fibres in a uniform block. Each inserted head is the midpoint of
two deleted Leech points, so it clears every retained Leech point by convexity.
All interactions between inserted midpoint points are checked explicitly.

The new ingredient: E7-dual centre directions
---------------------------------------------
Write E7 roots e with squared norm 8 and E7-dual line representatives d with
squared norm 6, in the same Euclidean space. They satisfy

  <d,e> in {-4,0,4},
  |<d,d'>| = 2 for distinct dual lines.

For a group g, choose a dual line d orthogonal to every midpoint tail used
in that group. At every residual r in the group add

  (r, +/- sqrt(8/3) d).

There are 64 such points per group. Their squared head and tail norms are
both 16. Use at most one dual line per group and at most one group per dual
line; the selection is a bipartite matching.

These centres clear the entire Leech shell. An explicit witness gives
r=(u+v)/2 for orthogonal Leech vectors u,v. The witnesses themselves have
product 16 with r; all other Leech points clear by convexity. The witnesses
need not be deleted.

For a midpoint in the same group the tail product is zero and the residual
product is at most 16. For a midpoint in another group the product is at most

  8 + sqrt(8/3)*4 = 8 + 8*sqrt(6)/3 < 16.

For centres with different matched groups and lines it is at most
8 + (8/3)*2 = 40/3 < 16. Within one centre block it is at most 16.

All 126 pure E7 points (0,2e) remain. They clear midpoint points by the
tail-norm Cauchy bound sqrt(32*8)=16. Their maximum product with a centre is
2*sqrt(8/3)*4 = 16*sqrt(6)/3 < 16.

Independent verification
------------------------
The standalone verifier uses Python and NumPy, without search code or
geometry caches. It regenerates the Leech shell from the binary Golay code
and checks shell membership, deleted-point uniqueness, endpoint witnesses,
inserted-point uniqueness, and all norms. It directly checks every pair of
inserted midpoint points, every midpoint-centre pair, every centre-centre
pair, and every interaction with the explicit pure tail set. Bulk clearance
uses the standard Leech-shell bound and the exact convexity arguments above.

All dot products computed by BLAS are small integers with bounded absolute
partial sums, so float64 arithmetic is exact. No tolerance is used. Radical
comparisons A+sqrt(8/3)*B <= 16 use signed integer squaring. The receipt says
PASS EXACT.

Research outcome
----------------
The successful change was choosing the correct centre directions and
optimizing their assignments together with midpoint replacements. Fixed
root-centre directions had many cross-group conflicts; E7-dual directions
clear all other groups automatically.

The proposed edge-trisection pool was also completed as a finite test.
An exact matching maps all 2290 candidate insertions to distinct conflicting
old caps, proving net gain at most zero for every subset of that pool, even
with shared deletions. That scoped negative result is recorded separately
in shared_triangle_matching.json; it is not a claim about all trisection
heads or all tail choices. No unsuccessful model is used to justify this
new lower bound: the explicit certificate is the evidence.
