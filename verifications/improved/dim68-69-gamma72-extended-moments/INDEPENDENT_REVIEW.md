# Independent review of the extended-normal bounds

The exact checks pass for **471,886,208 points in dimension 68** and
**746,325,051 points in dimension 69**, subject to the stated published
Gamma72 premises. No mathematical flaw was found in the nested averaging,
profile domain, moment normalization, weak-duality inequality, or head geometry.

`verify_independent.py` is a separate implementation. It imports neither the
producer, `exact_orbits.py`, nor `verify.py`. It reads only the package-local
catalogue Gram matrix, integral normal witness, and the two certificates.
Its complete receipt is `independent_verification.json`. The default replay
is read-only; an optional `--report` argument writes a fresh receipt.

## Complete finite domain

For orthonormal coordinates p in the seven-dimensional normal span, put
h = 4p. Integrality against every embedded root 2(e_i ± e_j) forces h to be
integral with a common coordinate parity. For a minimal vector other than
one of these roots, the lattice minimum gives
|h_i| + |h_j| ≤ 8 for every distinct i,j. Projection also gives |h|² ≤ 128.

The independent checker generates coordinates recursively under these exact
conditions. It finds **162,969 profiles**, partitioned into **60** signed
coordinate-permutation orbits. A separate count is 88,345 even profiles and
74,624 odd profiles. For rank seven the squared-norm bound does not remove
any additional profile, since the largest possible norm under the first two
conditions is 112. These statements cover the possible projection domain;
they do not enumerate Gamma72's ambient minimal shell.

The checker computes the Gram matrix of the actual seven integral lattice
vectors, proves the reference D7 basis has determinant of absolute value two,
and expresses all **84** normal roots as integer combinations of this basis.
Each has squared norm eight. These known roots are removed from the moment
right-hand sides and separately evaluated in the objective. Their contribution
to each chosen slice family is exactly zero.

## Why the two averages describe actual configurations

Only **four** normal directions are removed from a lattice vector. The other
three D7 coordinates remain part of its 68-dimensional body.

First choose a signed permutation of the seven-coordinate frame. Within its
first four coordinates, average the selected slices over the D4 root-system
symmetries generated by signed permutations and the half-Hadamard map. Each
such choice is an actual integral D4 normal tuple in the embedded lattice.
The D4 maps need not extend to D7 symmetries. Neither averaging step assumes
that these normal-coordinate symmetries preserve the ambient Gamma72 shell.

The independent checker constructs the D4 action by graph traversal of explicit
generators. It finds **1,681** profiles in **16** orientation orbits. On each
orbit, the average selection weight is the number of selected profiles divided
by the orbit population. It then sums these weights over every individual D7
profile. This reproduces the compact objective coefficients exactly.

The double average is an average of the cardinalities of actual configurations.
Consequently at least one configuration has at least the ceiling of its exact
mean lower bound. The additional normal coordinates strengthen the counting
constraints without changing the dimension removed by the geometric construction.

## Moments and exact duality

The h coordinates scale a radius-sqrt(8) spherical shell by four. Its moment
scale is therefore 128 to the relevant power. For total even degree 2s,
the denominator is 72·74·…·(72+2s−2), with the standard product of odd double
factorials in the numerator. All moments used have degree at most ten, within
the stated spherical 11-design premise.

For each certificate, the independent checker directly sums all **18** moment
rows over all 162,969 individual profiles. It independently regenerates their
right-hand sides, subtracts the 84 known roots, and checks every rational dual
slack. All slacks are nonnegative. The resulting exact mean bounds are

- 68D: **471,886,208**;
- 69D before poles: **6,716,925,440 / 9**.

Taking the ceiling in 69D gives 746,325,049 points. Adding the two checked poles
gives **746,325,051**. No floating-point solver output enters these conclusions.

## Geometry and distinctness

The independent checker verifies all **21** pair types in 68D and **55** pair
types in 69D using rational arithmetic. Before squaring an inequality, it
checks both sides have the required nonnegative signs. Every resulting point
has squared norm eight, and distinct points have product at most four.
The minimum strictly positive squared gaps are 176/403 and 1/180, respectively.

This pair bound also rules out projection or rescaling collisions: coincident
norm-eight images would have product eight. Every selected slice has a nonzero
body component. The two pure-head points are therefore distinct from all slice
images, and their products are valid because each chosen height has square at
most two.

## Inherited premises and scope

The proof retains the published identification of the pinned catalogue lattice
as Gamma72, its integrality and minimum squared norm eight, its 6,218,175,600
minimal vectors, and the spherical 11-design property of that shell. The exact
embedding, profile enumeration, averaging weights, moment equations, dual
inequality, and head geometry are checked here. Ambient shell enumeration and
a new proof of those published lattice theorems are outside this certificate.
