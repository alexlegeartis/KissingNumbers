A MODULAR-FORM IDENTITY FOR THE 40-DIMENSIONAL BODY POPULATIONS
Research derivation — 22 October 2026

This note concerns the pinned even rank-40 complement K of sqrt(3) E8
inside the extremal unimodular lattice P48n. Its discriminant group is
D = K*/K, an eight-dimensional nondegenerate quadratic space over F3.
Write q_D(c) = ||x||^2/2 mod 1 for x in class c, and

  theta_c(tau) = sum_{x in K+c} q^(||x||^2/2),  q = exp(2 pi i tau).

The theta vector Theta=(theta_c) has weight 20 for the finite-image Weil
representation of D (using the theta-series convention for that representation).

LEMMA

For any isometry g of the discriminant quadratic form,

  theta_c - theta_{g(c)} = (t_c - t_{g(c)}) eta(tau)^40,

where t_c counts the vectors of squared norm 10/3 in class c.
The statement does NOT assume that g lifts to an automorphism of K or P48n.

PROOF

1. Every nonzero vector of K* has squared norm at least 10/3.
   In every discriminant class the normal lattice has a gluing representative
   of squared norm at most 8/3. The exact E8 enumeration in geometry.py
   supplies these representatives. A shorter nonzero body, glued to its
   corresponding normal representative, would contradict the parent minimum 6.

2. An isometry of D permutes components and commutes with the Weil action:
   the matrices of T depend only on q_D, and those of S only on its associated
   bilinear form. Consequently F=Theta-gTheta is again a weight-20 vector
   modular form. Its constant term cancels, since g fixes the zero class.
   Step 1 gives F=O(q^(5/3)) at infinity.

3. eta^40 is holomorphic and nonvanishing on the upper half-plane. It has
   weight 20, a finite-order character, and expansion

     eta^40 = q^(5/3) (1 - 40 q + O(q^2)).

   Thus F/eta^40 is holomorphic there and bounded at infinity. Its weight-zero
   transformation law has finite image: both the Weil representation and
   the eta character have finite image. Passing to their common finite-index
   kernel makes every component an ordinary weight-zero modular function.
   The transformation law and boundedness at infinity imply boundedness at
   every cusp of this subgroup. Each component therefore extends to a
   holomorphic function on the compactified modular curve and is constant.
   Its constant is the coefficient of q^(5/3) in F, namely t_c-t_{g(c)}.
   This proves the identity.

CONSEQUENCES

The orthogonal group of a nondegenerate quadratic space over F3 is transitive
on the nonzero vectors of each fixed quadratic value (Witt's extension
lemma). The zero class is its own orbit. Comparing coefficients with the
already counted representative classes gives:

 body squared norm     class condition              exact population
 10/3                  q_D(c)=2/3                    t_c
 16/3                  q_D(c)=2/3                    116235 - 40 t_c
 14/3                  q_D(c)=1/3                    9180
 4                     nonzero q_D(c)=0              495
 6                     nonzero q_D(c)=0              1084590

For the 16/3 reference, a class with shortest normal squared norm 2/3 has
no body below 16/3, hence t_c=0. Its population 116235 is established by the
exact parent-shell moment identities. The 14/3 and 4 reference populations
are established by the same identities. The radius-six reference 1084590
is independently enumerated in the supplied standalone verifier.
The zero-class norm-six population is separately 1092000.

The formula at 16/3 explains why using 116235 for every affine outer core
family was incorrect. The correction depends on the actual short-body count.
All 73 independently enumerated research core classes agree with the formula.
This agreement is a check, not the proof of the lemma.

COMPLETENESS OF THE t_c TABLE

The explicit pool consists of 30240 distinct verified norm-10/3 bodies.
They occupy 1920 discriminant classes; each such class has nine shortest
normal representatives of squared norm 8/3. Other minimal-vector fibres are
counted by the exact moment identities. Together, the verified vectors give

  1092000 + 240*116235 + 2160*9180 + 6720*495
          + 9*30240 + 240 = 52416000.

The final 240 are the pure normal roots of sqrt(3) E8. These disjoint fibres
already exhaust the published size of the parent's minimal shell. An omitted
norm-10/3 body would glue to further minimal vectors and contradict this total.
Hence the explicit pool is complete, and population_table.npz gives the full
coefficient table, not merely a lower bound.

USE AND LIMITS

This identity makes arbitrary discriminant relabellings inexpensive to score.
It does not preserve the available short normal representatives or certify
angles automatically. Those conditions still have to be checked separately.
The new 41D/42D certificate does not depend on this analytical shortcut: its
outer classes have the directly proved uniform 116235 population, and its
central class is re-enumerated exactly.
