# Antipodal ternary-simplex construction in dimensions 88–91

Status: exact theorem-based lower bounds beating the supplied comparison targets. Global novelty has not been audited. The verification script does not independently enumerate minimum distances.

## Input theorem and exact representation

Gabriele Nebe, *On extremal self-dual ternary codes of length 48*, introduction and Lemma 3.5, https://arxiv.org/html/1109.1681 (published DOI 10.1155/2012/154281), establishes the known extended quadratic-residue ternary code Q48, a self-dual [48,24,15] code. Extremal means minimum distance 3 floor(48/12)+3 = 15.

Lemma 3.5 describes it as follows. Over F3, factor x^47-1=(x-1)g h into two degree-23 factors. Form the length-47 cyclic code generated by (x-1)g, append coordinate zero, and adjoin the all-ones vector of length 48. The two choices g,h give equivalent codes. `verify.py` explicitly lists both factors and constructs 23 shifted polynomial rows plus the all-ones row. It verifies the factorization, rank 24, and self-orthogonality, hence self-duality. The minimum-distance assertion relies on the cited established theorem, not merely on these finite checks.

Shortening on the first s coordinates means retaining words zero there, then removing those coordinates. For s=3 and s=4, the script constructs generators and checks exact ranks 21 and 20. Minimum distance remains at least 15. Independently, exact dimension follows from the dual-distance-15 property: projection on any fewer than 15 coordinates is surjective.

## Geometric lemma

Use triangle symbols t0=(1,0), t1=(-1/2,sqrt(3)/2), t2=(-1/2,-sqrt(3)/2). For a ternary word c of length n, let v(c) be their block concatenation. Then squared norm is n and

v(c).v(d) = n - (3/2) d_H(c,d).

For distinct words of minimum distance at least 15 and n<=45, this is at most n/2. For every two words, including identical words, it is at least -n/2, because d_H<=n. Thus {v(c),-v(c):c in C} is a kissing configuration after normalization by sqrt(n). No triangle symbol is the negative of another triangle symbol, so the two copies are disjoint and cardinality is exactly 2|C|.

## Dimension 89 via an origin hyperplane

Start with the length-45 configuration in R90 and require that the first Cartesian coordinate of triangle block 1 equal the first Cartesian coordinate of triangle block 2. This is one homogeneous real linear equation, so all surviving points lie in an 89-dimensional subspace, with unchanged norms and pairwise inner products. Global antipodes satisfy the same equation.

The first coordinate of t0 is 1, and those of t1,t2 are -1/2. Thus qualifying symbol pairs are (0,0), (1,1), (1,2), (2,1), (2,2), exactly five of nine possibilities. The script verifies rank 2 for projection of the length-45 shortened code onto its first two symbol positions; each pair therefore has exactly 3^19 preimages. Including antipodes gives 10*3^19 = 11,622,614,670, exceeding the supplied dimension-89 target 9,270,596,604 by 2,352,018,066.

An explicit orthonormal identification of this hyperplane with R89 combines the two equal x-coordinates by their sum divided by sqrt(2), retains both y-coordinates, and retains all remaining coordinates. This preserves all inner products exactly.

## Weight enumerator and a rejected different 89D construction

The script computes the ternary Gleason weight enumerator conditional on the established Q48 minimum-distance theorem. With t tracking weight/3, F=1+8t and H=t(1-t)^3,

W=F^12-96F^9 H+2400F^6 H^2-12704F^3 H^3+96H^4.

In particular A45=6,503,296. This is NOT an independent proof of minimum distance.

The alternative of restricting one symbol of length-45 code to two values, projecting away the constant triangle midpoint, and adjoining antipodes creates conflicts between words whose difference has full support. If such differences exist, the conflict graph on the candidate antipodal completion is a union of two positive-degree regular bipartite graphs, between opposite symbol/sign classes. Each has a perfect matching, so deletion alone loses at least half the candidates. This obstruction does not apply to the origin-hyperplane construction above, which never changes pairwise geometry.
