# 37D: 533,864; 38D: 643,836 by compressing QR coset codes

This replaces the entire preceding constructions in both dimensions.
The gains are **27,155** over the 506,709-point 37D certificate and
**32,038** over the 611,798-point 38D certificate. The previous
[Coxeter–Todd](../dim36-37-ct-cell-rebuild/) and
[Leech/Hamming](../dim38-triangle-hamming/) packages are unchanged.
No upper bound or optimality claim is made.

## Dense coordinate compression

In both dimensions begin with a binary coset-union code from
[Yang et al., Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35051v1).
Its minimum Hamming distance is 10. Interpret the bits as signs.
Replace its first two binary coordinates by one integer coordinate;
all other signs are scaled by two. The four source patterns in order
`(0,0), (1,0), (0,1), (1,1)` map as follows:

- **37D**, length-38 parent: `4, 1, -4, -1`. All 180,224 words survive.
  The squared norms are 160 and 145; the raw product bounds, in order
  (small, small), (small, large), (large, large), are 71, 76, and 80.
  Removing the old pair removes its contribution
  `4 (2 - 2 h)`, where h is the marked Hamming distance. Replacing
  it by the product of the new scalar labels gives the above ceilings.
- **38D**, length-39 parent: `3, discard, 0, -3`. Keep both equal
  sign patterns and one fixed orientation of the mixed sign patterns,
  three quarters of the 327,680 words. The squared norms are 157 and
  148; the raw product bounds are 77 between equal-pattern words,
  76 across types, and 68 between fixed-opposite-pattern words.
  Only one parent pattern is mapped to zero, so the map is injective.

In both rows the angle inequalities use integer arithmetic:
a positive raw product d is legal iff `4*d*d <= n1*n2`, for
squared norms n1 and n2. The unchanged signs, or equivalently all
possible differences of two affine offsets modulo the kernel, give
a second independent way to check the ceilings.

## Middle and root layers

On each weight-eight support use all 128 even-parity signings with
entries ±2 and zeros elsewhere. Use all signed D-type roots with two
entries ±4. The middle and root squared norms are 32. Distinct octads
meet in at most four coordinates and each same-support signing differs
in at least two signs, giving all internal sparse bounds.

The 37D middle layer avoids the merged coordinate. Its 2,742 supports
on the other 36 coordinates are generated by eight simultaneous
projective orbits and three appended masks. All 3,088 supports in Lin's
38D list can be used, including supports on the merged coordinate.
The corresponding cross-layer products are checked directly by the
integer ceilings in the verifiers. The 37D octad data regenerate
entirely offline. [Source attribution](THIRD_PARTY.md).

## Replay

From this directory:

```sh
python verify.py              # standalone standard library
python audit.py               # independent kernel-difference and support proof (NumPy)
python audit.py --full-dense  # independently enumerate all explicit dense-dense pairs
```

The complete proof is by reduction to checked product classes and
counts. The optional expansion is exhaustive over dense-dense pairs;
it does not rerun every mixed or sparse pair explicitly.
