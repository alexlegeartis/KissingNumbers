# Mixed triality six-row append in dimension 96

**209,290,447,680 kissing points**, **3,366,912** more than the previous **209,287,080,768**. This preserves every point of the [published complementary parity packing](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/main/verifications/improved/dim94-96-hexary-parity-packing).

Use the 24 ordinary four-coordinate rows, in physical order. Every line of witness.txt gives three disjoint 24-bit masks for axial, even-parity-flat and odd-parity-flat rows. Exactly six rows are occupied. The four projective axes of the axial row are the coordinate unit axes scaled by 2; the other two frames each consist of cube vertices with a fixed parity, modulo sign. Each row has squared radius 4. Use the explicit 128-word length-six quaternary axis code with Hamming distance at least three and every even six-bit sign word. Thus each of 822 masks gives 4,096 directions. There are 262 patterns with two axial rows and 560 with three.

On a fixed pattern, two distinct axis words agree in at most three rows, and different sign assignments on the same axis word reverse at least two rows. Against any different outer pattern, each coincident frame contributes at most four, each differing frame at most two; the checker tests the resulting normalized comparison on all outer pairs. The cap is raw 12 on squared-radius-24 vectors. Mixed pairs with every prior flat BCH/paired-BCH/hexary support are checked separately, with both old parity modes as required. Older flat RS points choose one row from each of the six physical groups of four consecutive rows, so their ordinary mixed bound follows from the per-group maximum.

The [inherited amplitude-four RS and grouped roots](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/main/verifications/improved/dim93-96-e7-rs-root-augmentation) have squared radius 96. In 96D the physical groups are six consecutive sets of 16 ordinary coordinates. The producer's verify_augmentation.py explicitly constructs each old root support as a pair within one such group, with no exceptional coordinate in dimension 96. Each new pattern uses at most one axial row per physical group. Therefore two old root magnitudes √48 see at most a sum of new amplitudes 3, and the raw dot is at most 3√48 < 24. The older amplitude-four RS points select at most one coordinate per group. Its maximum product is bounded groupwise by 8 if the new pattern has an axial row there, 4 if it has only flat rows and zero otherwise. The sum is checked to be at most 24. Any old dense or shifted-dodecacode row contributes at most four, giving raw mixed dot at most 24. Norms and row shapes separate the new and old layers.

Run python3 verify.py, with standard-library Python and assertions enabled. This checker uses the frozen 822-pattern witness; neither the search program nor optimization results are premises of the proof.
