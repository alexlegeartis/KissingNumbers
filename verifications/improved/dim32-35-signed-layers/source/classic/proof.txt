# Construction and exact verification

Write `pc(m)` for the number of set bits of a nonnegative integer. Bit `j`
denotes coordinate `j`, counted from zero. Normalize each vector by its own
Euclidean length. All base vectors have squared length 32.

## Base layers

The 17 generator masks in each certificate generate a binary linear code
`C` in 32 coordinates. Enumeration verifies `|C| = 131072` and minimum
nonzero weight 8. For a specified shift `r` (zero when omitted), include

`((-1)^(c_0+r_0), ..., (-1)^(c_31+r_31), 0, ..., 0)`, for `c in C`.

Distinct dense vectors have inner product at most `32 - 2*8 = 16`.
Translation by `r` preserves all these inner products.

For each mask in `full_even_supports`, include all 128 vectors with entries
`+2` or `-2` on its eight coordinates, zero elsewhere, and an even number
of minus signs. The verifier checks that every two supports meet in at most
four coordinates. On one support, distinct patterns differ in at least two
signs. Thus all distinct inner products within these full layers are at
most 16.

For an exceptional pair `(m,s)`, put `-2` on the coordinates in `s`, `+2` on
the remaining coordinates of `m`, and zero elsewhere; include its negative
as well. The least coordinate in `m` has positive sign, fixing one
representative of each antipodal line. For two representatives the inner
product is

`4 * (pc(m & k) - 2*pc((s ^ t) & m & k))`.

The absolute value is checked to be at most 16. Exceptional supports are
disjoint from the full-support list and meet each full support in at most
four coordinates.

Finally include the `2*n*(n-1)` roots `4*(±e_i ±e_j)`, `i<j`, subject to the
two specified deletions below. Distinct root inner products are at most 16.
The other base-layer bounds are immediate from the coordinate shapes:
dense/middle at most `8*2 = 16`, dense/root at most `2*4 = 8`, and
middle/root at most `2*2*4 = 16`. Different layers have different support
sizes, so their vectors cannot coincide.

The counts for the signed-replacement certificates are

| Dimension | Dense | Full middle | Exceptional middle | Roots | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 131072 | 1704*128 = 218112 | 816*2 = 1632 | 1984 | **352800** |
| 33 | 131072 | 1848*128 = 236544 | 320*2 = 640 | 2112 | **370368** |

## Four-head replacement in dimensions 34 and 35

Here all the middle supports remain the published ones. Use the dense-code
shift `r = 1605954 = 0x188142`. Exhaustive enumeration verifies that every
word of `C+r` has weight between 6 and 26. Consequently its sign vector
has coordinate sum between -20 and 20.

Let `a=n-2`, `b=n-1`; both lie outside the first 32 coordinates. Delete the
two roots `±4*(e_a-e_b)`. For each independent choice `epsilon,delta in
{-1,+1}`, add the unit vector `q/sqrt(n+16)`, where

`q_j = epsilon` for `j` outside `{a,b}`, and `q_a=3*delta`, `q_b=-3*delta`.

There are four distinct heads, each with `q.q = (n-2)+9+9 = n+16`.
They have full support, so cannot coincide with a base vector. Their largest
distinct mutual integer inner product is `n-20`, which is at most `(n+16)/2`
for both dimensions.

Their integer inner product with a dense vector is at most 20, by the
coset weight check. With a retained root it is at most 16. The only roots
attaining 24 are exactly the deleted antipodal pair.

Consider a full even-sign octad. If it contains no heavy coordinate, its
inner product with a head is at most 16. If it contains one, the bound is
`2*(7+3)=20`. If it contains both, the head's signs on the octad have odd
parity: there are six equal small signs and two opposite heavy signs.
An even-sign octad cannot match all eight signs. Its maximum dot product
is therefore `2*(6+3+3-2)=20`, since the cheapest sign disagreement costs 2
before the middle-layer scaling by 2.

For a base vector `v` of squared norm 32, the normalized condition is

`(q.v)/(sqrt(n+16)*sqrt(32)) <= 1/2`.

For a nonnegative integer dot product this is equivalent to
`4*(q.v)^2 <= 32*(n+16)`. The bound 20 suffices because
`4*20^2 = 1600 <= 32*50` in 34D and `1600 < 32*51` in 35D. Negative dot
products automatically satisfy the kissing condition. This checks all
mixed norms without rounding a square root.

We deleted two points and added four, giving

| Dimension | Dense | Middle | Retained roots | Heads | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 34 | 131072 | 2013*128 = 257664 | 2242 | 4 | **390982** |
| 35 | 131072 | 2202*128 = 281856 | 2378 | 4 | **415310** |

The two verification programs check the same complete constructions through
different representations: masks and sign formulas in `verify.py`, explicit
integer coordinates and matrix products in `verify_coordinates.py`.
