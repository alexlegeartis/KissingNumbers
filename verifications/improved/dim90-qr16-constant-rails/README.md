## Physical coordinates and inherited stock

Let t_j=(cos(2πj/3),sin(2πj/3)) for j∈F3 on each of 45 independent planes, and h_j=t_j−t_(j−1). Each h_j has norm squared 3, with h_i·h_j=−3/2 for i≠j. The mapping for a trit u_i and a phase label p_i is exactly 0 if u_i=0, +h_(p_i) if u_i=1, −h_(p_i) if u_i=2. Thus *global Euclidean negation* sends u to −u and **does not change the phase label p_i**. In particular the two donor marks are (0,+,−) and (0,−,+), with the identical constant rail j on both antipodal points. The negative old bank is the global Euclidean negative of the positive bank with phase code K+shift, not a ternary negation of those phase labels.

Use the canonical extended ternary quadratic-residue code QR48 over F3 (minimum Hamming distance 15; see [Nebe's QR/Pless source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.1681)). In the fixed code normalization, its all-ones vector belongs to Q and Q is self-dual. Omit the first three coordinates from the physical code. The dense stock contains both global signs of the 3²¹ images of the zero-shortening Q° through t_j. The 130,944 old sparse outer words are all weight-15 elements of Q°, as independently counted by the exact two-half QR enumeration. For a given old word, add an arbitrary ternary translation to the same previously verified 7,906-word Hamming-5 phase code K on its increasing 15 occupied coordinates. Distinct old outer words have squared distance ≥3×15 irrespective of any phase choice. Within one old outer word h_i−h_j has squared norm 9 for differing labels; translation preserves phase Hamming distance. Thus all old sparse points remain compatible. Their raw norm squares stay 45. Their dense-interface dot is at most 15×3/2=45/2, and the small-shell projection bounds in the pinned baseline are pointwise bounds on every signed root, independent of all rephasings. The existing 4-, 5-, 3- and 2-plane/root small-stock gates are unchanged; only the equal-size phase arrangements change.

## The added rays

Read the literal parent donor word from the witness. It has weight 18, prefix (0,1,2), and is in Q. Use its last 45 trits of weight 16, mapped to h_j or −h_j with the one constant rail j specified for that word, plus its Euclidean negative. Distinct parent donors in the given mark class erase injectively. Opposite mark classes cannot give the same shortened outer word, as then their full QR Hamming distance would be 2. All donors have raw norm squared 48.

For either sign of the dense triangle embedding, the per-plane maximizing trit of a donor differs from the rail on exactly one signed support, by −1 on that support. In the full QR weight-18 word the counts of +1 and −1 are both multiples of 3, by orthogonality to all ones and weight divisible by 3. The two marks of opposite signs remove one of each. Therefore on the shortened body the two maximizing trit patterns have nonzero syndromes against the donor (for a constant phase its contribution is 0, and the corrected signed support has cardinality 2 mod 3). Since the donor restriction annihilates the dense QR shortening, at least one prescribed maximizing trit cannot be met. Thus the maximum signed dot is ≤15×3/2=22.5, less than √(45·48)/2. The small shells see at most raw products 4√3, 7.5, 3√3, and 2√3 for square, rotated five-plane, three-plane, and D-root. These all pass the exact mixed norm thresholds (the square bound is equality).

For two different donor outer words, deleting the marks leaves ordinary distance d≥15 within one mark class and d≥13 between opposite classes. In the former case d≡0 mod 3, in the latter d≡1 mod 3. The ambient squared distance is at least 3d. At d≥16 that suffices. At d=13 or 15 the signed opposite overlap is nonempty (odd distance, with equal 16-point weights), and the same-signed overlap is also nonempty since d<16. Thus if the two constant rails coincide an opposite-signed overlap adds 9 beyond the baseline 3d; if the rails differ, a same-signed overlap adds 9. In either case squared separation ≥48. This accounts for both signs of every new point.

## Old/new exact replay

Only a full QR Hamming distance 15 can potentially fail; otherwise shortened distance ≥16 suffices. At distance 15 the shortened distance is 13. For an old word and either donor sign, let C be same-signed and O opposite-signed positions in the old support. A default 3d=39 becomes at least 48 if an old phase differs from j at any C position, or equals j at any O position. This follows from the explicit root map: equal signs give local squared distance 0/9 for phase equal/different; opposite signs give 12/3 for phase equal/different. Hence the forbidden case is exactly that the old j-symbol support contains C and avoids O. Phase label j is unchanged when the verifier tries neg(u), precisely because the coordinate map is σh_j, not a map that rotates on sign change. Checking both donor signs for each positive old outer word simultaneously proves all old-negative cross checks by global negation.

The standalone replay stores full literal donors and old shift indices. It also checks the canonical QR polynomial factorization into the two degree-23 factors and the conversion to the literal systematic basis; the standard minimum-15 theorem is the only inherited coding-distance premise. It independently enumerates all 415,104 old QR minimum words (shortened population 130,944), verifies all 31,248,465 pairs of the fixed phase code, and uses unweighted exact signed Hamming distances against both signs of each donor. At a short contact, it scans every symbol support C⊆T⊆complement(O) for the selected shifted old K. All new/new and dense/small inequalities are checked with integer or exact symbolic comparisons. It does not call a solver, the original seed hash, or the optimization code.

Run with Python ≥3.10 and a C++20 compiler: `python3 -B verify.py`. The independent replay prints `AUDIT PASS donor_pairs=2189 old_shift_pairs=56678 near_pairs=191522 exact_total=21956424500`.
