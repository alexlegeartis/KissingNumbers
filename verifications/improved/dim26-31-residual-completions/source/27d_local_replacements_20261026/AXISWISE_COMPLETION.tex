\section*{Axiswise completion when every normal frame is occupied}
The free-head completion does not require a common unused frame.
For each tail coordinate $a$, choose a frame $f_a$ such that every selected
group on $f_a$ has its $a$th tail coordinate zero.  Let $R_{f_a}$ be the
32 residual vectors, embedded in the complementary16 coordinates.  They
have squared norm16 and distinct residual inner products at most0.
Adjoin the16 signed normal coordinate vectors $\pm4e_i$ on the octad.
These48 heads are pairwise nonpositive and each has squared norm16.
For this coordinate insert $(h,\pm4e_a)$ for all48 heads.

Points associated with the same signed tail axis have inner product
at most16 by the nonpositive head bound.  Opposite signs on that axis
only reduce inner products.  Points on different tail axes have tail
inner product zero, and their head inner product is at most16 by
Cauchy--Schwarz, even if their residual frames differ.

For an inserted group on a frame different from $f_a$, the residual
inner product is at most8 and the tail inner product at most8.  On
$f_a$ itself, the residual inner product is at most16 and the chosen
tail coordinate is zero.  Normal coordinate heads use the existing
absolute-coordinate bound2.  Thus all these new points clear every
inserted group.  For the full-shell check, write each residual head as $h=(x+y)/2$
with orthogonal shell owners $x=h+a$, $y=h-a$, where $h\perp a$
and $\|h\|^2=\|a\|^2=16$.  Its dot product with either owner
is exactly16; with every other shell point it is an average of two
inner products at most16.  A normal coordinate head $4e_i$ similarly
is the midpoint of the shell points $4e_i+4e_j$ and $4e_i-4e_j$.
Thus every free head clears the entire shell, including its own owners.  Scaled pure $D_m$ tail roots have squared norm32 and
coordinate absolute values4, and clear these points and all groups.
There are therefore $96m$ completion points and $2m(m-1)$ pure tails
whenever a qualifying frame exists separately for each of the $m$ axes.

The29-dimensional160-group candidate uses all135 normal frames, but
qualifying frames for the five axes are respectively $(2,1,2,1,0)$.
Its expanded certificate has20480 distinct deletions,40960 distinct
insertions,480 free-head points and40 pure-tail points.  Its candidate
cardinality is
\[
196560-20480+40960+480+40=217560.
\]
Certification status is supplied separately by the independent complete
coordinate replay.  The frozen hashes are recorded in
