\section*{Larger pure-tail sets in dimensions30 and31}
These replacements do not change any Leech-shell deletion, inserted
midpoint group, or free head. Every inserted group's tail has squared
norm8, so its inner product with any pure tail of squared norm32 is at
most16 by Cauchy--Schwarz. Every free point has tail $\pm4e_i$, so a
pure tail with all coordinate absolute values at most4 also clears all
free points. Its inner product with every retained shell point is zero.
Thus it suffices to construct a norm32 kissing configuration inside
$[-4,4]^m$ in the tail space.

\paragraph{Six tails:72 points instead of60.}
Take the40 vectors $\pm4e_i\pm4e_j$ on the first five coordinates,
and the32 vectors
\[
 (2s_1,\ldots,2s_5,2\sqrt3\,s_1s_2s_3s_4s_5),\qquad s_i=\pm1.
\]
All have squared norm32 and coordinate absolute values at most4.
Two spinor-type vectors at Hamming distance$d$ have inner product
$20-8d+12(-1)^d$, at most16 unless identical. Their inner products with
any of the40 ordinary roots are at most16. The ordinary roots also
satisfy the usual norm32 pair bound16. Replacing the60 pure $D_6$ roots
by these72 points therefore gains12.

\paragraph{Seven tails:126 points instead of84.}
Start with the60 vectors $\pm4e_i\pm4e_j$ on the first six coordinates,
the two vectors $\pm4\sqrt2e_7$, and the64 vectors
\[
 (2s_1,\ldots,2s_6,2\sqrt2t),\qquad \prod_{i=1}^6s_i=1,\quad t=\pm1.
\]
Their norms are32. For spinor pairs the first-six Hamming distance$d$
is even and the inner product is $24-8d+8tt'$, at most16 for distinct
points. All other cross-family pair bounds follow directly from the
displayed coordinates. Rotate coordinates1 and7 by
\[
 y_1=(x_1+x_7)/\sqrt2,\qquad y_7=(x_7-x_1)/\sqrt2.
\]
The formerly long axis becomes $(\pm4,\pm4)$ in these two coordinates;
ordinary roots acquire coordinates at most$2\sqrt2$ there; spinor
coordinates become $2t\pm\sqrt2s_1$. Consequently all coordinates
have absolute value at most4. The rotation preserves norms and pair
inner products. Replacing84 pure $D_7$ roots by these126 points gains42.

\paragraph{Exact certificates.}
The frozen files \texttt{pure\_tail30\_exceptional.npz} and
\texttt{pure\_tail31\_exceptional.npz} store coordinates as $A+B\sqrt d$
with integer matrices$A,B$, $d=3,2$ respectively. In both cases the
radical part $AB^T+BA^T$ of the Gram matrix is identically zero, so all
norm and pair checks use the integer matrix $AA^T+dBB^T$.
The coordinate bound is certified by
$dB_{ij}^2\leq(4-|A_{ij}|)^2$ and $|A_{ij}|\leq4$.
Independent audit status and hashes are recorded separately; this note
is the construction argument, not a claim of a universal optimal tail code.
