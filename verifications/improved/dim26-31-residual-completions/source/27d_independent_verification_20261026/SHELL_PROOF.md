# Self-contained shell-pair lemma used by the independent verifier

No external Leech-lattice theorem is required for validity of these coordinates.
Let C be the binary linear code generated by the twelve shifts of polynomial
0xC75, each extended by its parity bit. The verifier enumerates all 4096 words,
checks distinctness and weight distribution {0:1,8:759,12:2576,16:759,24:1},
and checks even intersections of every pair of generators. Linearity therefore
gives minimum nonzero difference weight 8 and even intersection for every pair
of codewords. In particular, two distinct weight-eight supports intersect in
at most four positions.

Construct three types of integer vectors in 24 coordinates:
A: two entries +/-4 (all supports and signs), 1104 vectors.
B: entries +/-2 on a weight-eight codeword, with an even number of minus signs,
   759*128 = 97152 vectors.
C: sign vector s_i=(-1)^{w_i} for a codeword w, with one chosen coordinate j
   multiplied by -3, 4096*24 = 98304 vectors.
All have squared norm32. The verifier also checks their total distinct count
196560 directly.

Every two distinct vectors have inner product at most16:
- A/A: different supports share at most one position; on the same support,
  distinct sign choices have inner product at most0.
- A/B: at most two products, each at most8.
- A/C: the two absolute contributions are at most12 and4.
- B/B: different octads overlap in at most four positions, each contributing
  at most4. On the same octad, different even sign choices disagree in at
  least two positions, giving inner product <=32-8*2=16.
- C/C, same exceptional coordinate j: let h be codeword Hamming distance and
  d indicate disagreement at j. The inner product is32-2h-16d. Distinct vectors
  here require h>=8, so it is <=16.
- C/C, different exceptional positions j,k: put a=s_j t_j,b=s_k t_k. Inner
  product is24-2h-4(a+b). For distinct codewords h>=8, this is <=32-2h<=16.
  For identical codewords h=0 and a=b=1, it equals16.
- B/C: if exceptional position j is outside the octad, the bound16 is immediate.
  If j belongs to it, let h be the number of disagreements between B's signs
  and the underlying unmodified codeword sign vector on that octad, and let
  a be their sign product at j. Inner product is16-4h-8a. The even B sign parity
  and even codeword/octad intersection imply h is even. If a=1 the expression
  is <=8. If a=-1 then h>=2, so it is <=16.

## Old midpoint insertions versus retained shell
The independent verifier checks every removed vector is a distinct member of
the generated shell. For each old inserted27D point, it checks exactly that
its first24 coordinates are half the sum of the two indexed removed vectors.
A retained shell point is different from both removed owners, hence its dot
product with either owner is <=16. Its dot product with their midpoint is
therefore <=16. Its tail coordinates are zero, so adding the insertion's tail
does not change this inequality.

Old/old, old/pure, pure/pure, new/old, new/new, and new/pure pair bounds are
checked directly by streamed integer dot products. All192 new heads are also
checked directly against the ENTIRE196560-point shell, stronger than checking
only retained points. Distinctness and norms are checked independently.

Integer arithmetic uses int32, with every coordinate in [-4,4] and at most27
products per sum, so all partial sums are bounded by432 in absolute value.
No overflow assumption relies merely on a final inner product bound.
