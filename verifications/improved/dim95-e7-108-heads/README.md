# A 108-head Gram-dominated E7 configuration in dimension 95

**117,083,737,156 kissing points**, up **6,442,450,944** (about **5.82%**) from 110,641,286,212, the count after the preceding outer and flat Reed–Solomon extensions. This is a lower bound from explicit fibers, not an exact kissing number or an exhaustive worldwide priority claim.

Use the [pinned Z8 parent and 102-head E7 architecture](../dim94-95-e6-e7-head-exchange/), replacing the entire 102-fiber dense layer with 108 fibers. The first two four-coordinate blocks are deleted. A chosen prefix fixes these two Z8 symbols and leaves exactly 8^10 messages, with a body in the remaining 22 four-coordinate blocks. Each of the three body colors is one of the inherited crosspolytope alphabets (two reflected Walsh alphabets and the coordinate-axis alphabet). The rank, surjectivity of prefixes and homogeneous minimum 24 are from the pinned parent proof; the checker verifies the exact source SHA and triangular rank structure.

The witness `heads.json` gives a color, two-symbol prefix and integer head vector for each fiber. Each integer head has squared norm 8 and lies in x0+x4=0 in eight coordinates; after compression the seven orthonormal coordinates are (sqrt(2)*x0,x1,x2,x3,x5,x6,x7). All head and body squared radii are unchanged, with total 96. For any two distinct fibers of the same color, the new head dot is bounded by the dot of the two deleted body blocks, which preserves the parent cap 48. This also holds for distinct words in the same fiber, because their original and new head square norms are equal. For different colors the body cap is 22·2=44, and the new heads have dot at most 4. All 5,778 cross-fiber pairs are checked exactly in integer coordinates. Every pair has cap far below 96, so both normalized vector and fiber counts are injective.

The unchanged augmentation details are:
- The 1,838,148 retained original RS and root points. The amplitude-4 RS layer excludes the exceptional head coordinate sqrt(2)*x0. Every coordinate used by the RS support is bounded by 2 in the new dense witness, so dot ≤6·4·2=48. A root can use the one exceptional coordinate, but its max dot is at most sqrt(48)*(sqrt(8)+2)<48.
- The [1,064 shifted dodecacode patterns](../dim92-96-shifted-dodecacode-outer-extension/), each contributing 2^20 vectors, remain zero on the whole head. One occupied ordinary row has max dot 4 against all three dense body alphabets, giving max 48 over 12 occupied rows.
- The [flat RS layer](../dim92-96-flat-rs-layers/) of 2,097,152 points is also zero on the whole head. One occupied ordinary row has max dot 4, giving max 24 over six occupied rows. Its squared radius is 24, and 24 is half the product of the two radii.

All sparse–sparse comparisons are unchanged. The dense body has all 22 ordinary rows occupied, so none of these sparse vectors equals a dense vector.
