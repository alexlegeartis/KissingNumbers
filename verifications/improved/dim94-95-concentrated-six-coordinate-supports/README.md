# The concentrated six-coordinate layer also works in 94D and 95D

**New exact lower bounds: 74,147,571,308 in 94D and 117,097,244,644 in 95D**, both **+114,536** over the opening project comparison **74,147,456,772** and **117,097,130,108** (relative +0.00015447% and +0.00009781%). The previous totals are taken from the [pinned exact mixed-tetrad package](https://github.com/alexlegeartis/KissingNumbers/tree/abda686407447f0f2770d43077bf2b95fb8c29bf/verifications/improved/dim94-95-mixed-tetrad-axis-sign-fibres). The published external binary parent is [Duursma–Greferath–Litsyn–Schmidt 2001](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.923742); shortening it gives historical derived comparisons 34,359,738,368 and 68,719,476,736, respectively, not independently published exact-dimensional records or an exhaustive priority check.

Use the 88-coordinate body (coordinates 0 through 87; the new head is zero). Number its four-site rows 2 through 23, as the inherited certificates do. The amplitude-four RS coordinate groups in physical coordinates have lengths 14,16,16,16,16,16; the common body grouping is g(c)=0 when c<14, and g(c)=1+floor((c−14)/16) otherwise. In 95D the exceptional root/head coordinate 88 is omitted from that partition and unaffected. Use only rows entirely inside a single physical group; neither assume all rows fit nor count four-row groups. Each new support uses at most three physical groups. The optional complete canonical replay produces 11 axial row modes with the respective 164 inherited quaternary restricted-RS axis words (the old known code, not a coding-bound claim), and deterministically fills an additional 1,820 literal supports with at least one doubled row. No support has more than two sites in a row. The 3,624 support vectors each receive all 32 even sign words on six coordinates, amplitudes two, norm squared 24.

**New/New.** No shared four-subset between distinct supports, so raw dot ≤12. For the same support two even sign words disagree at least twice, so dot ≤8. This yields 115,968 distinct new points.

**Old/New: all smaller shells.** The flat stock (ordinary and paired BCH as well as any inherited flat RS) has amplitudes at most one, so sees raw dot ≤12. The old 925 short triality profiles are 473 mixed, 304 complementary hexary, 148 post-RS. Write A,F for the old axial and flat rows, U,D for new occupied and double rows. Every old codeword has raw dot at most 4|U∩A|+2|U∩F|+2|D∩F|. This is ≤12 against the five immutable fixture blobs (four data and one count). The 85 matching-row profiles are a separate short shell, with amplitudes sqrt(2) on two sites per row and old squared norm 4w for w occupied rows. With s=|U∩S|+|D∩S| for old row mask S, their raw dot is at most 2sqrt(2)s. The exact normalized comparison is s²≤3w, which the verifier checks across all 85 modes and all new supports. The latter is separate from the old hexary/triality inventory and is the extra transfer check.

**Long shells and payment.** All old dense, shifted Golay/dodecacode and later mixed/axis-sign long shells have norm squared 96 and amplitudes at most two on the common body; new points have zero head. Thus dot ≤24. All amplitude-four RS points have at most one nonzero per physical group, giving the cap 3×8=24. Remove the four roots sqrt(48)(±e_i±e_j) on each of the **358** body coordinate pairs in a single group occurring in at least one new support. Each old root remaining meets a new support in at most one coordinate (including 95D roots involving the exceptional head), so dot is at most 2sqrt(48)<24. These raw estimates also imply disjointness from the retained normalized old set. Exact net ledger: 115,968 − 1,432 = 114,536 in each dimension.
